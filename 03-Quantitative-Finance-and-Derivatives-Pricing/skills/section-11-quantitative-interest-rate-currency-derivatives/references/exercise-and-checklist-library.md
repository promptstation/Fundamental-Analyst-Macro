# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Navigate financial complex derivatives through the rate instrument system (SOFR-futures mechanics with convexity
**Scenario:** provide a realistic quantitative finance and derivatives pricing scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** navigate financial complex derivatives through the rate instrument system (SOFR-futures mechanics with convexity, Treasury CTD and net basis, swaps and FRAs, convergence trends), the rate option layer (swaption matrices, caps and floors with model conventions, surface complexity, duration-based hedging), rate analytics (duration-convexity and DV01 arithmetic, key-rate decomposition, carry-roll accounting, scenario revaluation), the currency system (forwards and basis swaps, futures liquidity cycles, delta-struck option conventions, NDF gaps), currency analytics (sizing conventions, carry-trade crash profiles, dealer-flow interactions), cross-complex linkages (rate-commodity carry transmission, dollar effects, index linkages, vol interaction), and financial practice (curve-surface fluency, fixing literacy, regulatory capacity awareness, transition history), avoiding DV01 blindness, CTD ignorance, convexity errors, convention confusion, carry crashes, and commodity isolation failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Integrate the complete instrument discipline through synthesis (two-directional literacy, pricing-stack coherence from carry through Greeks, mechanics-through-judgment arc), expression mastery (view-to-instrument pipeline, structure library, roll-carry management, risk overlay), market reading (three-lens implied extraction, coherence audits, flow awareness), diagnostic mastery (position decomposition, anomaly triage.
2. Model-failure recognition), capstone execution (live view through complete instrument analysis with curve documentation, implied extraction, surface analysis, structure comparison, risk measurement, sized expression, decision journal, live defense), professional identity (instrument-literate fundamental analysis, quantitative humility, two-sided understanding, continuous calibration), and forward evolution (frontier awareness, specialization routes, capability planning, community contribution), avoiding siloed practice, expression failure, risk-less positioning, implied illiteracy, simulation capstones, and destination illusions failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Consolidates the complete discipline from instrument landscapes through mechanics, pricing theory, Greeks, volatility, curves, positioning, financial complexes, numerics, risk, and professional practice into the integrated instrument mathematics of fundamental analysis: the discipline synthesis — the unified pricing and expression craft (the two-directional literacy completion where the analyst both expresses fundamental views through optimally structured instrument positions and reads market-implied fundamentals backward from prices, curves, surfaces, and positioning, the dual fluency this course exists to build; the pricing-stack coherence where no-arbitrage links carry into forwards, forwards into options through parity, options into surfaces through implied volatility, and surfaces into risk through Greeks, one consistent mathematics from the simplest spread to the most complex structure; the mechanics-through-judgment arc where contract details, margin behavior, and roll dynamics feed quantitative models whose outputs feed professional judgment under model risk, the full vertical integration); the expression-mastery integration (the view-to-instrument pipeline where every fundamental conclusion passes through horizon matching, conviction sizing, volatility comparison, curve position, and liquidity filtering before becoming a position, synthesizing the options, curve, Greeks, and practice sections; the structure-library fluency spanning outrights, spreads, margins, options, and combinations whose payoff-risk-cost profiles the analyst selects deliberately rather than by default; the roll-and-carry management where position lifecycle decisions account for curve compensation, event calendars, and margin cycles per the mechanics section; the risk-overlay discipline where Greek profiles, stress results, and limit systems bound every expression per the risk section); the market-reading integration (the implied-information extraction where curve shapes reveal inventory judgment, volatility surfaces reveal event expectation and fear asymmetry, and positioning data reveals crowding and commercial behavior, the three-lens market-reading system; the coherence-audit practice where fundamental views, curve signals, volatility pricing, and positioning evidence either corroborate or challenge each other with explicit resolution before conviction; the flow-awareness layer where hedging pressure, dealer gamma, index rebalancing, and liquidation mechanics explain short-run price behavior that pure fundamentals cannot, connecting the positioning and desk-perspective sections); the diagnostic mastery (the position-diagnostic protocol where any holding faces decomposition into directional, curve, volatility, and carry exposures with P-and-L attribution against each; the price-anomaly investigation where observed prices inconsistent with carry, parity, or surface logic get triaged through convention error, liquidity artifact, genuine arbitrage, and information-signal hypotheses; the model-failure recognition where calibration instability, backtest exceptions, and stress surprises signal assumption breakdown requiring the model-risk responses of the practice section); the capstone project — demonstrating integrated mastery (the capstone specification requiring candidates to take one live fundamental view from the companion economics courses through complete instrument analysis: full curve documentation with roll-yield arithmetic, implied-fundamental extraction from prices and positioning, volatility-surface analysis isolating relevant event premia, candidate structure construction across at least three expression alternatives with payoff-Greek-cost comparison, risk measurement including VaR and stress results, a selected expression with conviction-weighted sizing and pre-committed adjustment plan, and a decision journal tracking the position against evolving data with honest attribution; the authenticity requirement engaging live market data, real margin and liquidity constraints, and actual event calendars over an operating period; the evaluation criteria covering pricing correctness, mechanics literacy, structure-selection logic, risk-measurement quality, implied-information reading, and documented learning from outcome; the defense-and-portfolio development where the capstone receives written case documentation and oral defense applying professional communication standards); the professional-identity synthesis (the instrument-literate fundamental analyst whose supply-demand views survive contact with margin cycles, roll mechanics, volatility pricing, and flow dynamics that defeat view-only traders; the quantitative-humility balance wielding sophisticated mathematics while documenting its assumptions and failure modes per the model-risk traditions; the two-sided-market understanding where the analyst knows both the fundamental story and the positioning reality that prices trade at their intersection; the continuous-calibration commitment where every expression gets scored, attributed, and fed back into the judgment system); and the forward path (the discipline-frontier awareness covering cleared-OTC evolution, crypto-derivatives market structure, machine-learning pricing applications, and climate-risk instrument development; the specialization routes toward volatility trading, structured commodity solutions, risk management, or quantitative research building on this foundation; the capability-development planning against this course's fifteen-section skill inventory with deliberate practice targeting identified gaps; the contribution orientation where practitioners document expression frameworks, share calibration and risk practices, and participate in the professional derivatives community) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the section-siloed practitioner who prices options without curve context or reads positioning without mechanics literacy, remedied by the integration arcs and capstone completeness; the view-without-expression analyst whose fundamental correctness never survives instrument translation, remedied by the pipeline discipline; the expression-without-risk positioner whose structures lack Greek decomposition and stress assessment until losses provide them, remedied by the overlay requirements; the implied-information illiterate who trades personal forecasts without knowing what the market already prices, remedied by the three-lens reading system; the capstone-as-simulation whose paper exercise avoids real margin, liquidity, and event pressure, remedied by the authenticity requirement; and the destination-illusion treating course completion as mastery arrival rather than the start of position-by-position calibration, remedied by the continuous-calibration commitment with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Unit 2 — Capstone integration

**Purpose:** integrate every capability above on one realistic problem end to end.

**Structure:** the learner takes a single problem in quantitative finance and derivatives pricing through each unit's framework in sequence, producing a coherent deliverable that mirrors real professional documentation. Evaluation criteria: internal consistency across artifacts, evidence discipline, honest trade-off statements, and demonstrable use of each unit's framework.

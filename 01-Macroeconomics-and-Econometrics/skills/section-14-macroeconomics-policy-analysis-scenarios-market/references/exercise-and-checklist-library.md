# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Translate macro into markets through policy analysis (measure anatomy with stage slippage
**Scenario:** provide a realistic macroeconomics and econometrics scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** translate macro into markets through policy analysis (measure anatomy with stage slippage, fiscal scoring, monetary assessment, structural versus demand lags, policy-mix interaction), scenario methodology (definition standards, weighted families, cross-variable propagation, conditional falsifiability, asset mapping with consistency checks), event-impact analysis (event studies, sensitivity estimation, surprise infrastructure, asymmetries, decay patterns), release-calendar management (tiering, pre-release protocols, live process, post-release review), policy-communication analysis (statement parsing, guidance interpretation, calendar and speaker hierarchy, political channels), crisis-playbook literacy (response sequences, policy puts, exit problems, crisis dashboards), and decision integration (view-to-position translation, horizon allocation, crowding assessment, reflexivity humility), avoiding announcement taking, narrative scenarios, static sensitivities, calendar passivity, text ignoring, put assumption, and consensus blindness failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Consolidates the complete discipline from foundations through national accounts, inflation, monetary and fiscal policy, the global system, cycles, econometrics, forecasting, systems building, and market translation into integrated professional practice: the discipline synthesis — the unified analytical craft (the end-to-end macro analysis chain where data arrives through vintages, gets organized by the indicator system, interpreted through cycle and regime frameworks, modeled with the econometric toolkit, forecast under real-time discipline, translated into market views through scenario and event analysis, documented in the personal system, and reviewed against outcomes in the calibration record, with every section's craft operating as one workflow; the chain-integrity principle where analytical quality equals the weakest link, most often data vintage discipline, identification honesty, or out-of-sample humility; the feedback-loop closure where forecast records, decision journals, and post-event reviews continuously update sensitivities, priors, and frameworks); the knowledge-integration map (the theory-measurement-policy triad where economic frameworks, statistical systems, and policy institutions interlock: national accounts and price indices measure what theory defines, central banks and fiscal authorities respond to what measurement reveals, and markets price the expected responses; the domestic-integration of the growth, inflation, rates, fiscal, and cycle systems into one national-picture assessment; the international-integration where balance-of-payments identities, parity conditions, and divergence dynamics connect national pictures into the global system; the quantitative-integration where econometrics provides the estimation engine, forecasting provides the discipline, and the personal system provides the infrastructure; the market-integration where scenario, event, and translation crafts convert analysis into positions with honest confidence bounds); the diagnostic mastery — reading macro situations and analyses (the situation-diagnostic protocol where a new macro environment gets assessed through cycle phase, inflation regime, policy stance, fiscal posture, external position, and financial conditions in structured sequence; the analysis-audit protocol where any macro claim faces interrogation on data vintage, identification strategy, specification robustness, out-of-sample record, and regime conditionality, synthesizing the diagnostics and forecasting sections; the failure-pattern recognition drawing on all fourteen sections' anti-pattern libraries as the diagnostic reference; the root-cause tracing where forecast errors and misreadings get attributed to data, model, regime, or judgment failures with proportionate remedies); the capstone project — demonstrating integrated mastery (the capstone specification requiring candidates to build and operate a complete macro analysis system for a chosen market complex: a documented data layer with vintage archiving, an indicator dashboard with regime classification, at least one estimated relationship model passing full diagnostics, a running nowcast and forecast record against consensus, a scenario set with market translation for an upcoming policy or data event, and a decision journal with calibration review; the authenticity requirement engaging real release calendars and live data over a sustained operating period rather than sanitized historical exercises; the evaluation criteria covering data discipline, econometric correctness, forecast honesty, market-translation logic, documentation quality, and demonstrated learning from errors; the portfolio-and-presentation development where the capstone receives written case-study documentation and live defense applying professional communication standards); the professional-identity synthesis (the fundamental-analyst-macro-specialist role where this course's craft feeds the commodity, rates, FX, and index specializations of the companion courses, with the top-down framework course integrating all levels; the epistemic-humility-with-conviction balance where probabilistic language and falsification discipline coexist with decisive positioning when evidence supports; the continuous-calibration posture where the practitioner treats every forecast as a scored prediction and every regime shift as a model-audit trigger; the ethics-and-integrity foundation where analysis serves decision truth rather than narrative comfort, with the research-record honesty that survives public audit); and the forward path (the discipline-frontier awareness covering real-time data expansion, machine-learning integration into official statistics and forecasting, and the climate-macro interface as active development areas; the specialization routes into the companion courses' market complexes or deeper quantitative methodology; the capability-development planning against this course's fifteen-section skill inventory with deliberate practice on identified gaps; the contribution orientation where practitioners document methods, share forecast records, and participate in the professional research community) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the technique-collection-without-integration, mastering sections in isolation while the end-to-end chain breaks at unpracticed junctions, remedied by the capstone's sustained-operation requirement; the diagnostic-blindness, producing analyses never audited against the discipline's standards, remedied by the audit protocols and peer review; the capstone-as-checkpoint, treating the project as completion ritual rather than living system, remedied by the authenticity and operating-period requirements; the calibration-avoidance, keeping no forecast record so no error pattern ever surfaces, remedied by the decision-journal and scoring systems; the frontier-complacency, resting on current methods while data and modeling landscapes evolve, remedied by the frontier-awareness habits; and the destination-illusion, treating course completion as mastery arrival rather than the start of sustained practice, remedied by the continuous-calibration posture with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Unit 2 — Capstone integration

**Purpose:** integrate every capability above on one realistic problem end to end.

**Structure:** the learner takes a single problem in macroeconomics and econometrics through each unit's framework in sequence, producing a coherent deliverable that mirrors real professional documentation. Evaluation criteria: internal consistency across artifacts, evidence discipline, honest trade-off statements, and demonstrable use of each unit's framework.

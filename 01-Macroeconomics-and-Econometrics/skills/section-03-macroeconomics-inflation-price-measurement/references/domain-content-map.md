## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the measurement and theory of inflation that drive rates, real yields, and inflation-sensitive futures: the index architecture (the CPI construction with fixed baskets, owner-equivalent rent, and the weighting system whose update cadence shapes measured persistence; the PCE deflator with its formula advantages including substitution handling and scope coverage of spending on behalf of households; the PPI pipeline where producer prices lead consumer prices through the production chain with the core-goods transmission that analysts track for goods inflation turns; the core-versus-headline debate where food and energy exclusion trades signal for noise depending on the forecast target; the producer-commodity-price linkage where the futures complexes this discipline trades feed directly into PPI and then CPI with measurable lags); inflation theory for practitioners (the demand-pull channel where output gaps and fiscal impulses push prices; the cost-push channel where energy, wage, and import-price shocks shift supply curves with the pass-through elasticities that differ across regimes; the monetary phenomenon claim and its long-run versus short-run validity; the expectations-augmented Phillips curve where the slope flattens and steepens with anchor credibility; the wage-price dynamics with the bargaining-power and productivity determinants of unit labor costs); expectations measurement (the survey systems across households, businesses, and professional forecasters with their horizon and population differences; the market-based breakevens extracted from inflation-linked securities with their liquidity and risk-premium contamination that requires adjustment before use; the anchoring assessment where the dispersion and drift of long-horizon expectations signal regime stability or loss); inflation regimes and extremes (the postwar low-inflation era and its behavioral residue; the 1970s great-inflation anatomy with the policy-error sequence; the 2021-2023 pandemic-era surge with its supply-shock, stimulus, and labor-market ingredients; disinflation and deflation dynamics including the debt-deflation mechanism; hyperinflation political economy for tail-risk literacy); the relative-price layer (the sectoral-inflation dispersion where headline hides divergent component trends; the commodity supercycle interactions where resource prices transmit globally through trade and terms of effect; the services-inflation stickiness driven by wages, rents, and local market power); the policy interface (the targeting frameworks including flexible average targeting and the credibility mechanics; the reaction-function reading where the same inflation print moves markets by its distance from the central-bank response threshold; the real-rate arithmetic where nominal rates minus expected inflation drive capital flows and thus FX and rate-futures positioning per the companion sections); the release-day craft (the CPI trading anatomy where headline, core, and component surprises move curve shape and index futures within minutes; the consensus-formation ecosystem of nowcast vendors and survey medians; the data-break investigation protocol distinguishing genuine inflation shifts from methodology changes, base effects, and seasonal failures) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the base-effect-blind reader who calls an inflation surge in a month with a depressed year-ago comparison, remedied by the base-effect arithmetic habit; the breakeven-believer who treats unadjusted market measures as pure expectations, remedied by the premium-contamination adjustments; the single-index partisan who argues CPI or PCE superiority without reference to the question at hand, remedied by the purpose-index matching discipline; the anchor-assumer who extrapolates low inflation through a policy-credibility break, remedied by the regime-assessment systems; and the pass-through-constant modeler who applies historical commodity-to-CPI elasticities across regimes, remedied by the regime-dependent elasticity testing with detection methods as the diagnostic.

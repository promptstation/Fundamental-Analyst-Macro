# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Navigate the government channel through fiscal architecture (budget anatomy
**Scenario:** provide a realistic macroeconomics and econometrics scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** navigate the government channel through fiscal architecture (budget anatomy, automatic stabilizers, cyclically adjusted impulse with multiplier debates), stabilization history (Keynesian-austerity-pandemic episodes, MMT claims), debt dynamics (accumulation identity, r versus g framework, official DSA methods, maturity management), financing interface (issuance calendars, investor bases, fiscal dominance, ratings mechanics, doom loops), subnational layers, political economy (budget calendars, debt-ceiling episodes, election cycles), long-run pressures (aging, climate, productivity uncertainty), and analyst craft (stance classification, spillover mapping, scenario weighting), avoiding deficit-hawk constancy, impulse ignorance, auction blindness, shutdown doomsaying, and long-run fatalism failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.

# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Navigate the multi-polar policy system through comparative architecture (ECB complexity
**Scenario:** provide a realistic interest rates and foreign exchange scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** navigate the multi-polar policy system through comparative architecture (ECB complexity, BoJ exceptionalism, BoE framework, PBoC distinct categories), divergence analysis (path comparison, differential drivers, convergence judgment, historical library), small-open-economy constraints (trilemma bindings, managed regimes, Fed dependence, vulnerability mapping), toolkit comparison (rate conventions, balance-sheet variety, macroprudential layers, FX integration), meeting intelligence (global sequencing, communication styles, surprise measurement, cross-meeting transmission), inflation-regime comparison (target variety, historical memory, structural persistence, credibility assessment), and multi-bank craft (institution-specific models, relative positioning, flow mapping, surprise scenarios), avoiding Fed-proxy modeling, exit-less divergence trades, regime misclassification, calendar sleep, credibility assumption, and toolkit confusion failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.

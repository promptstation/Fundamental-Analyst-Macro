## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the regime layer where market psychology, haven demand, and crisis mechanics override normal-state relationships in rate and currency markets: the risk-sentiment framework (the sentiment-continuum concept where risk appetite from euphoria through normalcy to panic reorganizes asset demand hierarchies with observable indicator systems; the measurement-dashboard spanning equity-volatility indices, credit spreads, currency-implied-vol surfaces, cross-currency basis, and flow surveys whose composite reading locates regime state per the monitoring disciplines; the sentiment-driver analysis distinguishing fundamental shocks, positioning mechanics, and reflexive dynamics whose mixture determines persistence and reversal character; the regime-transition-signal monitoring where indicator divergence, correlation shifts, and liquidity deterioration precede overt stress with the false-signal base rates documented); the safe-haven system (the haven-hierarchy literature where the dollar, yen, Swiss franc, gold, and core government bonds carry different haven properties by crisis type with the evidence-based selection rather than tradition; the dollar-haven mechanics where funding-market structure makes dollar demand self-reinforcing in stress per the global-dollar section; the JGB-and-yen paradoxes where domestic-deflation-crisis and carry-unwind dynamics produce haven behavior with conditional reliability; the government-bond-haven qualification where fiscal-origin crises invert bond-haven properties, the 2022 UK gilt and historical sovereign-stress evidence; the haven-effect-decay patterns where protection trades crowd, premium builds, and crisis resolution triggers violent normalization); the flight-to-quality mechanics (the liquidity-premium dynamics where stress widens spreads across funding, credit, and cross-currency markets simultaneously through dealer-balance-sheet retreat per the capacity literature; the deleveraging-cascade analysis where margin calls, VaR limits, and redemption pressure force selling regardless of valuation, the mechanism connecting positioning to crisis depth; the correlation-convergence phenomenon where normal-state diversification fails precisely when needed as cross-asset correlations move toward one, the documented stress regularity; the money-market-freeze patterns where commercial-paper, repo, and deposit markets dislocate with the facility-response mapping from central-bank toolkits); the crisis-episode library (the 1997-98 Asian and Russian sequence establishing the EM-crisis transmission template with contagion debates; the 2008 global financial crisis whose funding-freeze, basis-blowout, and coordinated-response sequence defines modern systemic stress; the 2010-12 euro-area crisis demonstrating sovereign-bank doom loops and currency-union stress mechanics; the 2015-16 China-devaluation episode revealing managed-regime spillover through reserve and expectation channels; the March-2020 pandemic dash-for-cash where even Treasuries sold off before facility response, the most complete funding-stress episode on record; the 2022 UK LDI episode where leverage in liability-hedging created gilt-market spiral requiring central-bank purchase intervention; the episode-pattern extraction where common sequences of trigger, amplification, policy response, and normalization inform scenario construction and early-warning calibration); the crisis-response analysis (the central-bank-crisis-toolkit spanning rate cuts, liquidity facilities, asset purchases, swap lines, and communication escalation whose sequencing patterns are documented across episodes; the fiscal-response interaction where crisis politics determine stimulus speed and composition with market-reading implications; the international-coordination realities where G7-G20 statements, IMF programs, and bilateral support carry varying credibility weight; the response-effectiveness assessment where episode evidence distinguishes stabilizing interventions from ineffective ones by design and timing features); the stress-positioning craft (the portfolio-stress-testing application where rate and FX positions face historical and hypothetical crisis scenarios per the risk companion's disciplines with the funding-liquidity dimension included; the hedge-design-under-stress where protection instruments get selected for crisis-state liquidity and counterparty reliability rather than calm-state cost alone; the tail-expression structures where options, convexity positions, and haven allocations provide asymmetric crisis participation with carry-cost budgeting per the volatility and portfolio disciplines; the crisis-opportunity preparation where dislocation scenarios identify potential relative-value opportunities requiring capital and conviction available during stress, the preparation-before-panic discipline); and the normalization analysis (the recovery-sequence mapping where funding markets stabilize before credit before risk assets with the historical ordering evidence; the policy-exit-dynamics where facility withdrawal and balance-sheet normalization trigger regime transitions with taper-tantrum case evidence; the scar-effect monitoring where crisis experience reshapes investor behavior, regulation, and market structure for years, conditioning future transmission; the regime-declaration humility where analysts state stress-state assessments probabilistically given the documented difficulty of real-time crisis dating per the cycle-dating lessons) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the fair-weather portfolio whose diversification and hedges work only in calm states, remediated by the stress-testing and correlation-convergence discipline; the haven-tradition follower who buys yen or bonds in fiscal crises where their haven properties invert, remediated by the conditional-haven evidence base; the dash-for-cash denier whose crisis models assume liquidity continuity in funding markets, remediated by the episode-library study; the policy-response instant-assumer whose stress scenarios omit the facility interventions that historically truncate spirals, remediated by the response-analysis integration; the bottom-fisher without-capital-plan who identifies crisis dislocations but lacks the margin endurance to hold them, remediated by the stress-positioning craft; and the normalization-timer whose recovery calls ignore the documented sequencing and scar effects, remediated by the recovery-pattern discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

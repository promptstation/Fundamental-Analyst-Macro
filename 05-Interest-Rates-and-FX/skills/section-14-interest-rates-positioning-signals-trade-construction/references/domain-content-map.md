## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the decision layer where analysis, market-implied information, and positioning evidence combine into constructed trades with managed lifecycle: the positioning-intelligence system (the futures-positioning data where CFTC reports across currency and rate contracts separate leveraged-fund, asset-manager, and dealer categories with the interpretation cautions from the derivatives companion applied to rate and FX specifics; the option-implied-positioning inference where risk reversals and volatility skew reveal directional preference and tail hedging demand per the surface-reading disciplines; the flow-survey ecosystems including bank-client surveys and prime-brokerage data whose coverage biases require calibration against observable price behavior; the crowding-assessment methodology where positioning extremes relative to history and capacity define reversal-vulnerability rankings per the crowded-trade detection traditions); the signal-construction discipline (the fundamental-signal family where policy divergence, growth differentials, terms-of-trade shifts, and external-balance trends generate medium-horizon directional evidence per the theory sections; the market-implied-signal family where curve shape, basis levels, carry differentials, and volatility structure reveal what prices already embed, the expectation baseline every view must beat; the flow-and-positioning signals where crowding, sentiment regimes, and dealer-inventory states condition timing and expression per the structure sections; the signal-combination framework where independent evidence streams aggregate into conviction scores with the correlation-awareness preventing triple-counting of one underlying factor); the trade-construction process (the thesis-statement discipline where every position articulates its fundamental driver, market-implied expectation, divergence, catalyst, and horizon in written form per the view-documentation traditions; the expression-selection matrix mapping thesis characteristics to instrument choice across spot, futures, forwards, swaps, options, and spreads with cost-risk-liquidity comparison per the strategy libraries; the sizing-determination integrating conviction probability, expected payoff asymmetry, volatility state, and portfolio-factor budget per the risk-budgeting disciplines; the entry-timing craft where catalyst calendars, flow windows, and technical levels sequence execution without subordinating fundamental logic to chart patterns); the lifecycle-management system (the pre-committed adjustment plan where favorable, adverse, and time-decay scenarios carry predetermined responses removing improvisation under stress per the event-discipline traditions; the thesis-invalidation monitoring where defined data thresholds and market evidence trigger reassessment rather than hope-based holding; the profit-taking-and-roll decisions where horizon arrival, crowding development, and carry-cost evolution guide exit timing per the edge-decay awareness; the position-journal discipline recording entry rationale, evolution, and outcome attribution feeding the calibration systems per the professional-practice traditions); the multi-position portfolio craft (the relative-value-book construction where paired and basket positions express cleaner views with reduced market beta per the strategy sections; the cross-complex-coherence auditing where rate, FX, commodity, and index positions get tested for hidden factor concentration per the transmission web; the liquidity-tier management where portfolio positions distribute across liquidity classes matching conviction strength and holding horizon; the rebalancing-rules discipline where systematic thresholds govern adjustment frequency preventing both neglect and overtrading); the performance-feedback integration (the trade-level attribution separating thesis correctness, expression efficiency, timing contribution, and luck per the evaluation disciplines; the signal-scorecard maintenance where each evidence family's hit rates and payoff profiles get tracked enabling weight adjustment with the overfitting-caution from the modeling companions; the process-quality review where decision adherence to documented frameworks gets assessed independently of outcomes per the decision-quality traditions; the drawdown-response protocols where portfolio stress triggers predetermined de-risking sequences rather than discretionary panic, connecting to the crisis disciplines) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the conviction-without-construction analyst whose correct fundamental view loses through wrong instrument, size, or timing selection, remediated by the construction-process discipline; the positioning-ignorant contrarian whose fade-the-crowd trades lack capacity and catalyst analysis, remediated by the crowding-assessment methodology; the triple-counted-signal believer whose three confirming indicators all reflect one factor, inflating false conviction, remediated by the correlation-aware combination framework; the improvising position-manager whose adjustment decisions arrive under stress without pre-commitment, remediated by the lifecycle system; the outcome-graded decision-maker whose learning confuses good process with lucky results, remediated by the process-quality review; and the attribution-free performer whose returns never get decomposed into thesis, expression, and timing contributions, preventing skill development, remediated by the feedback integration with detection methods as the diagnostic.

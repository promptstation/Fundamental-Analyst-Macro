## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the exchange-rate determination frameworks whose empirical behavior — more failure than success at short horizons — defines the professional analytical challenge: the parity-conditions system (the purchasing-power-parity tradition whose long-run anchoring claim survives weakly across decades while failing completely at tradeable horizons, with the Balassa-Samuelson refinement explaining systematic deviations through productivity differentials; the uncovered-interest-parity puzzle whose empirical violation defines the carry-trade opportunity documented across decades and currency universes; the covered-interest-parity relationship whose post-2008 persistent deviations reveal balance-sheet constraints and dollar-funding scarcity rather than arbitrage failure; the international-Fisher-effect connecting rate differentials to expected inflation differentials with the real-rate-parity implications); the asset-market approach (the monetary-model tradition where relative money supplies and demands determine prices through flexible-price logic whose short-run failures motivated successor frameworks; the portfolio-balance approach where currency values equilibrate asset-stock demands across countries with the flow-accumulation dynamics; the exchange-rate-as-asset-price insight where news-driven repricing dominates measurable fundamentals, the Meese-Rogoff forecasting-pessimism result that every practitioner must internalize; the expectation-formation layer where survey data, option-implied distributions, and positioning reveal market beliefs that pure models omit); the fundamental-driver hierarchy by horizon (the seconds-to-days horizon where flow imbalances, positioning adjustments, and technical levels dominate with fundamentals nearly irrelevant; the weeks-to-quarters horizon where policy divergence, growth differentials, and risk sentiment drive trends per the divergence frameworks; the years horizon where terms of trade, productivity differentials, balance-sheet adjustment, and PPP-gravity reassert measurable influence; the horizon-matching discipline requiring every currency view to declare its timeframe and corresponding driver set, preventing framework confusion); the commodity-and-terms-of-trade channel (the commodity-currency system where Australian, Canadian, and emerging-market currencies track export prices with pass-through elasticities varying by regime per the cross-complex linkage discipline; the oil-currency relationships spanning petro-exporter currencies and oil-importer terms-of-trade effects with the petrodollar-flow mechanics; the agricultural-exporter currencies where crop prices and volumes shape external balances for Brazil and others per the ag-economics companion; the terms-of-trade-shock transmission where commodity moves hit trade balances, fiscal revenues, and inflation simultaneously, complicating policy response and currency equilibrium); the policy-and-institutional determinants (the inflation-differential accumulation where sustained inflation gaps erode competitiveness requiring nominal adjustment absent real convergence, the long-run PPP mechanism operating through policy credibility; the central-bank-credibility pricing where anchor strength differences embed in term premia and currency risk premiums per the comparative-policy section; the fiscal-sustainability channel where debt trajectories and current-account financing needs create currency vulnerability rankings per the fiscal and external-sustainability disciplines; the capital-control and regime-change effects where institutional shifts reprice currencies discontinuously, defying continuous-model frameworks); the empirical-modeling reality (the forecasting-failure literature where no model beat random walk consistently out-of-sample at short horizons, the humility baseline; the conditional-success findings where models work in specific regimes, horizons, and currency sets with the identification discipline; the machine-learning attempts whose pattern detection improves some horizons without overturning the fundamental unpredictability at short ranges; the practitioner-synthesis approach combining driver-horizon mapping, flow and positioning evidence, policy divergence analysis, and valuation anchors into probabilistic views rather than point forecasts); and the view-construction craft (the fair-value-framework discipline maintaining institutional fair-value estimates from PPP variants, real effective rates, and external-balance approaches whose gaps define long-horizon anchors; the catalyst-identification practice where scheduled policy decisions, data sequences, and flow events provide the timing that valuation alone cannot; the asymmetry-assessment where managed regimes, intervention threat, and positioning concentration create skewed risk profiles independent of directional view; the cross-rate-coherence checking where bilateral views must triangulate consistently or reveal relative-conviction information per the coherence-audit traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the PPP-timer who forecasts near-term reversions to purchasing parity against decades of documented failure, remediated by the horizon-matching discipline; the UIP-believer who cannot explain why carry trades historically earned returns against the parity prediction, remediated by the violation-premium literature; the model-nihilist who concludes from forecast failure that fundamentals never matter, missing the horizon-dependent influence structure, remediated by the driver-hierarchy framework; the flow-ignorant theorist whose elegant equilibrium analysis ignores that flows drive prices for months at a time, remediated by the asset-market and microstructure integration; the commodity-currency mechanical trader whose AUD and CAD views apply fixed beta to commodity moves across regimes, remediated by the regime-varying pass-through analysis; and the valuation-without-catalyst positioner whose correct fair-value views bleed carry for years awaiting reversion, remediated by the catalyst-timing discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

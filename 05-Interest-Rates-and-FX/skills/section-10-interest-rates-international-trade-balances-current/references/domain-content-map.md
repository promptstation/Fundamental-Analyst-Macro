## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the trade and current-account analysis whose flows and financing dynamics shape currency values and transmit shocks between economies: the current-account architecture (the account decomposition where goods trade, services including the digitally-delivered growth segments, primary income from cross-border investment positions, and secondary transfers compose the balance with component-specific drivers; the savings-investment identity grounding where current accounts equal national saving minus investment, making trade balances the external face of domestic sector behavior per the macro companion's identities; the trade-balance versus income-balance evolution where mature creditor nations earn income surpluses despite goods deficits and mature debtors face the reverse, the stock-flow distinction analysts must maintain; the measurement-system literacy where customs data, balance-of-payments adjustments, and mirror statistics diverge systematically requiring source-appropriate interpretation per the international-accounting foundations); the trade-flow analysis craft (the export-import decomposition where volume and price effects separate through unit-value analysis, distinguishing quantity trends from commodity-price effects per the terms-of-trade frameworks; the partner-and-product concentration analysis where dependence on specific markets and products creates vulnerability profiles; the value-chain positioning where gross flows double-count intermediate trade, requiring value-added measures for competitiveness assessment per the global-production literature; the leading-indicator extraction where trade data reveals global demand cycles, inventory positions, and supply-chain health ahead of domestic activity statistics); the balance-determinant analysis (the exchange-rate pass-through evidence where depreciation improves balances with J-curve timing and elasticity conditions documented across episodes per the adjustment literature; the demand-asymmetry principle where partner growth drives exports more than own currency moves in trade-weighted evidence, the growth-dominance finding; the savings-behavior drivers where demographic transitions, fiscal positions, corporate retention patterns, and financial-development shape the national saving rates underlying balances; the structural-shift tracking where energy independence, manufacturing relocation, and services digitization rewrite balance patterns across decades defying short-sample extrapolation); the financing-and-sustainability assessment (the external-financing-requirement calculation where current-account deficits demand equivalent capital inflows whose composition determines vulnerability; the flow-quality hierarchy where foreign-direct investment stability contrasts with portfolio and banking flow reversibility, the sudden-stop risk ranking per the capital-flow literature; the external-debt-structure analysis where currency composition, maturity profile, and holder identity determine stress sensitivity per the vulnerability frameworks; the stock-position dynamics where valuation effects on external assets and liabilities shift net positions independent of flows, the dark-matter and balance-sheet adjustment insights); the policy-and-agreement landscape (the trade-policy instruments where tariffs, quotas, and industrial policy shift bilateral balances with general-equilibrium offsets documented across trade-war episodes; the agreement-architecture from WTO frameworks through regional agreements whose rules-of-origin and liberalization schedules reshape flow patterns; the imbalance-politics where deficit and surplus nation friction drives policy coordination attempts and unilateral measures with currency-manipulation designation systems; the capital-control toolkit whose effectiveness evidence varies by control type, timing, and evasion capacity per the management-regime literature); the data-and-forecasting system (the publication-calendar discipline where trade releases across major economies arrive with lags and revision patterns requiring nowcast integration per the forecasting companion; the oil-and-commodity trade adjustment where energy price moves mechanically shift importer-exporter balances requiring underlying-trend extraction; the seasonal-and-calendar effects where holiday timing and working-day variations distort monthly comparisons demanding adjustment literacy; the trade-forecast methodology combining global demand models, elasticity estimates, and commodity-price scenarios whose accuracy records inform weight assignment); and the currency-linkage synthesis (the balance-to-currency transmission mapping where sustainable deficits with quality financing support currencies while deteriorating compositions pressure them, the conditional rather than mechanical relationship; the twin-deficit analysis connecting fiscal and external positions through savings identities per the fiscal companion; the intervention-reserve interaction where official accumulation and drawdown mediate balance-currency transmission in managed regimes; the crisis-pattern library where balance deterioration, financing composition shift, and confidence loss combine in documented currency-crisis sequences informing vulnerability monitoring) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the bilateral-deficit obsession whose policy and trading analysis fixates on two-country gaps missing the multilateral savings-investment determinants, remediated by the identity-grounded framework; the gross-flow analyst whose competitiveness conclusions rest on value-chain-inflated trade statistics, remediated by the value-added measures; the J-curve-impatient forecaster whose depreciation-benefit timing ignores elasticity lags, remediated by the adjustment-evidence literature; the flow-only sustainability assessor ignoring stock positions and valuation effects, remediated by the balance-sheet integration; the commodity-blind trend reader whose balance interpretations mix volume and price effects without decomposition, remediated by the unit-value analysis; and the mechanical-balance-currency modeler whose FX forecasts apply fixed trade-response elasticities across financing and regime contexts, remediated by the conditional-transmission discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

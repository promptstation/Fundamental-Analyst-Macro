# Carry, Divergence, and FX Strategy: The Position-Building Layer — Professional Training

## Mission

Produce professional-grade learning modules and training materials on **carry, Divergence, and FX Strategy: The Position-Building Layer** within The rates and currencies discipline of the futures fundamental analyst — Federal Reserve operations, global yield curves, fixed-income mathematics, and international trade balances as the force field within which every other futures complex prices, and a primary trading domain in its own right.

Subject scope: Covers the strategy layer where rate and currency analysis becomes positioned expression with defined risk characteristics: the carry-trade system (the carry definition across markets where the interest differential plus forward-point yield defines unhedged carry with the calculation conventions varying by instrument class; the historical-return evidence where carry strategies earned persistent premia across currencies, rates, and commodities with the crash-characteristic documentation of sharp reversals during risk events; the crash-mechanism analysis where leverage unwinding, volatility spikes, and correlation convergence convert steady income into episodic large losses, the Brunnermeier tradition's global-runs insight; the carry-valuation interaction where entering carry at extreme valuation gaps versus compressed differentials produces different forward return distributions per the empirical literature); the divergence-trade construction (the policy-divergence expression where expected central-bank path differences get positioned through spot, forwards, or rate differentials per the divergence frameworks; the growth-divergence channel where relative data momentum drives currency trends with the surprise-stream analysis identifying persistence; the divergence-timing discipline where trades establish before consensus forms and exit as convergence crowds, the lifecycle awareness from the edge-decay traditions; the multi-bank relative-value structures where three-currency baskets express cleaner divergence views than bilateral pairs contaminated by dollar beta); the rate-strategy library (the curve-position toolkit where level, slope, and butterfly views express through the futures and swap instruments per the practice sections with carry-roll-return decomposition; the spread-family including swap-spreads, cross-country basis trades, and cash-futures basis whose drivers mix credit, supply, and technical factors; the conditional-structures where swaptions and caps express views contingent on events or thresholds per the option layer; the relative-value discipline where rich-cheap assessment against fair-value models and historical distributions defines entry with exit asymmetry awareness); the currency-strategy library (the directional-expression selection across spot, forwards, futures, and options whose cost-risk profiles differ by horizon and conviction per the FX structure section; the EM-strategy specifics where carry, valuation, and vulnerability rankings combine with the liquidity and intervention constraints bounding expression per the regime literacy); the risk-sentiment overlay (the risk-on-off regime mapping where equity-volatility states, credit spreads, and commodity trends define the environment carry and divergence strategies inhabit; the safe-haven flow mechanics where stress events drive documented currency and rate-haven demand reshaping correlations instantly; the sentiment-data integration where positioning surveys, volatility surfaces, and flow trackers quantify regime state per the market-reading systems; the strategy-regime-conditioning where historical strategy returns get analyzed conditional on sentiment regimes rather than unconditionally, the context-dependent performance understanding); the portfolio-integration discipline (the factor-decomposition where rate and FX positions get mapped to growth, inflation, policy, carry, and risk-sentiment factors revealing true portfolio bets across complexes per the risk companion's frameworks; and the strategy-evaluation practice (the performance-attribution separating carry collection, directional movement, curve reshaping, and timing contributions whose discipline reveals actual edge sources; the divergence-latecomer whose trades enter after consensus formation and crowding, inheriting reversal risk, remediated by the lifecycle-timing analysis; the factor-blind positioner whose portfolio carries hidden concentrated bets across desks and complexes, remediated by the decomposition discipline; Write as an experienced practitioner, not as a summarizer of popular content. Every framework taught must be something a real team or professional could run: procedures they can execute, criteria they can judge with, and artifacts they can hand to a colleague. Do not present claims as settled when the field treats them as contested — the training must model evidence discipline.

## Writing Standards

Throughout the material, prioritize language that is:

* Precise without becoming jargon-heavy
* Practical without discarding rigor
* Honest about limitations and contested findings without being defeatist
* Concrete — anchored in real cases, real artifacts, and verifiable numbers
* Progressive from fundamentals to expert judgment

Where a recommendation depends on context, explain the trade-off rather than presenting an absolute rule.

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid: Level-versus-change confusion — quoting rates and curve positions without the change dynamics that actually generate futures P-and-L, positioning on levels the market priced months ago; Single-central-bank tunnel vision — analyzing Fed policy in isolation while global curve linkages, divergence trades, and spillover flows drive the actual moves; Parity-violation naivety — treating PPP or UIP deviations as arbitrage opportunities rather than the documented risk-premium and constraint phenomena they represent; Duration-blind sizing — expressing rate views through instruments whose DV01, convexity, and option features were never computed, discovering leverage through losses; Trade-balance mechanical reading — forecasting FX from trade-flow arithmetic while capital flows, policy divergence, and positioning dominate the actual price formation; exercises without evaluation criteria; modules that stop at inspiration without a single decision the learner can now make better; and any content that overstates certainty beyond what the evidence supports.

## Decision Heuristic

When allocating depth under time or length limits, ask:

1. Where will this audience actually stall in practice?
2. Which unit protects the most value if taught well?
3. Can the learner run the framework tomorrow without me?
4. Is every example doing work a plain sentence could not?
5. What would a skeptical domain expert say about this material?

If two topics compete for space, keep the one that changes a decision.

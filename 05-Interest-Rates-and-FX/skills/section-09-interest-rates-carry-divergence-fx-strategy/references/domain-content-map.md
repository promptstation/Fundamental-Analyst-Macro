## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the strategy layer where rate and currency analysis becomes positioned expression with defined risk characteristics: the carry-trade system (the carry definition across markets where the interest differential plus forward-point yield defines unhedged carry with the calculation conventions varying by instrument class; the historical-return evidence where carry strategies earned persistent premia across currencies, rates, and commodities with the crash-characteristic documentation of sharp reversals during risk events; the crash-mechanism analysis where leverage unwinding, volatility spikes, and correlation convergence convert steady income into episodic large losses, the Brunnermeier tradition's global-runs insight; the carry-valuation interaction where entering carry at extreme valuation gaps versus compressed differentials produces different forward return distributions per the empirical literature); the divergence-trade construction (the policy-divergence expression where expected central-bank path differences get positioned through spot, forwards, or rate differentials per the divergence frameworks; the growth-divergence channel where relative data momentum drives currency trends with the surprise-stream analysis identifying persistence; the divergence-timing discipline where trades establish before consensus forms and exit as convergence crowds, the lifecycle awareness from the edge-decay traditions; the multi-bank relative-value structures where three-currency baskets express cleaner divergence views than bilateral pairs contaminated by dollar beta); the rate-strategy library (the curve-position toolkit where level, slope, and butterfly views express through the futures and swap instruments per the practice sections with carry-roll-return decomposition; the spread-family including swap-spreads, cross-country basis trades, and cash-futures basis whose drivers mix credit, supply, and technical factors; the conditional-structures where swaptions and caps express views contingent on events or thresholds per the option layer; the relative-value discipline where rich-cheap assessment against fair-value models and historical distributions defines entry with exit asymmetry awareness); the currency-strategy library (the directional-expression selection across spot, forwards, futures, and options whose cost-risk profiles differ by horizon and conviction per the FX structure section; the volatility-expression layer where implied-vol levels and skew shape determine whether views get expressed directionally or through premium structures per the volatility companion; the cross-and-basket construction isolating bilateral views from broad dollar moves through trade-weighted hedging; the EM-strategy specifics where carry, valuation, and vulnerability rankings combine with the liquidity and intervention constraints bounding expression per the regime literacy); the risk-sentiment overlay (the risk-on-off regime mapping where equity-volatility states, credit spreads, and commodity trends define the environment carry and divergence strategies inhabit; the safe-haven flow mechanics where stress events drive documented currency and rate-haven demand reshaping correlations instantly; the sentiment-data integration where positioning surveys, volatility surfaces, and flow trackers quantify regime state per the market-reading systems; the strategy-regime-conditioning where historical strategy returns get analyzed conditional on sentiment regimes rather than unconditionally, the context-dependent performance understanding); the portfolio-integration discipline (the factor-decomposition where rate and FX positions get mapped to growth, inflation, policy, carry, and risk-sentiment factors revealing true portfolio bets across complexes per the risk companion's frameworks; the cross-complex-netting awareness where commodity, rate, and currency positions combine into net exposures invisible at single-desk level per the integration discipline of the intelligence companion; the correlation-regime tracking where cross-asset relationships shift between regimes, demanding conditional rather than historical-average diversification assumptions; the sizing-and-conviction integration where Kelly-tradition fraction sizing meets fundamental confidence assessment and market-implied probability comparison per the professional-practice traditions); and the strategy-evaluation practice (the performance-attribution separating carry collection, directional movement, curve reshaping, and timing contributions whose discipline reveals actual edge sources; the benchmark-comparison judgment against appropriate references including cash carry, index returns, and volatility-adjusted alternatives; the drawdown-and-recovery analysis where strategy stress behavior gets assessed against historical analogs and forward scenarios; the capacity-and-decay monitoring where crowding evidence and return compression signal strategy lifecycle position per the alpha-lifecycle traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the carry-collector without crash protection whose multi-year premium harvest faces unhedged tail exposure until one event erases the record, remediated by the crash-mechanism sizing and option-overlay discipline; the divergence-latecomer whose trades enter after consensus formation and crowding, inheriting reversal risk, remediated by the lifecycle-timing analysis; the factor-blind positioner whose portfolio carries hidden concentrated bets across desks and complexes, remediated by the decomposition discipline; the regime-unconditioned backtester whose strategy evaluation averages across incompatible market states, remediated by the conditional-performance analysis; the benchmark-free performer who cannot state what returns mean without reference comparison, remediated by the evaluation practice; and the rich-cheap-fade trader who shorts apparent valuation extremes without catalyst or funding-horizon analysis, remediated by the convergence-mechanism discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

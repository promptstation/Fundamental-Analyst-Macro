## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the yield curve as analytical object in full depth, extending the macro companion's curve foundations into professional curve analytics: the curve-construction craft (the bootstrap methodology deriving spot and forward curves from coupon-instrument prices with the instrument-selection, sequencing, and interpolation decisions that make vendor curves differ; the spline-and-parametric fitting families including the Nelson-Siegel and Svensson traditions whose factor interpretations connect to level, slope, and curvature; the day-count and compounding-convention mastery where actual-actual versus thirty-360 and simple versus continuous conventions create systematic comparison errors; the curve-quality diagnostics where fit residuals, smoothness, and instrument coverage validate construction before analysis); the term-structure decomposition (the principal-component tradition where level, slope, and curvature factors explain the vast majority of curve variation with the residual-risk awareness for butterfly and spread positions; the forward-curve extraction and interpretation where implied forward rates express the market's marginal pricing between tenors with the expectation-premium entanglement; the term-premium estimation methods spanning survey-based, model-based including the ACM tradition, and consensus-forecast-based approaches whose disagreement defines estimation uncertainty; the risk-neutral versus real-world curve reading where futures-implied paths price under risk-neutral measures while forecast views require real-world probability adjustment per the pricing-measure discipline); the curve-shape analysis (the shape-measure taxonomy spanning benchmark spreads, factor scores, and full-curve comparison metrics with the question-measure matching discipline; the historical-shape-database where current configurations get located against episode archives whose subsequent growth, inflation, and policy outcomes provide conditional base rates; the inversion-analysis depth where different spread pairs carry different recession records with the mechanism debates from liquidity preference through policy-expectation channels; the international-shape-comparison where curve configurations across major economies reveal divergence positioning and relative-value opportunities per the central-bank section); the curve-dynamics modeling (the movement-taxonomy discipline where bear and bull steepening and flattening classify every curve move by driver, with the analytical content in identifying which force dominates; the policy-expectation transmission where front-end moves reflect repriced central-bank paths per the implied-path systems; the term-premium-driven long-end behavior where supply, inflation-risk, fiscal credibility, and foreign demand shift long rates independently of policy expectations, connecting to the fiscal and international sections; the cross-market curve-linkage where global term-premium co-movement and divergence flows connect national curves with the synchronization evidence and exceptions documented); the curve-trading analytics (the duration-neutral spread construction where positions isolate slope and curvature from level exposure through DV01-weighted ratios per the fixed-income mathematics section; the roll-down and carry calculation where curve position generates predictable return components whose arithmetic determines spread-trade economics; the butterfly and curvature expression where mid-versus-wings positioning trades the third factor with the liquidity and hedging complexity awareness; the curve-option layer where swaption structures express conditional curve views with the volatility-surface literacy from the derivatives companion applied to rate dimensions); the curve-information extraction (the growth-signal reading where slope behavior conditions on policy-regime phase provides probabilistic recession and recovery information per the empirical literature; the inflation-expectation extraction where nominal and real curve comparisons plus survey anchoring triangulate market inflation beliefs; the fiscal-signal interpretation where issuance composition, foreign demand evidence, and term-premium moves reveal bond-market assessment of sovereign trajectories per the fiscal companion; the policy-credibility monitoring where long-end behavior during tightening cycles signals confidence in central-bank anchoring, the regime-health diagnostic) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the vendor-curve consumer whose analysis never verifies construction conventions, producing phantom spreads and mispriced positions, remediated by the bootstrap and convention mastery; the PCA-reductionist whose three-factor comfort ignores the residual moves that damage butterfly positions, remediated by the residual-risk awareness; the forward-literalist who reads implied forwards as forecasts rather than risk-neutral prices, remediated by the measure-discipline literacy; the single-spread recessionist who cites one benchmark spread without regime conditioning or mechanism analysis, remediated by the inversion-depth study; the unhedged-slope trader whose steepener carries unintended level duration through ratio miscalculation, remediated by the DV01-neutral construction discipline; and the term-premium-point-estimator who quotes one model's premium as truth while estimates diverge widely, remediated by the estimation-uncertainty presentation with detection methods as the diagnostic.

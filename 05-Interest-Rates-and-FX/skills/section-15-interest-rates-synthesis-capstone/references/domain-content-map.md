## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Consolidates the complete discipline from market landscapes through central-bank operations, curve analytics, fixed-income mathematics, derivatives practice, FX structure and theory, strategy construction, external-sector analysis, the dollar system, transmission webs, crisis dynamics, and positioning craft into integrated professional practice: the discipline synthesis — the unified rates-and-currencies craft (the policy-to-price chain where economic data feeds central-bank reaction functions, reaction functions shape expected paths, expected paths price into curves and currencies, prices transmit across complexes through the web, and positioning conditions the entire chain's behavior, with every section operating as one system; the dual-domain fluency where rate analysis through curves, duration, and swaps combines with currency analysis through parity conditions, flows, and regimes into the integrated macro-financial view that defines the specialization; the instrument-mastery foundation where mathematical precision, derivative mechanics, and market-structure literacy ensure views survive contact with execution reality per the derivatives and economics companions); the analysis-mode integration (the policy-watch mode where central-bank communication, data dependence, and implied-path analysis drive daily rate and currency assessment per the Fed and divergence sections; the curve-and-carry mode where term-structure positioning, roll economics, and cross-market relative value provide the continuous income and expression layer; the flow-and-regime mode where positioning, sentiment states, and dollar-cycle conditions frame what prices can sustain per the structure and stress sections; the crisis-readiness mode where funding-market indicators, haven properties, and stress scenarios maintain systemic awareness per the crisis disciplines); the diagnostic mastery — reading rate and currency situations (the market-diagnostic protocol where any rate or FX move decomposes into policy-expectation shift, term-premium movement, flow imbalance, positioning adjustment, and cross-asset transmission with evidence per component per the decomposition traditions; the analysis-audit protocol where any rates-FX claim faces interrogation on data vintage, parity-consistency, regime-conditioning, instrument-convention accuracy, and positioning context, synthesizing all sections' failure libraries; the incoherence-detection skill where curves, currencies, volatility surfaces, and positioning that disagree reveal either relative-value opportunity or analytical error requiring resolution before conviction per the coherence-audit disciplines); the capstone project — demonstrating integrated mastery (the capstone specification requiring candidates to produce and defend a complete rates-and-FX analysis package on a live divergence or regime question: central-bank reaction-function analysis with implied-path extraction versus personal forecast for at least two institutions, curve and currency fair-value assessment with term-premium and parity-anchored valuation, positioning and sentiment audit of the relevant markets, cross-asset transmission mapping of the thesis through equities and commodities per the web discipline, a constructed trade recommendation spanning instrument selection, sizing, and lifecycle plan per the decision layer, stress-scenario analysis including a funding-stress variant per the crisis section, and a decision journal tracking the view against live data and market evolution with honest attribution; the authenticity requirement engaging real publication calendars, live curve and currency behavior, and actual positioning data over an operating period; the evaluation criteria covering analytical correctness, convention precision, regime-conditioning quality, expression logic, risk-measurement completeness, and demonstrated learning from outcome; the defense-and-portfolio development where capstone work receives written case documentation and oral defense applying professional standards); the professional-identity synthesis (the rates-FX-specialist identity where macro literacy, mathematical precision, market-structure depth, and positioning intelligence combine into the discipline's most technically demanding fundamental specialization; the forecast-humility-with-decision-courage balance where Meese-Rogoff pessimism about point prediction coexists with disciplined probabilistic positioning per the theory sections; the systemic-awareness posture where dollar-funding conditions, crisis patterns, and transmission webs frame every position as embedded in the global financial system rather than isolated bets; the continuous-calibration commitment where every forecast, trade, and crisis call enters the scored record feeding improvement per the professional-practice traditions); and the forward path (the discipline-frontier awareness covering central-bank digital currency evolution, market-structure electronification and basis-trade regulation, climate-policy transmission into rates and currencies, and machine-learning applications to flow and sentiment analysis; the specialization routes toward relative-value trading, macro research, central-bank and official-sector analysis, or risk management building on this foundation; the capability-development planning against this course's fifteen-section skill inventory with deliberate practice on identified gaps; the contribution orientation where practitioners document frameworks, share calibration evidence, and participate in the professional macro-markets community) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the section-siloed specialist who knows curves or currencies but not their interaction system, remediated by the synthesis arcs and capstone integration; the diagnostic-blindness, forming views never decomposed against market evidence and transmission logic, remediated by the diagnostic protocols; the capstone-as-simulation whose historical replay avoids live uncertainty and real positioning context, remediated by the authenticity requirement; the convention-sloppy professional whose analysis carries systematic day-count, quotation, or adjustment errors, remediated by the arithmetic-discipline foundations; the humility-courage imbalance oscillating between forecast overconfidence and decision paralysis, remediated by the probabilistic-positioning posture; and the destination-illusion treating course completion as mastery arrival rather than the start of cycle-by-cycle calibration, remediated by the continuous-improvement commitment with detection methods as the diagnostic.

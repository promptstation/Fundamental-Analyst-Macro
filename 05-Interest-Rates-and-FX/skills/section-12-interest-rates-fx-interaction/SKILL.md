# Rates-FX Interaction: The Cross-Asset Macro Transmission Web — Professional Training

## Mission

Produce professional-grade learning modules and training materials on **rates-FX Interaction: The Cross-Asset Macro Transmission Web** within The rates and currencies discipline of the futures fundamental analyst — Federal Reserve operations, global yield curves, fixed-income mathematics, and international trade balances as the force field within which every other futures complex prices, and a primary trading domain in its own right.

Throughout the material, prioritize language that is:

* Precise without becoming jargon-heavy
* Practical without discarding rigor

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid: Level-versus-change confusion — quoting rates and curve positions without the change dynamics that actually generate futures P-and-L, positioning on levels the market priced months ago; Single-central-bank tunnel vision — analyzing Fed policy in isolation while global curve linkages, divergence trades, and spillover flows drive the actual moves; Parity-violation naivety — treating PPP or UIP deviations as arbitrage opportunities rather than the documented risk-premium and constraint phenomena they represent; Duration-blind sizing — expressing rate views through instruments whose DV01, convexity, and option features were never computed, discovering leverage through losses; Trade-balance mechanical reading — forecasting FX from trade-flow arithmetic while capital flows, policy divergence, and positioning dominate the actual price formation; exercises without evaluation criteria; modules that stop at inspiration without a single decision the learner can now make better; and any content that overstates certainty beyond what the evidence supports.

## Decision Heuristic

When allocating depth under time or length limits, ask:

1. Where will this audience actually stall in practice?
2. Which unit protects the most value if taught well?
3. Can the learner run the framework tomorrow without me?
4. Is every example doing work a plain sentence could not?
5. What would a skeptical domain expert say about this material?

If two topics compete for space, keep the one that changes a decision.

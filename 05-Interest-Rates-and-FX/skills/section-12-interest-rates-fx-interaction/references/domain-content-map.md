## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the interaction system where rate and currency markets transmit into every other complex, completing the analyst's macro-financial transmission map: the core interaction mechanics (the differential-driven capital-flow channel where relative rate expectations move currencies through portfolio allocation per the divergence frameworks, with the flow-magnitude evidence varying by regime and currency class; the currency-imported inflation channel where depreciation raises import prices feeding domestic inflation and thus policy response, the feedback loop connecting FX to rate paths; the rate-driven carry-flow mechanics where policy divergence generates funding and investment flows whose reversal dynamics define crash risk per the carry literature; the real-rate convergence channel where inflation differentials and nominal adjustments interact toward real-rate parity with the deviation persistence documented); the equity-index transmission (the discount-rate channel where real yields drive equity valuation multiples with the duration-analogy framework treating growth stocks as long-duration assets per the index companion's valuation systems; the currency-earnings translation where multinational index earnings convert at exchange rates affecting reported growth independent of operations; the risk-appetite co-movement where rate volatility states condition equity risk premia through the vol-of-vol transmission documented across regimes; the rotation-channel dynamics where yield-curve phases drive sector rotation patterns per the asset-clock frameworks of the macro companion); the commodity transmission web (the dollar-denomination effect where broad dollar strength mechanically pressures dollar-priced commodities with the elasticity estimates varying by complex and regime per the commodity companions; the financing-cost channel where rate levels drive inventory-carrying economics, storage decisions, and thus curve structures per the theory-of-storage linkages; the demand-transmission where rate-sensitive sectors including housing, autos, and capital goods drive industrial-metal and energy consumption with the lag structures documented; the EM-producer channel where currency moves shift producer margins, export competitiveness, and supply response for major commodity exporters connecting trade balances to supply decisions); the cross-asset regime analysis (the growth-inflation regime quadrants where asset correlations reorganize by macro state, the sixty-forty and rate-equity correlation sign-flip evidence of recent decades; the policy-regime overlay where easing and tightening phases condition transmission strength and direction with the historical episode documentation; the volatility-regime interaction where calm and stressed states carry different correlation structures per the stress-testing disciplines; the regime-identification practice combining macro data, policy positioning, and market behavior into current-state assessment with transition-signal monitoring per the cycle frameworks); the transmission-measurement toolkit (the event-study methodology isolating cross-asset reactions to policy surprises and data releases with the sensitivity-coefficient estimation per the event-analysis traditions; the rolling-correlation and beta monitoring where transmission parameters get tracked for stability with regime-break detection per the econometric diagnostics; the variance-decomposition analysis attributing asset-move shares to rate, currency, and idiosyncratic factors per the VAR traditions; the scenario-propagation modeling where macro scenarios transmit through estimated relationships into multi-asset price paths per the scenario disciplines); the portfolio-level synthesis (the macro-factor portfolio mapping where multi-complex positions decompose into growth, inflation, policy, dollar, and risk-factor exposures per the risk companion; the hedging-interaction design where rate hedges of currency exposure and vice versa account for basis and carry costs per the cross-instrument disciplines; the diversification-honesty assessment where cross-complex correlation regimes determine genuine versus illusory diversification per the stress-testing traditions; the transmission-break protocols where observed cross-asset behavior contradicting established channels triggers regime-reassessment rather than forced explanation); and the whole-system view (the global-financial-cycle concept where a dominant factor synchronized by dollar funding and risk appetite drives cross-border flows and asset prices per the research tradition; the synchronization-versus-desynchronization analysis where national cycles align or diverge with relative-value consequences per the cycle companion; the systemic-risk monitoring where funding stress, leverage build-up, and correlation convergence combine into fragility assessment per the vulnerability frameworks; the humility-integration recognizing that transmission maps describe tendencies whose breakdown defines both the largest risks and the rarest opportunities) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the complex-isolation analyst whose commodity, rate, or currency views never reconcile with the transmission web they inhabit, remediated by the interaction-mechanics discipline; the static-beta modeler whose cross-asset sensitivities survive regime changes unrecalibrated, remediated by the rolling-monitoring toolkit; the correlation-comfort diversifier whose multi-asset portfolio assumes stable negative correlations that invert in stress, remediated by the regime-conditional assessment; the single-channel thinker who traces rate moves to commodities through only the dollar channel while financing and demand channels offset, remediated by the multi-channel mapping; the event-study-absent sensitivity user whose cross-asset reaction coefficients lack empirical estimation, remediated by the measurement toolkit; and the break-denier who explains away transmission failures with narratives instead of regime investigation, remediated by the break protocols with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the multi-year production-cycle dynamics through which price signals convert into physical supply waves across every commodity complex: the cycle-universal framework (the signal-to-supply lag structure where every commodity translates prices into output with delays set by biology, capital intensity, and decision structure, the master variable of cycle analysis; the cobweb-and-accelerator synthesis where naive price response amplifies swings while investment behavior adds capital-stock momentum; the cycle-anatomy standard of contraction, investment decision, construction or gestation, supply arrival, price collapse, and disinvestment with the phase-identification task facing every analyst; the amplitude-determinants comparison where cycle violence scales with supply elasticity, storage buffer size, demand elasticity, and information quality); the biological-cycle inventory (the cattle cycle's ten-to-twelve-year rhythm driven by heifer-retention decisions that simultaneously cut current supply and build future supply, the classic expansion-liquidation engine; the hog cycle compressed by prolificacy and integration; the tree-crop cycles of citrus, coffee, and cocoa where alternate bearing and multi-year establishment create supply memory; the fishery-stock dynamics where harvest pressure interacts with population recovery as a renewable-resource cobweb); the capital-cycle inventory (the mine-cycle analysis where discovery-to-production timelines of a decade mean today's shortages commission tomorrow's gluts, with the exploration-spending data as the leading indicator per the metals section; the upstream-oil investment cycle where shale's short cycle and conventional's long cycle now overlay each other creating dual-frequency supply response; the shipping-cycle parallel where vessel-orderbook-to-delivery lags generate freight cobwebs; the processing-capacity cycles in crushing, packing, and refining whose utilization swings redistribute margins across the chain); the investment-decision mechanics (the price-expectation formation where producers extrapolate, hedge, or contract against the prices guiding investment, making expectations self-influencing; the financing-condition overlay where credit availability accelerates or throttles response independent of prices, connecting to the macro course's financial-conditions systems; the cost-inflation feedback where boom-phase input costs for rigs, vessels, seed stock, and construction rise with activity, raising the break-even of late-cycle projects; the corporate-governance filter where shareholder-return disciplines of the modern era dampened the growth-maximizing investment of prior cycles); the cycle-phase assessment craft (the phase-indicator sets per complex where cattle cycles read through heifer-slaughter ratios, mines through capex-to-depletion ratios, and shipping through orderbook-to-fleet percentages; the supply-pipeline accounting tracking committed projects, construction stages, and start-up schedules into forward supply curves; the disinvestment-signal reading where closures, idling, and scrap sales mark trough formation; the cycle-position probability language acknowledging phase overlap and the policy and shock events that interrupt rhythm); the cycle-market interaction (the futures-curve reflection where deferred contracts price expected cycle supply with the contango-backwardation transitions across cycle phases; the equity-of-producers cycle where mining, energy, and processor stocks lead commodity prices through investment-signal channels; the credit-and-distress cycle where producer balance sheets deteriorate at troughs generating forced selling and consolidation; the historical-analogue discipline where cycle comparisons across eras inform but never determine because structure evolves); the policy-and-cycle entanglement (the support-program distortion where subsidies, insurance, and price floors alter trough behavior and prolong surplus phases; the strategic-reserve intervention absorbing or releasing supply against cycle phases; the environmental-and-permitting constraints lengthening supply response in mining and energy; the trade-policy timing where tariffs and quotas land differently across cycle phases with amplified or muted effects); and the cycle-synthesis analysis (the multi-complex cycle mapping where simultaneous phases across grains, livestock, energy, and metals create the cross-commodity macro environment; the supercycle concept where demand booms from industrialization align with supply underinvestment across complexes, with the term's overuse caution; the structural-versus-cyclical separation discipline distinguishing temporary phase effects from permanent shifts in cost curves, demand levels, and market structure, the central judgment task of fundamental analysis) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the phase-blind price reader who interprets high prices as strength without locating the investment response they have already triggered, remedied by the cycle-phase assessment craft; the universal-cycle-timer who applies one complex's rhythm to another's different biology and capital structure, remedied by the lag-structure comparison; the pipeline-ignorer whose supply forecasts omit committed projects and gestating herds, remedied by the forward-supply accounting; the this-time-absolute who dismisses structural change as cycle noise or vice versa without evidence, remedied by the structural-cyclical separation discipline; the governance-static modeler who assumes prior-era investment behavior persists through capital-discipline regimes, remedied by the corporate-filter analysis; and the curve-misreader who sees backwardation as permanent tightness rather than cycle-phase expression, remedied by the cycle-market interaction literacy with detection methods as the diagnostic.

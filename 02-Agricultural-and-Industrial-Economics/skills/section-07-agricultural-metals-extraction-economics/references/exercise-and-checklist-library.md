# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Master metals extraction economics through taxonomy (precious-base-ferrous-battery systems)
**Scenario:** provide a realistic agricultural and industrial economics scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** master metals extraction economics through taxonomy (precious-base-ferrous-battery systems), extraction core (mine lifecycle timelines, grade decline, cash versus AISC cost curves, by-product economics, reserve classification), investment cycles (exploration response, capital discipline eras, project pipelines, jurisdiction risk, scrap supply), the smelting layer (TC-RC signals, capacity geography, quality distinctions), demand systems (industrial elasticity with China dominance, electrification thesis, precious demand anatomy, substitution and thrifting), market structure (LME warehouses and queues, OTC duality with the nickel episode, shadow stocks, carry trades), and price synthesis (soft cost support, inventory relationships, dollar effects, financialization intensity), avoiding agriculture analogies, cost-floor literalism, by-product blindness, TC-RC illiteracy, visible-stock-only analysis, and China monolith modeling failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.

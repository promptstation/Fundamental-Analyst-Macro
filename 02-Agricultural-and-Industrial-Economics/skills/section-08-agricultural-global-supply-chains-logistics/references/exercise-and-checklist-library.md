# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Command supply chain logistics through system architecture (origin-destination nodes
**Scenario:** provide a realistic agricultural and industrial economics scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** command supply chain logistics through system architecture (origin-destination nodes, modal economics, chokepoint geography, capacity constraints), freight economics (bulk-container-tanker structures, rate systems and indices, shipbuilding cobweb, bunker costs, demurrage), trade-flow analysis (export competition windows, delivered-price arbitrage computation, disruption history, inland regulation), storage and handling (elevator systems and basis, port stocks, storage-cost integration, quality preservation), energy and metals logistics (pipeline differentials, LNG carriers, bulk metals shipping, container visibility), the data ecosystem (rate publications, AIS flows, throughput statistics, basis networks, congestion indicators), and risk frameworks (failure-point analysis, resilience shifts, sanctions logistics, climate exposure), avoiding teleporting commodities, term confusion, chokepoint panic, static capacity, freight-ignored spreads, and shadow blindness failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.

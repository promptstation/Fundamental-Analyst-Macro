## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the text and digital-exhaust discipline whose processing of news, documents, job postings, and online activity extracts signals from the largest and least structured alternative-data category: the digital-exhaust taxonomy (the news-and-media streams where publication volume, sentiment, and topic coverage track attention and narrative shifts per the media-analytics traditions; the official-document flows where regulatory filings, tender notices, shipping documents, and government publications carry structured information within unstructured formats per the document-processing literatures; the employment-signal category where job postings reveal corporate expansion, technology adoption, and sector health ahead of payroll statistics per the labor-market-intelligence research; the consumer-voice category spanning reviews, social media, search trends, and app-store activity whose aggregate patterns proxy demand and sentiment per the consumer-analytics traditions; the web-traffic-and-infrastructure signals where site visits, download patterns, and digital-service usage track economic activity per the web-analytics literatures); the text-processing toolkit (the extraction-and-parsing systems where HTML content, PDF documents, and structured text convert into analysis-ready formats per the acquisition and processing disciplines; the natural-language-processing foundations where tokenization, entity recognition, and topic classification organize text at scale with the pre-trained-model application and fine-tuning judgment per the NLP traditions; the sentiment-analysis-methods spanning dictionary approaches, supervised classifiers, and large-language-model assessment whose accuracy varies by domain requiring financial and commodity vocabulary specialization per the sentiment literatures; the event-extraction systems where structured facts including dates, quantities, locations, and actors emerge from narrative text per the information-extraction traditions); the news-and-narrative analytics (the news-flow-indicator construction where article volume, source tier, and sentiment by entity and topic create quantified attention measures per the media-monitoring traditions; the narrative-shift-detection where dominant story evolution tracks regime changes in market interpretation per the narrative-tracking disciplines of the index companion; the cross-language-monitoring where non-English sources covering producing regions provide information advantages with the translation-technology integration per the global-intelligence traditions; the misinformation-and-manipulation awareness where coordinated campaigns and unreliable sources require credibility-weighting systems per the source-hierarchy disciplines); the employment-signal analytics (the job-posting-aggregation where corporate hiring patterns by sector, geography, and skill reveal expansion and contraction signals per the labor-intelligence literatures; the skills-and-technology-tracking where posting content reveals technology adoption and strategic direction ahead of financial disclosure per the corporate-intelligence traditions; the nowcast-integration where hiring indicators feed employment nowcasts complementing official labor statistics per the mixed-frequency traditions; the sector-comparison-analysis where relative posting momentum across industries signals rotation and cycle dynamics per the sector-analysis frameworks); the consumer-signal analytics (the search-trend-analysis where query volumes proxy interest and demand with the documented correlation to consumption and market behavior per the search-data literatures; the review-and-rating-mining where product feedback aggregates reveal quality perception and competitive dynamics per the consumer-intelligence traditions; the app-economy-observation where download rankings and usage estimates track digital-service demand per the app-analytics literatures; the social-sentiment-caution where platform signals carry manipulation, bot, and selection-bias contamination requiring the skepticism disciplines documented across sentiment research); the document-intelligence applications (the regulatory-filing-processing where structured extraction from corporate and commodity-market filings automates disclosure monitoring per the filing-analytics traditions; the tender-and-procurement-tracking where government and corporate purchasing notices reveal investment and demand intentions per the procurement-intelligence literatures; the shipping-document-analysis where bills of lading and customs records quantify trade flows at transaction level per the trade-data traditions; the contract-and-announcement monitoring where deal announcements, force-majeure notices, and corporate communications carry market-moving information per the event-detection systems); and the text-analytics practice (the pipeline-engineering discipline where text processing operates at scale through maintained pipelines with quality monitoring per the processing traditions; the validation-against-outcomes where text-derived indicators face the same accuracy and decay testing as quantitative sources per the verification disciplines; the LLM-integration-frontier where large-language-model processing expands capability with the hallucination-awareness, cost-management, and reproducibility disciplines emerging practice requires; the integration-workflow design where text signals feed fundamental analysis through documented indicator construction per the integration principles) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the sentiment-score-trader whose positions follow unvalidated sentiment classifiers with domain-inappropriate training, remediated by the validation discipline; the bot-contaminated analyst whose social signals include coordinated inauthentic activity undetected, remediated by the credibility-weighting systems; the English-only monitor whose producing-region intelligence misses native-language sources, remediated by the cross-language capabilities; the LLM-hallucination-launderer whose model-generated extractions enter analysis without verification against source documents, remediated by the verification requirements; the exhaust-permanence-assumer whose digital-signal relationships survive platform and behavior evolution unrecalibrated, remediated by the decay-monitoring discipline; and the text-silo analyst whose unstructured observations never integrate with quantitative and fundamental analysis, remediated by the integration workflow with detection methods as the diagnostic.

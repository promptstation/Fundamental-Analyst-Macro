## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the evaluation discipline that separates genuine alternative-data edge from statistical mirage, the quality gate protecting capital from unverified signals: the signal-evaluation framework (the hypothesis-first discipline where candidate signals begin with explicit economic rationale for why the observation should predict the outcome before any statistical testing, the theory-anchoring that prevents data-mining per the specification traditions of the econometrics companion; the evaluation-pipeline architecture where signals progress through descriptive analysis, predictive testing, out-of-sample validation, and economic-value assessment in documented stages per the quality-gate architectures; the fitness-for-purpose criteria where signal evaluation standards match the intended use from balance-sheet input through standalone trade generation with proportionate rigor; the evaluation-documentation systems where every tested signal carries complete methodology records enabling replication and audit per the research-integrity traditions); the predictive-testing methodology (the lead-lag analysis where cross-correlation and information-timing tests establish whether the signal genuinely precedes the target outcome versus coincident or lagged relationship per the lead-lag disciplines; the information-coefficient measurement where correlation, rank statistics, and mutual information quantify predictive strength with the statistical-significance assessment accounting for sample size per the signal-quality traditions; the conditional-performance analysis where signal accuracy varies across regimes, seasons, and market states requiring the conditional decomposition per the regime-conditioning disciplines; the incremental-value testing where signals get evaluated against existing information sets determining marginal contribution rather than standalone accuracy per the information-economics framework); the backtesting discipline (the point-in-time reconstruction where historical signal values use only data available at each historical moment with the vintage-control systems preventing look-ahead contamination per the real-time-data traditions of the forecasting companion; the survivorship-and-coverage-bias management where historical dataset completeness differs from current coverage requiring explicit bias assessment per the backtesting-hygiene literatures; the multiple-testing correction where the volume of evaluated signals, features, and parameters inflates false-discovery rates demanding deflation adjustments per the statistical-rigor traditions; the transaction-cost-and-capacity integration where paper backtests incorporate realistic execution costs and size limits per the implementation-shortfall disciplines of the CFA companion); the overfitting-defense systems (the degrees-of-freedom accounting where feature counts, parameter choices, and selection steps get tracked with complexity penalties per the model-selection traditions; the cross-validation-methodology where temporal data splits respecting time ordering replace random splitting that leaks future information per the validation literatures; the purged-and-embargoed techniques where observation overlap and serial correlation receive specialized validation treatment per the financial-machine-learning traditions; the simplicity-preference discipline where parsimonious signals with economic rationale outrank complex constructions of equal backtest performance per the parsimony traditions); the alpha-decay analysis (the decay-mechanism taxonomy where signal erosion occurs through crowding diffusion, source-coverage changes, structural market evolution, and data-quality degradation with distinct detection and response patterns per the edge-lifecycle literatures; the decay-monitoring systems where live signal performance gets tracked against backtest expectations with rolling-window analysis detecting degradation per the performance-monitoring traditions; the crowding-assessment where vendor client counts, publication exposure, and positioning evidence estimate signal diffusion per the exclusivity-economics of the landscape section; the lifecycle-management practice where signals receive staged capital allocation as evidence accumulates and retirement protocols as decay confirms per the strategy-lifecycle disciplines); the economic-value assessment (the decision-improvement measurement where signal value expresses through better forecasts, earlier detection, or improved positioning rather than abstract accuracy metrics per the value-assessment traditions; the cost-benefit-integration where data acquisition, processing infrastructure, and analyst time costs face realized value with the honest-attribution challenges documented per the data-economics framework; the portfolio-contribution-analysis where signal-derived positions receive performance attribution separating signal value from market beta and execution effects per the attribution traditions; the counterfactual-assessment where decision outcomes with and without signal input get compared through documented analysis where controlled experiment is impossible); and the evaluation-culture practices (the skepticism-by-default posture where new signals face adversarial review seeking disconfirmation before adoption per the falsification disciplines; the replication-requirement where significant signal claims reproduce across independent samples, methods, and analysts before production weight per the verification traditions; the failure-documentation discipline where rejected signals and failed evaluations accumulate as institutional knowledge preventing repeated exploration per the knowledge-management systems; the calibration-integration where signal-confidence assessments face outcome tracking feeding probability-judgment improvement per the calibration traditions across this curriculum family) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the hypothesis-free miner whose signal discovery proceeds through feature-search until statistics cooperate, remediated by the theory-first discipline; the look-ahead-contaminated backtester whose spectacular historical performance rests on future-information leakage, remediated by the point-in-time reconstruction; the multiple-testing ignoramus whose hundred evaluated signals produce five false discoveries celebrated as edge, remediated by the correction frameworks; the overfitting-collector whose complex models fit noise with parameter counts rivaling observations, remediated by the defense systems; the decay-denial operator whose live signal degradation gets explained away until capital losses force retirement, remediated by the monitoring and lifecycle discipline; and the accuracy-metric-obsessor whose signal evaluation stops at correlation without economic-value assessment, remediated by the value-measurement framework with detection methods as the diagnostic.

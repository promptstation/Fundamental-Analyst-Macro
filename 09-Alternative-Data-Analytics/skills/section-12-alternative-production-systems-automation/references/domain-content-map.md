## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the production engineering that transforms analyst-scale experiments into reliable operational systems sustaining daily intelligence: the production-architecture design (the system-requirements definition where analytical needs, update frequency, latency tolerance, and reliability expectations specify infrastructure requirements before technology selection per the systems-design traditions; the environment-architecture spanning development, testing, and production separation where analysis code graduates through validation before operational deployment per the software-engineering disciplines; the infrastructure-selection judgment where cloud services, local systems, and hybrid approaches match workload characteristics and cost constraints per the platform-selection frameworks; the scalability-design-principles where systems accommodate source growth, frequency increases, and historical-depth extension without redesign per the scaling traditions); the pipeline-production engineering (the orchestration-systems deployment where Airflow-style schedulers manage collection, processing, and publication dependencies with retry and alert logic per the orchestration literatures; the idempotent-pipeline-design where reruns produce consistent results enabling safe failure recovery per the engineering-reliability traditions; the incremental-processing-optimization where only new and changed data flows through expensive transformation stages per the efficiency disciplines; the pipeline-versioning where processing logic changes deploy through controlled releases with rollback capability per the deployment traditions); the data-infrastructure management (the storage-tier-architecture where hot analytical databases, warm query systems, and cold archives balance access speed against cost per the storage-design traditions; the data-catalog-systems where dataset documentation, lineage tracking, and discovery tools maintain estate navigability per the catalog traditions of the BI companion; the access-and-security-management where permissions, encryption, and audit logging govern data handling per the security-architecture disciplines; the retention-and-lifecycle-policies where data aging, archival, and deletion follow documented schedules meeting analytical and compliance requirements per the records-management traditions); the monitoring-and-observability systems (the pipeline-health-monitoring where execution success, duration trends, and throughput metrics receive continuous tracking with alert thresholds per the observability disciplines; the data-quality-surveillance where automated checks on completeness, distribution, and freshness run alongside processing with anomaly escalation per the quality-monitoring systems; the dependency-failure-detection where upstream source outages, API changes, and vendor disruptions trigger notification and contingency activation per the dependency-management traditions; the cost-monitoring-systems where cloud consumption, API usage, and vendor charges receive tracking against budgets per the cost-management disciplines); the reliability-engineering practice (the failure-mode-analysis where system components face documented failure scenarios with impact assessment and mitigation per the reliability traditions; the redundancy-and-failover-design where critical collection and processing paths maintain backup capability per the resilience disciplines; the disaster-recovery-planning where data loss, system failure, and extended outage scenarios receive documented recovery procedures per the continuity traditions; the incident-management-workflows where production failures follow triage, communication, resolution, and post-incident-review protocols per the incident disciplines); the automation-strategy discipline (the automation-candidate-assessment where repetitive analytical tasks face cost-benefit evaluation for automation investment per the automation-economics traditions; the human-in-the-loop-design where judgment-requiring steps retain analyst participation with automation handling preparation and presentation per the hybrid-workflow literatures; the alert-fatigue-management where automated notification volume gets tuned preserving signal salience per the attention-management disciplines across this curriculum family; the documentation-and-knowledge-systems where automated systems carry operating manuals, runbooks, and architecture documentation enabling maintenance beyond original builders per the knowledge-management traditions); and the system-evolution management (the technical-debt-tracking where expedient solutions accumulate documented obligations whose repayment schedules prevent system decay per the engineering-management traditions; the technology-refresh-planning where language, library, and platform evolution receives managed adoption per the frontier-monitoring disciplines; the capability-extension-roadmaps where system growth follows analytical-need demonstration rather than technical temptation per the minimum-viable-system traditions; the team-scaling-considerations where system complexity growth matches maintenance capability per the sustainability disciplines) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the laptop-production operator whose critical intelligence depends on personal-machine scripts without backup, monitoring, or documentation, remediated by the production-architecture discipline; the pipeline-jenga stack whose interdependent scripts fail cascade-style because idempotence and isolation were never designed, remediated by the engineering principles; the storage-swamp accumulator whose undocumented datasets across ad-hoc locations prevent discovery and quality assurance, remediated by the infrastructure-management systems; the monitoring-blind automator whose pipelines fail silently for weeks corrupting downstream analysis, remediated by the observability systems; the over-automator whose judgment tasks get forced into brittle automation producing confident errors at scale, remediated by the human-in-the-loop design; and the technical-debt-denier whose system accumulates fragility until maintenance collapse forces rebuild, remediated by the evolution-management discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

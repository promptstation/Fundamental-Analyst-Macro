## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Operate alternative-data programs through architecture design (maturity models.
2. Build-buy-partner strategy, team structures, roadmaps), portfolio management (source governance, usage tracking, differentiation strategy, asset documentation), collaboration systems (workflow integration, prototype-to-production pathways, skill boundaries.
3. Review practices), decision integration (signal-to-decision workflows, confidence standards, feedback loops, governance oversight), program evaluation (value measurement, cost efficiency, capability reviews, direction adjustment), risk and compliance operations (compliance integration, operational risk, knowledge continuity.
4. Audit readiness), and culture foundations (evidence culture, experimentation tolerance, cross-functional respect, continuous learning), avoiding pilot purgatory, subscription sprawl, organizational silos, feedback orphans, value opacity, and compliance bypass failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the program-level discipline where individual analytical capability scales into sustained organizational alternative-data practice: the program-architecture design (the capability-maturity-model where alternative-data programs progress from ad-hoc exploration through systematic evaluation to industrialized operation with the stage-appropriate investment discipline per the maturity traditions; the build-buy-partner strategy where internal capability, vendor data, and specialist partnerships combine across domains with the make-or-buy analysis per the sourcing frameworks; the team-structure options spanning centralized data-science functions, embedded analyst-engineer hybrids, and federated models with the coordination and specialization tradeoffs per the organizational-design literatures; the roadmap-development practice where capability growth follows documented analytical priorities rather than data-availability temptation per the strategic-planning disciplines); the portfolio-management of data assets (the source-portfolio-governance where active data subscriptions face periodic value review, renewal negotiation, and retirement decisions per the vendor-management traditions; the usage-tracking systems where data-asset consumption measurement reveals unused subscriptions and high-value sources informing portfolio optimization per the utilization-analytics disciplines; the exclusivity-and-differentiation strategy where proprietary collection capability and exclusive arrangements sustain edge against vendor-diffusion per the edge-lifecycle frameworks; the data-asset-documentation where sources, methodologies, validation records, and integration points maintain institutional knowledge per the catalog traditions); the analyst-engineer collaboration (the workflow-integration design where engineering builds infrastructure and analysts define requirements with the translation-layer practices bridging vocabulary gaps per the collaboration traditions; the prototyping-to-production pathway where analyst notebooks graduate into maintained systems through documented handoff and joint ownership per the pipeline traditions; the skill-boundary-management where T-shaped development gives analysts engineering literacy and engineers domain knowledge per the capability-development disciplines; the review-and-pairing practices where joint code review and analysis review maintain quality across skill boundaries per the peer-review traditions); the decision-integration systems (the signal-to-decision-workflow where validated alternative indicators enter the fundamental frameworks and trading processes through defined channels per the EIC-integration disciplines; the confidence-communication-standards where alternative-data assessments carry calibrated reliability statements matching validation evidence per the uncertainty-communication traditions; the feedback-loop-construction where decision outcomes trace back to contributing signals enabling attribution and improvement per the calibration systems; the governance-and-oversight where program outputs face review committees, audit trails, and compliance verification per the governance traditions of the compliance section); the program-evaluation practice (the value-measurement-frameworks where program contribution assesses through decision improvement, forecast accuracy, and portfolio attribution with the honest-causality limitations documented per the measurement traditions; the cost-efficiency-analysis where total program costs across data, infrastructure, and personnel face value delivery with the benchmark comparisons per the operational-economics disciplines; the capability-assessment-reviews where team skills, system maturity, and process quality face periodic evaluation against objectives per the assessment traditions; the strategic-direction-adjustment where program evolution responds to measured performance, market evolution, and technology frontiers per the adaptive-management disciplines); the risk-and-compliance operations (the compliance-integration where legal and ethical review embeds into source acquisition and use-case development workflows per the compliance-operations of the prior section; the operational-risk-management where system failures, data corruption, and vendor disruptions receive risk assessment and mitigation per the reliability traditions; the key-person-and-knowledge-risk where documentation, cross-training, and system design prevent capability concentration per the continuity disciplines; the audit-and-examination-readiness where program records support regulatory examination and internal audit per the assurance traditions); and the program-culture foundations (the evidence-culture establishment where alternative-data claims face the same verification standards as traditional analysis preventing exotic-source credulity per the skepticism traditions; the experimentation-tolerance where systematic trial-and-error proceeds within documented boundaries accepting failure rates as information costs per the innovation-culture literatures; the cross-functional-respect where data engineering, fundamental analysis, trading, and compliance contributions receive equal professional valuation per the collaboration cultures; the continuous-learning-orientation where methodology currency through research tracking, conference participation, and skill development sustains program capability per the learning-ecosystem traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the pilot-purgatory program whose alternative-data experiments never graduate to production integration or formal retirement, remediated by the pathway and governance disciplines; the subscription-sprawl victim whose data portfolio accumulates unused sources because renewal reviews never occur, remediated by the portfolio-governance systems; the analyst-engineer-siloed organization where infrastructure and analysis develop in mutual ignorance producing fragile or irrelevant systems, remediated by the collaboration design; the feedback-orphan program whose signals influence decisions without outcome attribution preventing improvement, remediated by the loop-construction discipline; the value-unmeasured program whose budget survival depends on narrative rather than demonstrated contribution, remediated by the measurement frameworks; and the compliance-bypass operator whose speed culture treats legal review as obstacle until enforcement pauses the program, remediated by the integration practice with detection methods as the diagnostic.

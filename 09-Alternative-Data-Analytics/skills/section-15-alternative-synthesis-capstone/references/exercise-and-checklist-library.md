# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Integrate the complete alternative-data discipline through synthesis (end-to-end observation-to-edge chains
**Scenario:** provide a realistic alternative data analytics scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** integrate the complete alternative-data discipline through synthesis (end-to-end observation-to-edge chains, technical-analytical-fundamental triad, edge-lifecycle awareness), domain mastery (physical and digital observation command, official-data bridges, cross-domain fusion), engineering-professional integration (production reliability, evaluation rigor, compliance by design, documentation discipline), diagnostic mastery (source-signal-system-vendor audit protocols), capstone execution (complete operating systems with source portfolios, production pipelines, domain analysis, evaluation records, integration outputs, documentation, decision journals, live defense), professional identity (analyst integration, skeptic-builder paradox, fundamental service, continuous evolution), and forward paths (frontier awareness, specialization routes, capability planning, contribution orientation), avoiding technique collecting, edge-less enthusiasm, late compliance, isolated systems, demo capstones, and destination illusions failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.

# Domain Content Map — Examination Strategy and Study Systems: Passing All Three Levels

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Pass all three levels through study-system design (schedule architecture, sequencing strategy, resource discipline, active recall application), weight optimization (weighting analysis, LOS targeting, diminishing returns, evolution tracking), question practice (item-set mastery, vignette management, constructed-response craft, mock protocols), exam execution (interface familiarity, time budgets, elimination techniques, stress management), failure navigation (results analysis, retake adjustment, resilience practice, sunk-cost honesty), charter transition (experience documentation, application navigation, knowledge maintenance, integration launch), and study wisdom (peer leverage, work integration, journey architecture, process orientation), avoiding passive rereading, schedule optimism, question avoidance, mock skipping, response neglect, failure spirals, and credential-only pursuit failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the meta-discipline of CFA examination success whose study-system design and exam-execution craft determine whether knowledge becomes credential: the study-system design (the schedule-architecture where three-hundred-hour budgets decompose across weeks with topic sequencing, review cycles, and mock-exam windows explicitly planned against work and life constraints per the commitment-analysis of the Program section; the topic-sequencing-strategy where quantitative methods and FSA early placement pays compound interest through downstream deployment while ethics distributes across the entire preparation per the integration-point mapping; the resource-selection-discipline where official curriculum, question banks, prep-provider materials, and mock exams combine with the depth-coverage tradeoff analysis preventing resource-scattering per the ecosystem literacy; the active-recall-and-spaced-repetition application where learning-science methods replace passive rereading with the flashcard, self-testing, and interval systems documented to improve retention per the learning traditions); the topic-weight optimization (the weighting-analysis where topic-area exam weights across levels guide time allocation with the ethics-adjustment significance and high-weight-topic priority understanding; the LOS-level-targeting where Bloom-verb analysis of Learning Outcome Statements directs study depth per statement preventing both under and over-preparation of specific requirements; the diminishing-returns-awareness where marginal study hours allocate toward weak high-weight topics rather than comfortable mastery deepening; the cross-level-content-evolution tracking where topic treatment deepens across levels requiring recognition of what Level II and III add beyond Level I foundations per the architecture literacy); the question-practice discipline (the item-set-mastery for Levels I-II where question-bank volume, explanation-study, and wrong-answer-analysis build the pattern recognition multiple-choice performance requires; the vignette-time-management where Level II case-sets demand reading-strategy, question-first approaches, and per-vignette time budgets rehearsed under simulated conditions; the constructed-response-craft for Level III where answer-structure, command-verb compliance, show-your-work conventions, and concise-terminology practice determine scoring on essay-format questions; the mock-exam-protocol where full-length simulations under real timing and interface conditions reveal stamina, pacing, and knowledge gaps with the post-mock analysis discipline extracting maximum diagnostic value); the exam-execution craft (the interface-and-logistics-familiarity where computer-based testing environment, calculator policies, and testing-center procedures receive advance rehearsal eliminating execution-day surprises; the time-budget-management where per-question allocation, skip-and-return strategies, and flagging discipline optimize completion with the point-value-awareness across question formats; the answer-elimination-techniques where wrong-option identification, qualifier-word attention, and calculation-verification shortcuts improve accuracy under pressure; the stress-and-stamina-management where sleep, nutrition, focus-training, and anxiety-protocols support peak cognitive performance across multi-hour sessions per the performance-psychology traditions); the failure-and-retake navigation (the results-analysis discipline where per-topic performance bands guide remediation targeting after unsuccessful attempts; the retake-strategy-adjustment where preparation modifications address diagnosed failure modes rather than repeating approaches that failed; the psychological-resilience-practice where setback processing, motivation maintenance, and attempt-sequencing decisions sustain multi-year journeys; the sunk-cost-honesty where continuation decisions weigh genuine capability development against credential pursuit per the professional-identity frameworks); the charter-completion transition (the work-experience-documentation where qualified hours get recorded, verified, and articulated meeting Institute requirements per the membership pathway; the reference-and-application-navigation where professional endorsements and application review complete the credential process; the knowledge-maintenance-system where post-charter currency through continuing education, curriculum-evolution awareness, and applied practice prevents decay per the professional-identity obligations; the designation-integration-launch where charter knowledge explicitly connects to the holder's futures specialization through deliberate application projects per the capstone traditions); and the study-craft wisdom (the peer-and-group-leverage where study partnerships, forums, and local-society connections provide accountability, explanation, and morale with the selection-quality awareness; the work-study-integration where professional application of curriculum topics reinforces learning with the employer-communication about preparation commitments; the multi-year-journey-architecture where three-level progression across career stages receives life-planning integration rather than collision; the process-over-outcome orientation where genuine capability development rather than passage alone defines success, producing both better preparation and better professional identity per the mastery-over-memorization discipline) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the passive-rereader whose familiarity-based confidence meets application questions the recognition study never built capability for, remediated by the active-recall systems; the schedule-optimist whose plan assumes perfect weeks until work crises consume study blocks without buffer, remediated by the realistic-architecture discipline; the question-avoider whose reading-heavy preparation meets unfamiliar formats on exam day, remediated by the practice-volume discipline; the mock-skipper whose first full-length simulation occurs during the actual exam, remediated by the simulation protocol; the constructed-response-neglecter whose Level III essay practice never happened under time pressure, remediated by the response-craft rehearsal; the failure-spiral victim whose retake repeats failed methods without diagnostic adjustment, remediated by the results-analysis discipline; and the credential-only pursuer whose passage without integration leaves practice unchanged and knowledge decaying, remediated by the process-orientation and integration-launch with detection methods as the diagnostic.

# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Integrate the CFA body of knowledge through curriculum synthesis (topic-interconnection web
**Scenario:** provide a realistic the CFA body of knowledge scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** integrate the CFA body of knowledge through curriculum synthesis (topic-interconnection web, three-level progression logic, half-life maintenance), futures integration (macro-quantitative fusion, instrument-pricing mastery, physical-financial bridge, portfolio perspective), ethical practice (Standards application, market-integrity contribution, conflict navigation, research integrity), diagnostic mastery (valuation-coherence, financial-quality, risk-completeness, ethics-communication audits), capstone execution (multi-asset packages with valuation anchoring, FSA assessment, derivative expression, factor decomposition, IPS documentation, GIPS measurement, Standards review, decision journal, live defense), professional identity (charter-analyst profile, lifelong learning, community participation, integrity core), and forward evolution (frontier awareness, specialization routes, capability planning, contribution orientation), avoiding topic silos, credential separation, ethics compartmentalization, knowledge decay, community isolation, and destination illusions failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.

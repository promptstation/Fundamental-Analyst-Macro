## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Consolidates the complete CFA body of knowledge and its deliberate integration into futures fundamental analysis practice, the credential-capability synthesis this course exists to produce: the body-of-knowledge synthesis — the unified curriculum map (the topic-interconnection web where ethics governs all application, quantitative methods power every analysis, economics frames the environment, FSA reveals issuer reality, corporate issuers explain capital behavior, asset-class topics value the instruments, and portfolio management unifies everything into client-serving process, with the connection-mastery defining true Program command versus topic-isolated passage; the three-level-progression logic where comprehension, application, and synthesis stages build the same knowledge into progressively deployed capability, the vertical-integration the compartmentalization anti-pattern defeats; the knowledge-half-life awareness where curriculum revisions, market evolution, and skill decay demand maintenance systems per the professional-identity obligations); the futures-integration synthesis (the macro-quantitative fusion where Program economics and quantitative methods combine with the macroeconomics and econometrics companion depth into complete macro-analytical capability serving rate, currency, and index futures analysis per those courses' frameworks; the instrument-pricing-mastery fusion where Program derivatives and fixed income integrate with the quantitative-finance companion into full instrument command spanning valuation, Greeks, curves, and strategy per the derivatives discipline; the physical-financial bridge where Program alternatives and equity knowledge connect to the agricultural, energy, and metals economics companions, enabling analysis spanning financial claims and physical supply chains per the commodity courses; the portfolio-perspective addition where Program portfolio management supplies the allocation, risk-budgeting, and performance frameworks that position futures analysis within whole-portfolio context per the portfolio disciplines); the ethical-practice synthesis (the Standards-application-to-analysis where material-nonpublic-information boundaries, reasonable-basis requirements, and communication standards govern fundamental research practice per the ethics foundations applied daily; the market-integrity-contribution where accurate analysis, honest uncertainty communication, and manipulation-avoidance serve the capital-market function the charter exists to support per the Code traditions; the conflict-navigation practice where employer, client, and personal interests get managed through disclosure and priority systems per the Standards methodology; the research-integrity-discipline where forecast records, attribution honesty, and bias awareness meet the Program's professional standards per the calibration traditions across this curriculum family); the diagnostic mastery — auditing analysis through Program lenses (the valuation-coherence audit where any market view faces Program-framework interrogation across discount-rate consistency, cash-flow logic, multiple justification, and portfolio implication per the valuation disciplines; the financial-quality audit where issuer and sector conclusions face FSA-based quality assessment including accrual patterns, cash-flow triangulation, and disclosure examination per the statement-analysis section; the risk-completeness audit where positions and portfolios face duration, factor, liquidity, and tail-risk measurement per the risk frameworks spanning Program and companion courses; the ethics-and-communication audit where research outputs face Standards compliance, presentation integrity, and audience-appropriateness review per the ethics and communication traditions); the capstone project — demonstrating integrated mastery (the capstone specification requiring candidates to produce and defend a complete futures-market analysis package explicitly deploying Program frameworks alongside companion-course systems: a multi-asset fundamental view spanning at least one rate, one equity-index, and one commodity complex with Program-consistent valuation anchoring including ERP and cost-of-capital reasoning, FSA-informed sector and issuer assessment where relevant, derivative-based expression analysis with parity-consistent pricing verification, factor and risk decomposition of the proposed positioning per the pricing-model frameworks, an IPS-style documentation of objectives-constraints-risk-tolerance for the analytical mandate, GIPS-aware performance-measurement design for evaluating the resulting strategy, explicit Standards-compliance review of the research process, and a decision journal with calibration tracking per the professional-practice traditions; the integration-visibility requirement where Program-knowledge deployment gets explicitly identified and justified throughout the package, demonstrating the synthesis this course certifies; the evaluation criteria covering framework-application correctness, cross-topic integration depth, quantitative precision, ethical-review quality, communication effectiveness, and demonstrated learning from outcome; the defense-and-portfolio development where capstone work receives written case documentation and oral defense applying the constructed-response and professional-communication standards of Level III); the professional-identity synthesis (the charter-analyst identity where the CFA body of knowledge, ethical commitment, and futures-market specialization combine into a professional profile distinct from both generic charterholders and uncredentialed specialists; the lifelong-learning architecture where continuing education, curriculum-currency, and deliberate practice sustain capability across career decades per the maintenance disciplines; the community-participation orientation where society involvement, publication contribution, and mentoring extend credential value into professional contribution per the Institute traditions; the integrity-identity-core where the Standards internalization defines professional character whose consistency under pressure constitutes the charter's deepest meaning per the ethics foundations); and the forward path (the discipline-frontier awareness covering curriculum evolution in private markets, fintech, climate, and data topics whose integration extends the foundation per the revision-cadence monitoring; the specialization-evolution routes toward portfolio management, research leadership, risk management, or continued futures specialization building on the credential; the capability-development planning against this course's fifteen-section skill inventory and the Program's topic map with deliberate practice on identified gaps; the contribution-orientation where charterholders document methods, mentor candidates, and uphold Standards actively advancing the profession whose reputation the credential shares) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the topic-siloed charterholder whose passed examinations never integrated into deployed analytical capability, remediated by the interconnection-web mastery and capstone integration; the credential-practice-separator whose charter coexists with unchanged analytical habits, remediated by the integration-visibility requirement; the ethics-compartmentalizer whose Standards knowledge activates for exam vignettes but not daily research decisions, remediated by the application-synthesis discipline; the knowledge-decayer whose post-charter practice rests on preparation-era understanding while markets and curriculum evolve, remediated by the maintenance architecture; the community-isolationist whose charter delivers credential without professional-network and contribution participation, remediated by the participation orientation; and the destination-illusion treating charter award as professional arrival rather than the maintenance-and-application beginning, remediated by the lifelong-learning architecture with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Unit 2 — Capstone integration

**Purpose:** integrate every capability above on one realistic problem end to end.

**Structure:** the learner takes a single problem in the CFA body of knowledge through each unit's framework in sequence, producing a coherent deliverable that mirrors real professional documentation. Evaluation criteria: internal consistency across artifacts, evidence discipline, honest trade-off statements, and demonstrable use of each unit's framework.

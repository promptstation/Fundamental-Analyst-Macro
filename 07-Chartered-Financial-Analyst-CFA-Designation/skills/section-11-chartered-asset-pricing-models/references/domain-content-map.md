# Domain Content Map — Asset Pricing Models: CAPM, APT, and Multi-Factor Systems

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Master asset pricing through CAPM foundations (derivation architecture, beta estimation practice, application systems, empirical literature), multi-factor evolution (APT framework, Fama-French progression, factor-type distinction, interpretation debates), term-structure and credit pricing (rate models, structural-reduced-form credit, premium integration), application systems (cost of capital, performance evaluation, factor investing, risk management), limitation literacy (error propagation, regime dependence, structural change.
2. Model pluralism), behavioral extensions (pricing alternatives, friction integration, SDF unification), and futures synthesis (commodity pricing connection, cross-asset architecture, trade construction, complete literacy), avoiding single-factor discounting, beta illusions, mechanism partisanship, regime blindness, frictionless theory, and kernel ignorance failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the asset-pricing theory whose models connect risk to expected return across the curriculum and underpin cost-of-capital, performance-evaluation, and factor-analysis practice: the CAPM foundation (the derivation-architecture where homogeneous expectations, frictionless markets, and efficient-frontier logic produce the security market line through the market-portfolio tangency condition, the assumption-chain whose relaxation sequence builds understanding per the theory-mastery disciplines; the beta-estimation practice where regression-derived market sensitivity carries estimation-error, time-variation, and adjustment controversies the curriculum examines with practitioner awareness; the CAPM-application-system where cost-of-equity computation feeds WACC and valuation models across the equity and corporate topics per the discount-rate assembly of the valuation sections; the empirical-CAPM-literature where flat-SML findings, size effects, and beta anomalies motivate the multi-factor evolution with the theory-evidence tension honestly presented); the multi-factor evolution (the APT-framework where arbitrage logic across multiple risk factors produces expected-return relations without market-portfolio assumptions, the Ross contribution whose flexibility and factor-ambiguity define its character; the Fama-French progression where three-factor market-size-value models extended through momentum, profitability, and investment factors in the five-factor specification document the empirical factor discovery sequence; the macro-versus-statistical-factor distinction where economic factors carry interpretability while statistical extraction captures variance without meaning, the factor-selection judgment for application contexts; the factor-risk-premium interpretation debates where risk-compensation and mispricing explanations compete with the practical-agnosticism that deploys factors regardless of mechanism per the factor-literature balance of the index companion); the term-structure-and-credit pricing (the interest-rate-models coverage where Vasicek, Cox-Ingersoll-Ross, and Ho-Lee frameworks model rate evolution for contingent-claim pricing at the Program's required depth per the fixed-income section; the credit-risk-pricing where structural Merton-style approaches treating equity as call options on assets connect to reduced-form default-intensity models with the spread-decomposition applications; the term-premium-and-expectations integration where yield-curve pricing combines expected-path and premium components per the curve-analytics of the rates companion); the pricing-model application systems (the cost-of-capital-practice where model selection, parameter estimation, and cross-validation determine discount rates whose errors propagate through every valuation per the discount-rate disciplines; the performance-evaluation deployment where Jensen's alpha, Treynor-Mazuy, and Henriksson-Merton models assess manager skill against factor benchmarks per the evaluation section; the factor-investing-implementation where systematic exposure harvesting deploys pricing models as strategy blueprints with the crowding and decay awareness of the index companion; the risk-management-application where factor decomposition identifies portfolio exposures and hedge construction per the factor-mapping traditions); the model-limitation literacy (the estimation-error-propagation where beta, premium, and correlation uncertainty compounds through model applications demanding humility ranges per the forecast-uncertainty disciplines; the regime-dependence evidence where factor premia, betas, and correlations shift across market states conditioning all model outputs per the regime-frameworks across this curriculum; the structural-change-awareness where market evolution from passive growth through zero-days ecosystems alters the data-generating processes models assume per the structure-respect disciplines; the model-pluralism practice where important conclusions get tested across model specifications before conviction per the professional-judgment traditions); the behavioral-and-friction extensions (the behavioral-pricing-alternatives where sentiment, limits-to-arbitrage, and bias-driven pricing complement equilibrium models with the anomaly-explanation competition; the friction-integration where taxes, transaction costs, liquidity premia, and constraints modify theoretical prices toward observed reality per the market-structure literatures; the stochastic-discount-factor unification where the pricing-kernel framework subsumes model variety into one mathematical structure for the theoretically inclined per the pricing-engine foundations of the derivatives companion); and the futures-analyst synthesis (the commodity-pricing-connection where storage theory, hedging pressure, and risk-premium models price futures curves through the commodity-asset-pricing literature per the curve-mechanics sections of the companions; the cross-asset-factor-architecture where growth, inflation, carry, value, and momentum factors span asset classes enabling unified portfolio analysis per the factor-mapping of the rates and index companions; the model-driven-trade-construction where pricing-model divergences between theoretical and observed values generate relative-value opportunities per the rich-cheap disciplines; the complete-pricing-literacy where Program models and companion depth combine into professional command of how risk becomes expected return across every instrument this discipline analyzes) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the CAPM-only discounter whose single-beta cost of equity ignores documented factor structure, systematically mispricing risk, remediated by the multi-factor deployment; the beta-precision-illusionist whose regression betas carry false confidence through estimation error and instability, remediated by the estimation-humility discipline; the factor-mechanism-partisan whose value or momentum deployment collapses when the favored explanation fails, remediated by the interpretation-agnosticism balance; the regime-blind modeler whose static parameters survive market-state transitions unadjusted, remediated by the dependence literacy; the frictionless-theorist whose equilibrium prices never meet the tax, cost, and liquidity reality of observed markets, remediated by the extension integration; and the pricing-kernel-ignorant practitioner who applies models without understanding their common mathematical foundation limiting cross-model coherence checks, remediated by the unification literacy with detection methods as the diagnostic.

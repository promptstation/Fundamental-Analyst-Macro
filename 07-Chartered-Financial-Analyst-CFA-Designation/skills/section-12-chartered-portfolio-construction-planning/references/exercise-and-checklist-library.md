# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Construct portfolios at Level III depth through private wealth (case architecture
**Scenario:** provide a realistic the CFA body of knowledge scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** construct portfolios at Level III depth through private wealth (case architecture, tax awareness, concentrated positions, goals-based approaches), institutional landscapes (pension management with LDI, endowment models, insurance portfolios, sovereign funds), implementation craft (manager selection, trading management, rebalancing execution, technology infrastructure), risk integration (enterprise frameworks, tail management, operational coverage, multi-asset aggregation), performance systems (GIPS compliance, client reporting, attribution depth, governance meetings), and examination synthesis (constructed-response mastery, case integration, pathway navigation, ethical synthesis), avoiding template planning, institution confusion, implementation afterthoughts, tail deferral, GIPS ignorance, and response avoidance failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.

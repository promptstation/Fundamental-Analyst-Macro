## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the derivatives topic area whose Program treatment of instrument mechanics, no-arbitrage valuation, and strategy application integrates with the dedicated derivatives companion into complete instrument command: the Program derivatives architecture (the Level I instrument-survey covering forward, futures, option, and swap basics with benefit-cost and purpose analysis establishing foundational literacy; the Level II valuation-depth where no-arbitrage pricing across instrument classes receives quantitative treatment with the binomial and BSM application the curriculum tests computationally; the Level III risk-management deployment where derivative strategies serve portfolio hedging, yield enhancement, and exposure adjustment within institutional mandates; the companion-integration pattern where this Program progression and the dedicated quantitative-finance course reinforce from different angles, the survey-then-depth structure maximizing retention); the forward-and-futures framework (the forward-pricing-and-valuation mechanics where cost-of-carry logic prices forwards and marks value through contract life per the no-arbitrage foundations of the derivatives companion applied at curriculum depth; the futures-versus-forwards distinction where daily settlement, marking, and convexity differences carry analytical consequences the Program examines precisely; the swap-pricing-system where swaps decompose into forward sequences with the fixed-rate derivation and equity-commodity-currency variants receiving full treatment; the hedging-application-craft where short-long hedge construction, basis-risk awareness, and hedge-ratio computation deploy across portfolio contexts); the option-framework mastery (the option-basics-and-strategy coverage where payoff diagrams, moneyness vocabulary, and the strategy families from protective puts through spreads establish the Program foundation per the options sections of the companion; the put-call-parity-and-conversion-system where parity relationships and their extension to forwards and currencies provide the consistency-check machinery the curriculum tests; the BSM-application-literacy where model inputs, Greek computation, and assumption-awareness operate at the Program's required depth with the violation-map understanding from the companion; the option-strategy-analysis where combined-position payoffs, breakevens, and risk profiles receive systematic treatment including covered calls, collars, and spread constructions); the valuation-computation discipline (the binomial-tree-mechanics where one and two-period risk-neutral valuation with backward induction provides the computational core Level II examines; the continuous-time-application where BSM formula deployment with d-term interpretation and Greek extraction demands calculator fluency; the swap-and-forward-computation chain where multi-step valuation sequences combine discounting, carry, and parity logic under time pressure; the cross-instrument-consistency verification where parity and put-call-forward relationships validate computations per the relationship-web discipline of the companion); the derivative-strategy portfolio applications (the risk-management-uses where equity, currency, and rate risk modification through futures, options, and swaps serve Level III portfolio cases with the mandate-constraint awareness; the yield-enhancement-and-income strategies where covered calls and cash-secured puts operate with the risk-return tradeoff honesty the curriculum demands; the leverage-and-exposure-adjustment applications where futures-based equity exposure and synthetic constructions adjust portfolio beta per the beta-management traditions; the structured-product-literacy where embedded-derivative analysis connects retail products to component valuation per the product-ecosystem awareness); the commodity-derivative specifics (the commodity-forward-and-futures pricing where storage costs, convenience yields, and collateral interest modify financial carry logic per the storage-economics integration of the companion; the commodity-option applications where agricultural and energy option markets deploy the general framework with the liquidity and event-pattern specifics per the companion courses' market literacy; the commodity-swap-and-index structures where producer-consumer hedging instruments and index products connect derivative mechanics to the physical markets per the economics companions); and the exam-and-practice integration (the computation-heavy-question strategy where derivatives questions reward procedural fluency and formula-intuition with the time-budget allocation discipline; the vignette-derivative-integration at Level II where derivatives appear embedded in portfolio and company cases requiring recognition before computation; the Level III constructed-response craft where strategy recommendation and justification demand clear derivative-logic communication per the writing traditions; the professional-application bridge where Program derivative knowledge deploys in futures analysis, hedging consultation, and risk communication per the discipline's practice orientation) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the formula-reciter whose BSM and binomial computations proceed without no-arbitrage intuition, failing applied interpretation questions, remediated by the foundation-integration discipline; the parity-blind computer whose multi-step valuations never cross-check through put-call relationships, missing self-detected errors, remediated by the consistency-verification habit; the strategy-diagram-illiterate whose combined-position analysis cannot construct payoff profiles, remediated by the strategy-analysis mastery; the commodity-carry-confuser who prices commodity forwards with financial-asset logic omitting storage and convenience components, remediated by the commodity-specifics coverage; the Level-III-technician who computes derivative values but cannot frame recommendations in portfolio-mandate language, remediated by the constructed-response craft; and the companion-compartmentalizer whose Program derivatives never integrate with the deep companion treatment, remediated by the synthesis pattern with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Master fixed income through market foundations (segment structure, feature literacy, index systems, global context), valuation mechanics (discounting architecture, yield measures, arbitrage-free approaches, matrix pricing), the risk system (duration-convexity variants, spread risk, hedging techniques, multi-factor awareness), credit analysis (risk framework, corporate methodology, structured literacy, cycle integration), rate-structure analysis (spot-forward systems, term-structure theories, strategy toolkit, international comparison), advanced depth (term-structure models, LDI frameworks, portfolio construction), and futures integration (Treasury futures bridge, hedging application, spread monitoring, complete literacy synthesis), avoiding YTM simplification, duration-only hedging, ratio mechanics, structured black boxes, OAS avoidance, and companion disconnection failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the fixed income topic area whose Program treatment of bond analysis, valuation, and rate structure integrates with the dedicated rates companion into complete fixed-income capability: the fixed-income-market foundation (the market-structure coverage where government, agency, corporate, securitized, and non-US segments with their issuance and trading mechanics establish the instrument landscape per the rate-market geography of the rates companion; the bond-feature literacy spanning option-embedded structures, covenants, and tax treatments whose cash-flow consequences shape analysis; the index-and-benchmark systems where bond-index construction and characteristics connect to portfolio benchmarking per the index-methodology disciplines; the global-market context where cross-border issuance, currency denomination, and EM-market development extend the analysis frame per the international sections); the valuation-mechanics core (the cash-flow-discounting foundation where benchmark-rate-plus-spread architecture prices bonds with the spot-curve and no-arbitrage valuation the curriculum develops rigorously; the yield-measure system spanning yield-to-maturity, current yield, and money-market conventions with their limitations and comparability rules per the fixed-income mathematics of the rates companion; the arbitrage-free-valuation approach where binomial interest-rate trees and option-adjusted-spread concepts value embedded-option bonds at the Program's technical depth; the matrix-pricing and illiquid-security techniques where comparable-security logic prices thinly-traded bonds per the Level II applications); the risk-measurement system (the duration-convexity framework where price-yield sensitivity measurement across effective, modified, key-rate, and money-duration variants provides the position-arithmetic foundation per the mathematics section of the rates companion; the yield-spread-risk analysis where spread movement drivers across credit, liquidity, and structure components inform relative-value assessment; the interest-rate-risk-management techniques where futures, forwards, and duration-matching strategies hedge rate exposure connecting curriculum knowledge to the derivatives companion; the multi-factor-risk awareness where level, slope, and curvature exposures require key-rate analysis beyond single-duration summary per the curve-analytics traditions); the credit-analysis discipline (the credit-risk-framework where default probability, loss severity, and expected-loss computation structure credit assessment with the four-C and capacity-collateral-covenant traditions; the corporate-credit-analysis methodology where industry position, financial ratios, cash-flow adequacy, and governance combine into rating-relevant assessment per the FSA and issuers connections; the structured-credit-literacy covering securitization mechanics, tranche waterfall analysis, and agency-risk factors whose 2008 lessons the curriculum encodes; the credit-cycle-integration where spread behavior across cycle phases connects credit analysis to the macro frameworks per the financial-cycle sections); the rate-structure-and-curve analysis (the spot-forward-relationship system where bootstrapping, forward extraction, and realized-return decomposition build curve literacy per the curve-construction craft of the rates companion; the term-structure-theory coverage where expectations, liquidity-premium, and segmentation frameworks explain curve shapes with the empirical-evidence balance; the yield-curve-strategy toolkit where roll-down, bullet-barbell positioning, and curve-reshaping trades deploy curve views per the curve-trading analytics of the companion; the international-yield-comparison where cross-country curve analysis informs global relative-value per the global-curve ecosystem); the Level II-III fixed-income depth (the advanced-valuation coverage where term-structure models, OAS analysis, and mortgage-backed prepayment modeling receive the Program's deepest technical treatment; the liability-driven-investment frameworks where immunization, cash-flow matching, and duration-gap management serve institutional portfolios with the LDI-crisis lessons connecting to the rates companion's stress episodes; the portfolio-construction integration where fixed-income allocation, sector positioning, and security selection combine per the Level III emphasis); and the futures-analyst integration (the Treasury-futures-mastery bridge where deliverable-basket mechanics, conversion factors, and cheapest-to-deliver analysis connect curriculum bond knowledge to the futures instruments per the CTD disciplines of the derivatives companion; the rate-futures-hedging application where bond-portfolio hedging through Treasury and SOFR futures deploys duration arithmetic per the conversion fluency of the mathematics sections; the credit-spread-monitoring integration where spread analysis feeds financial-conditions assessment for equity and commodity transmission per the cross-asset webs; the complete-rate-literacy synthesis where Program fixed income and companion rates courses combine into professional-grade interest-rate analysis capability spanning instruments, valuation, risk, and strategy) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the YTM-comparison-simplifier who ranks bonds by yield-to-maturity ignoring option features, tax status, and reinvestment assumptions, remediated by the yield-measure literacy; the duration-only hedger whose single-number sensitivity ignores curve reshaping risk across key rates, remediated by the multi-factor awareness; the credit-ratio-mechanic whose checklist ratios never integrate into capacity and cycle judgment, remediated by the analysis-methodology discipline; the structured-credit-blackboxer whose securitized analysis trusts ratings without waterfall and incentive examination, remediated by the agency-risk literacy; the OAS-avoider whose embedded-option bonds get valued at static spreads missing the optionality pricing, remediated by the arbitrage-free valuation mastery; and the curriculum-companion disconnector whose Program fixed income never integrates with the deep rates companion systems, remediated by the synthesis discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

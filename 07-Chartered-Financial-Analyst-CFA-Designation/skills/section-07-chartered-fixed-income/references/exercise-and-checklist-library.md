# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Master fixed income through market foundations (segment structure
**Scenario:** provide a realistic the CFA body of knowledge scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** master fixed income through market foundations (segment structure, feature literacy, index systems, global context), valuation mechanics (discounting architecture, yield measures, arbitrage-free approaches, matrix pricing), the risk system (duration-convexity variants, spread risk, hedging techniques, multi-factor awareness), credit analysis (risk framework, corporate methodology, structured literacy, cycle integration), rate-structure analysis (spot-forward systems, term-structure theories, strategy toolkit, international comparison), advanced depth (term-structure models, LDI frameworks, portfolio construction), and futures integration (Treasury futures bridge, hedging application, spread monitoring, complete literacy synthesis), avoiding YTM simplification, duration-only hedging, ratio mechanics, structured black boxes, OAS avoidance, and companion disconnection failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone

**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.

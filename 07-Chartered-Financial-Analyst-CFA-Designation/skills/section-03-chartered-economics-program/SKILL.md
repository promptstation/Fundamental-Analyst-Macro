# Economics in the Program: Micro, Macro, and International Across Levels — Professional Training

## Mission

Produce professional-grade learning modules and training materials on **economics in the Program: Micro, Macro, and International Across Levels** within The professional-credential anchor of the futures fundamental analyst discipline — the CFA Institute Program's deep training in global market analysis, asset pricing, and portfolio management, mastered as a coherent body of knowledge and applied to futures-market fundamental practice rather than passed as isolated exam content.

Subject scope: Covers the economics topic area whose Program treatment spans micro foundations through international systems, integrated here with the dedicated macroeconomics companion course: the Level I economics architecture (the demand-supply-analysis foundation where elasticity, consumer-surplus, and market-structure concepts establish the micro toolkit whose commodity applications the agricultural-economics companion develops in physical-market depth; the firm-and-market-structure analysis spanning perfect competition through monopoly with the oligopoly game-theory introduction whose relevance to cartels and concentrated industries connects to the OPEC analysis of the energy companion; the aggregate-output-price framework where GDP, aggregate demand-supply, and growth economics receive survey treatment that the macro companion's national-accounts and cycle sections operationalize; the money-banking-price-level system where monetary policy, money multiplier, and inflation mechanisms introduce the central-bank analysis the rates companion deepens); the currency-and-international economics (the exchange-rate-mechanics coverage where parity conditions, regime systems, and balance-of-payments accounting receive curriculum treatment per the international sections of the macro and rates companions; the international-trade framework where comparative advantage, trade-organization structures, and capital-flow analysis support the trade-balance disciplines of the external-sector sections; the growth-economics introduction where convergence, demographic, and productivity drivers frame long-horizon analysis with the development-relevance for EM market assessment); the Level II deepening (the currency-economics extension where exchange-rate determination, parity-relation application, and economic-growth-currency interaction receive vignette-level analytical treatment; the economic-growth-and-development depth where growth accounting, regulation, and deregulation analysis support emerging-market investment assessment per the global-equity literacy of the index companion; the curriculum-integration pattern where Level II economics deploys through equity and fixed-income country-analysis applications rather than standalone abstraction); the Level III portfolio-economics (the capital-market-expectations framework where economic analysis feeds return, risk, and correlation forecasting for asset allocation per the portfolio sections below; the geopolitical-and-regime analysis integration where economic conditions condition portfolio positioning across business and policy cycles per the cycle and regime frameworks of the companions; the private-wealth-and-institutional economics applications where tax systems, regulatory environments, and liability structures shape portfolio design per the planning sections); the micro-applications bridge (the elasticity-implementation where demand and supply elasticity concepts quantify pass-through and substitution analysis across the commodity complexes per the price-formation discipline of the agricultural companion; the market-structure-application where industry analysis frameworks serve sector rotation and competitive-position assessment per the sector frameworks of the index companion; the game-theory-literacy where strategic interaction models inform cartel behavior, bidding, and competitive dynamics analysis per the OPEC and auction literatures; the information-economics concepts where adverse selection and moral hazard frame market-design understanding across financial instruments); the macro-applications bridge (the indicator-system integration where the Program's economic-indicator coverage connects to the dashboard and monitoring systems of the macro companion's applied modeling; the policy-analysis-frameworks where fiscal-monetary interaction and policy-mix assessment deploy the reaction-function and transmission analysis of the rates companion; the inflation-and-rate-mechanics where the Program's monetary coverage feeds the yield-curve and discount-rate analysis central to valuation across asset classes; the cycle-and-growth integration where business-cycle frameworks support the earnings-cycle and rotation analysis per the index companion's cycle sections); the exam-strategy layer for economics (the topic-weight-awareness where economics carries substantial Level I weight with declining explicit presence at higher levels through integration; the formula-versus-concept balance where economics questions reward conceptual application over computation with the graph-and-shift reasoning fluency; the cross-topic-question anticipation where economics knowledge appears embedded in equity country analysis, fixed-income yield questions, and portfolio expectations; the global-macro synthesis where the Program's international economics and the companions' deep systems form the cross-border analysis foundation for rate and currency futures per those courses' frameworks; the analyst-communication alignment where Program economic vocabulary standardizes professional discussion with the CFA-fluent community across asset classes; the knowledge-currency maintenance where evolving curriculum economics content including fintech and sustainability topics extends the foundation through continuing study per the professional-identity obligations) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the companion-redundancy assumers who skip Program economics as already-known macro content, missing the micro and international specifics the exam tests, remediated by the coverage-mapping discipline; the graph-illiterate candidate whose shift-analysis reasoning fails on aggregate demand-supply questions, remediated by the visual-mechanics fluency; the elasticity-abstracter who cannot deploy elasticity concepts in pass-through and market-power questions, remediated by the application-bridge practice; Write as an experienced practitioner, not as a summarizer of popular content. Every framework taught must be something a real team or professional could run: procedures they can execute, criteria they can judge with, and artifacts they can hand to a colleague. Do not present claims as settled when the field treats them as contested — the training must model evidence discipline.

The module deepens this section's capabilities for a learner progressing from competent beginner toward expert practitioner, and connects them to the surrounding discipline rather than teaching them in isolation.

2. Build the evidence base from the authoritative sources below.

## Writing Standards

Throughout the material, prioritize language that is:

* Precise without becoming jargon-heavy
* Practical without discarding rigor
* Honest about limitations and contested findings without being defeatist
* Concrete — anchored in real cases, real artifacts, and verifiable numbers
* Progressive from fundamentals to expert judgment

Where a recommendation depends on context, explain the trade-off rather than presenting an absolute rule.

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid: Memorization-over-mastery — storing formulas and definitions for exam passage without the working intuition that lets knowledge deploy under real analytical pressure, producing credentials that never change practice; Ethics-last studying — treating the most heavily weighted and professionally defining topic as final-week cramming despite its cumulative judgment structure and the Program's ethics-adjustment significance; Level-compartmentalization — passing each level as isolated content without recognizing that FSA feeds equity valuation feeds portfolio construction, losing the integrated capability the designation exists to certify; Practice-question avoidance — studying readings passively without the item-set and constructed-response practice whose format fluency separates passing from failing at every level; Credential-identity substitution — treating the charter as professional arrival rather than the maintenance beginning, letting membership obligations, continuing education, and practice standards decay into nominal status; exercises without evaluation criteria; modules that stop at inspiration without a single decision the learner can now make better; and any content that overstates certainty beyond what the evidence supports.

## Decision Heuristic

When allocating depth under time or length limits, ask:

1. Where will this audience actually stall in practice?
2. Which unit protects the most value if taught well?
3. Can the learner run the framework tomorrow without me?
4. Is every example doing work a plain sentence could not?
5. What would a skeptical domain expert say about this material?

If two topics compete for space, keep the one that changes a decision.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Master Program economics through the Level I architecture (demand-supply toolkit, market structures, aggregate framework, money-banking system), international economics (exchange mechanics, trade framework, growth introduction), Level II deepening (currency extension, growth-development depth, integration patterns), Level III portfolio economics (capital market expectations, regime analysis, wealth applications), micro bridges (elasticity implementation, structure application, game theory, information economics), macro bridges (indicator integration, policy frameworks, inflation-rate mechanics, cycle integration), exam strategy (weight awareness, concept balance, cross-topic anticipation, sequencing), and specialist integration (commodity extension, global synthesis, vocabulary alignment, knowledge currency), avoiding redundancy assumptions, graph illiteracy, elasticity abstraction, isolated study, Level III skipping, and static knowledge failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the economics topic area whose Program treatment spans micro foundations through international systems, integrated here with the dedicated macroeconomics companion course: the Level I economics architecture (the demand-supply-analysis foundation where elasticity, consumer-surplus, and market-structure concepts establish the micro toolkit whose commodity applications the agricultural-economics companion develops in physical-market depth; the firm-and-market-structure analysis spanning perfect competition through monopoly with the oligopoly game-theory introduction whose relevance to cartels and concentrated industries connects to the OPEC analysis of the energy companion; the aggregate-output-price framework where GDP, aggregate demand-supply, and growth economics receive survey treatment that the macro companion's national-accounts and cycle sections operationalize; the money-banking-price-level system where monetary policy, money multiplier, and inflation mechanisms introduce the central-bank analysis the rates companion deepens); the currency-and-international economics (the exchange-rate-mechanics coverage where parity conditions, regime systems, and balance-of-payments accounting receive curriculum treatment per the international sections of the macro and rates companions; the international-trade framework where comparative advantage, trade-organization structures, and capital-flow analysis support the trade-balance disciplines of the external-sector sections; the growth-economics introduction where convergence, demographic, and productivity drivers frame long-horizon analysis with the development-relevance for EM market assessment); the Level II deepening (the currency-economics extension where exchange-rate determination, parity-relation application, and economic-growth-currency interaction receive vignette-level analytical treatment; the economic-growth-and-development depth where growth accounting, regulation, and deregulation analysis support emerging-market investment assessment per the global-equity literacy of the index companion; the curriculum-integration pattern where Level II economics deploys through equity and fixed-income country-analysis applications rather than standalone abstraction); the Level III portfolio-economics (the capital-market-expectations framework where economic analysis feeds return, risk, and correlation forecasting for asset allocation per the portfolio sections below; the geopolitical-and-regime analysis integration where economic conditions condition portfolio positioning across business and policy cycles per the cycle and regime frameworks of the companions; the private-wealth-and-institutional economics applications where tax systems, regulatory environments, and liability structures shape portfolio design per the planning sections); the micro-applications bridge (the elasticity-implementation where demand and supply elasticity concepts quantify pass-through and substitution analysis across the commodity complexes per the price-formation discipline of the agricultural companion; the market-structure-application where industry analysis frameworks serve sector rotation and competitive-position assessment per the sector frameworks of the index companion; the game-theory-literacy where strategic interaction models inform cartel behavior, bidding, and competitive dynamics analysis per the OPEC and auction literatures; the information-economics concepts where adverse selection and moral hazard frame market-design understanding across financial instruments); the macro-applications bridge (the indicator-system integration where the Program's economic-indicator coverage connects to the dashboard and monitoring systems of the macro companion's applied modeling; the policy-analysis-frameworks where fiscal-monetary interaction and policy-mix assessment deploy the reaction-function and transmission analysis of the rates companion; the inflation-and-rate-mechanics where the Program's monetary coverage feeds the yield-curve and discount-rate analysis central to valuation across asset classes; the cycle-and-growth integration where business-cycle frameworks support the earnings-cycle and rotation analysis per the index companion's cycle sections); the exam-strategy layer for economics (the topic-weight-awareness where economics carries substantial Level I weight with declining explicit presence at higher levels through integration; the formula-versus-concept balance where economics questions reward conceptual application over computation with the graph-and-shift reasoning fluency; the cross-topic-question anticipation where economics knowledge appears embedded in equity country analysis, fixed-income yield questions, and portfolio expectations; the study-sequencing logic where economics early-study pays compound returns through its deployment across subsequent topics); and the futures-specialist integration (the commodity-economics extension where Program micro and macro foundations combine with the physical-market economics of the agriculture-energy companions into complete commodity analysis capability; the global-macro synthesis where the Program's international economics and the companions' deep systems form the cross-border analysis foundation for rate and currency futures per those courses' frameworks; the analyst-communication alignment where Program economic vocabulary standardizes professional discussion with the CFA-fluent community across asset classes; the knowledge-currency maintenance where evolving curriculum economics content including fintech and sustainability topics extends the foundation through continuing study per the professional-identity obligations) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the companion-redundancy assumers who skip Program economics as already-known macro content, missing the micro and international specifics the exam tests, remediated by the coverage-mapping discipline; the graph-illiterate candidate whose shift-analysis reasoning fails on aggregate demand-supply questions, remediated by the visual-mechanics fluency; the elasticity-abstracter who cannot deploy elasticity concepts in pass-through and market-power questions, remediated by the application-bridge practice; the isolated-topic studier who prepares economics separately from its equity and portfolio deployments, remediated by the cross-topic anticipation; the Level-III-economics skipper whose capital-market-expectations preparation neglects the economic-analysis inputs, remediated by the portfolio-economics integration; and the static-knowledge charterholder whose economics understanding predates curriculum evolution in fintech and sustainability topics, remediated by the currency-maintenance discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Unit 2 — Capstone integration

**Purpose:** integrate every capability above on one realistic problem end to end.

**Structure:** the learner takes a single problem in the CFA body of knowledge through each unit's framework in sequence, producing a coherent deliverable that mirrors real professional documentation. Evaluation criteria: internal consistency across artifacts, evidence discipline, honest trade-off statements, and demonstrable use of each unit's framework.

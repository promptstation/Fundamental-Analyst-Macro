# Domain Content Map — Performance Evaluation and Attribution: Measuring Investment Skill

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Measure investment performance through return foundations (TWR-MWR standards, tax and fee distinctions, convention literacy), risk-adjusted systems (Sharpe traditions, Treynor-IR family, Jensen deployment, downside measures), attribution mastery (Brinson architecture with linking, fixed-income systems, factor approaches, data discipline), presentation ethics (GIPS architecture, bias elimination, marketing integrity, client communication), manager evaluation (quantitative screening, qualitative frameworks, persistence evidence, monitoring systems), personal feedback (decision journals, process-outcome separation, skill tracking, record honesty), advanced topics (appraisal statistics.
2. Model verification, private adjustments, behavioral analysis), and futures application (strategy attribution, benchmark judgment, calibration integration, stakeholder reporting), avoiding raw comparison, attribution approximation, presentation casuality, persistence belief, outcome grading, and small-sample claims failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the performance measurement and attribution systems that close the feedback loop on portfolio decisions and connect to the calibration disciplines across this curriculum family: the return-measurement foundation (the time-weighted-return standard whose subperiod compounding removes cash-flow distortion for manager evaluation with the geometric-linkage mechanics the curriculum computes precisely; the money-weighted-return alternative where internal-rate-of-money logic captures investor timing experience appropriate for client-outcome assessment; the pre-tax-after-tax and gross-net distinctions whose selection matches evaluation purpose with the fee-impact decomposition revealing cost consumption of returns; the return-convention literacy spanning arithmetic and geometric differences, annualization methods, and currency-effect inclusion whose consistency governs comparability); the risk-adjusted-measurement system (the Sharpe-ratio tradition measuring total-risk-adjusted excess return with the estimation-error and non-normality caveats the curriculum examines; the Treynor-and-information-ratio family separating systematic-risk adjustment and active-management efficiency with the benchmark-dependence awareness; the Jensen's-alpha deployment where CAPM-based skill measurement connects pricing models to evaluation per the asset-pricing section; the downside-and-distribution-aware measures including Sortino ratios, maximum-drawdown analysis, and VaR-integrated assessment whose relevance grows with return non-normality per the risk-measurement traditions); the attribution-framework mastery (the Brinson-attribution architecture decomposing return differences into allocation, selection, and interaction effects across the hierarchy with the multi-period linking methods that make sequential attribution coherent; the fixed-income-attribution systems where curve-positioning, spread, carry, and duration contributions decompose bond performance per the rate-strategy frameworks; the factor-attribution approaches where multi-factor models explain returns through systematic exposures with the residual-alpha interpretation per the pricing-model applications; the attribution-data-discipline where holdings-based versus returns-based methods trade precision against availability with the style-analysis traditions connecting to the factor frameworks); the performance-presentation ethics (the GIPS-standards architecture where firm-level compliance, composite construction, and disclosure requirements govern honest presentation with the verification tradition; the survivorship-and-selection-bias elimination where composite inclusion rules and track-record portability standards prevent misleading history per the data-hygiene disciplines; the marketing-material-review where performance advertising meets Standards obligations on fairness, substantiation, and cherry-picking prohibition per the ethics foundations; the client-communication-integrity where expectation setting, benchmark selection transparency, and fee disclosure serve informed consent per the fiduciary frameworks); the manager-evaluation practice (the quantitative-screening systems where return, risk, consistency, and capacity metrics filter manager universes with the data-vendor and database-bias awareness; the qualitative-assessment-framework where investment philosophy, process coherence, personnel stability, and operational quality complete evaluation per the due-diligence traditions; the performance-persistence evidence where limited persistence across horizons and styles tempers selection confidence with the skill-luck separation literature; the ongoing-monitoring-systems where style-drift detection, capacity assessment, and process-fidelity review maintain delegated portfolios per the implementation sections); the personal-performance-feedback loop (the decision-journal-integration where individual analyst and trader records connect return attribution to decision-process quality per the calibration traditions across this curriculum family; the outcome-versus-process separation where good decisions with bad outcomes and reverse combinations get distinguished preventing result-oriented learning corruption per the decision-quality disciplines; the skill-development-tracking where attribution patterns reveal genuine capability areas and systematic weaknesses guiding deliberate practice per the professional-development traditions; the career-record-honesty where personal track records face the same bias-elimination and presentation standards applied to institutional evaluation per the ethics integration); the advanced-measurement topics (the performance-appraisal-statistics where significance testing of alpha, information-ratio confidence, and sample-size requirements address the skill-detection problem with the small-sample humility; the attribution-of-attribution challenge where factor-model selection itself drives measured skill, requiring multi-model verification per the model-pluralism disciplines; the private-market-performance-adjustments where illiquidity smoothing, vintage normalization, and self-reporting bias correction modify alternatives evaluation per the alternatives-section challenges; the behavioral-performance-analysis where manager bias patterns, risk-shifting after drawdowns, and herding tendencies appear in return data per the behavioral integrations); and the futures-analyst application (the strategy-attribution-deployment where macro and commodity strategy performance decomposes through timing, selection, and exposure attribution per the strategy-evaluation traditions of the companion courses; the benchmark-construction-judgment where futures strategies face appropriate comparison selection including carry-adjusted and volatility-scaled references per the benchmark disciplines; the calibration-record-integration where attribution results feed the forecast and decision scoring systems that drive analyst improvement per the professional-practice traditions; the client-and-stakeholder reporting where performance explanation serves investors, management, and committees through the communication crafts of the executive companion) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the raw-return comparer whose manager and strategy rankings ignore risk, cost, and benchmark differences producing meaningless league tables, remediated by the risk-adjusted system; the attribution-approximator whose allocation-selection decomposition rests on inconsistent holdings and benchmark data, remediated by the data-discipline standards; the GIPS-casual presenter whose track records embed survivorship and cherry-picking bias risking Standards violation, remediated by the presentation-ethics mastery; the persistence-believer whose manager selection assumes demonstrated skill continues against weak-persistence evidence, remediated by the skill-luck literature; the outcome-graded decision-maker whose personal feedback loop confuses lucky results with good process, corrupting development, remediated by the process-outcome separation; and the small-sample-significance claimer whose alpha assertions rest on statistically meaningless histories, remediated by the appraisal-statistics discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

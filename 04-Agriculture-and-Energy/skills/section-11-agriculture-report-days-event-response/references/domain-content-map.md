## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the operational discipline of scheduled data events where weeks of analysis meet seconds of market response: the event-landscape mapping (the tier-one-calendar construction where WASDE, EIA weekly, OPEC meetings, Grain Stocks, Prospective Plantings, and major macro releases define the year's high-volatility skeleton per the calendar-integration discipline; the tiering-by-regime adjustment where event weight shifts with market context as tight-balance eras elevate supply reports and recession fears elevate demand data; the global-time-zone management where Asian, European, and American publications create around-the-clock event coverage requirements; the event-cluster awareness where stacked releases within days compound interpretation demands and position risk); the pre-event preparation system (the scenario-branch construction where expected report outcomes get pre-analyzed against balances and positions, converting release seconds into execution of prior thinking rather than improvisation; the consensus-baseline establishment collecting survey medians, whisper ranges, and market-implied moves per the pre-report craft across preceding sections; the sensitivity-estimation work quantifying expected price response per unit of surprise from historical analogs and current regime conditions; the position-risk-review where event exposure gets assessed against conviction, sizing, and adjustment plans with the deliberate decision to hold, reduce, or hedge into the release); the release-second execution (the data-ingestion automation where API feeds, report parsers, and alert systems deliver key numbers faster than manual reading, connecting to the programming companion's tooling; the checklist-reading discipline where trained extraction sequences cover headline, components, and revisions in priority order under time pressure; the first-reaction observation where initial algorithmic and human responses often overshoot, creating the considered-entry opportunities for prepared analysts; the incoherence-detection where internally inconsistent report numbers signal data problems warranting caution before full interpretation per the coherence disciplines); the post-event analysis craft (the surprise-decomposition documentation attributing observed price moves to specific deviations from expectation, building the sensitivity database across events; the assimilation-window monitoring where true implications emerge over hours and days as deferred contracts, spreads, and related complexes adjust; the fade-versus-follow assessment distinguishing overshoot reversion from information-driven continuation through volume, positioning, and fundamental coherence evidence; the position-adjustment execution where pre-committed plans meet actual outcomes with the discipline to follow analysis rather than hope); the event-history systems (the reaction-database maintenance where every major report's surprise, initial move, and assimilation path gets logged enabling statistical sensitivity estimation per the event-study methods of the macro companion; the regime-conditioning analysis where reaction functions shift with balance states, volatility regimes, and positioning crowding, demanding conditional rather than average sensitivities; the analog-event library where current situations match historical episodes by stocks position, trend context, and crowd structure; the calibration-review practice where predicted versus realized reactions feed estimation improvement per the professional-judgment traditions); the unscheduled-event response (the breaking-news protocols where geopolitical attacks, weather disasters, and infrastructure failures trigger the scenario-branch discipline at zero preparation time; the information-triage sequence establishing what happened, what is affected, what is confirmed, before position decisions per the source-hierarchy standards; the volatility-regime management where unscheduled events shift markets into wide-spread, thin-depth conditions requiring execution adaptation; the follow-up-systematization where crisis response gets documented and converted into prepared scenarios for recurrence); and the event-integration wisdom (the expected-value framing where event positions combine surprise probability, expected reaction, and execution costs into rational hold-hedge-exit decisions per the derivatives companion's event economics; the edge-honesty assessment recognizing when event outcomes are genuinely unpredictable coin flips where sizing and hedging beat prediction; the cumulative-event-strategy design where calendar structure enables position building through event sequences rather than single-release bets; the psychological-discipline maintenance where event-day adrenaline, regret from past fades and follows, and result-outcome confusion get managed through process adherence per the decision-quality traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the unprepared gambler who enters release seconds without scenarios or consensus baselines, remediated by the preparation system; the automation-absent reader whose manual report parsing misses the reaction window entirely, remediated by the ingestion tooling; the first-tick follower whose entries chase overshooting initial moves against assimilation evidence, remediated by the reaction-observation discipline; the decomposition-avoider who never documents why events moved as they did, leaving sensitivity estimation forever anecdotal, remediated by the history systems; the unscheduled-event freezer whose breaking-news positions default to inaction until losses force response, remediated by the triage protocols; and the result-oriented reviewer who grades event decisions by outcome rather than process quality, corrupting learning, remediated by the calibration and decision-quality discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

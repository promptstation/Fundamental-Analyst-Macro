## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the inventory-tracking systems across complexes whose buffer levels determine price sensitivity, curve structure, and crisis vulnerability: the inventory-concept framework (the buffer-function principle where stocks absorb supply-demand shocks with the size-relative-to-flow measure determining how much price movement a given imbalance requires, the stocks-to-use logic extended across complexes; the visible-invisible spectrum where exchange-registered, reported, and tracked inventories understate total holdings including farm storage, commercial tanks, and strategic reserves; the inventory-quality dimension where aged grain, sour crude in suboptimal storage, and degraded products differ from fresh supply in usable value; the location-matters principle where stocks concentrated away from demand centers or delivery points carry different price implications than distributed holdings per the logistics discipline); the agricultural inventory systems (the Grain Stocks quarterly report whose on-farm and off-farm split reveals marketing behavior and tests consumption residuals per the USDA section; the export-elevator and interior-storage tracking through basis behavior, throughput data, and commercial estimates whose tightness signals local scarcity ahead of national data; the global-stock estimation challenges where Chinese state reserves, Indian buffer stocks, and former Soviet holdings carry transparency ranges from opaque to invisible, requiring triangulation through import behavior and satellite observation; the carryover-quality tracking where multi-year-old corn and wheat stocks affect usability and price discounts); the energy inventory systems (the crude-storage ecology spanning Cushing hub tanks whose aerial and satellite-verified capacity utilization moves WTI spreads, Gulf Coast commercial tanks, and global onshore capacity per the weekly-data section; the floating-storage dimension where laden tankers in transit and idle positions form a mobile buffer whose economics follow the curve per the derivatives companion's carry analysis; the product-inventory tracking across gasoline, distillate, and jet with the seasonal-band interpretation discipline; the strategic-reserve accounting where SPR levels, release schedules, and refill programs enter US balances with policy-driven timing; the global-stock triangulation combining OECD reported data, Chinese inventory estimates, satellite tank-shadow measurement, and flow-based inference per the remote-sensing methods); the metals inventory dimension (the exchange-warrant systems where LME and COMEX registered stocks provide visible buffer data with the off-warrant and shadow-inventory problem per the metals economics companion; the warehouse-queue dynamics whose cancellation ratios signal physical demand pressure; the country-location shifts where stock movements between hubs reveal regional tightness and financing-deal activity); the storage-economics integration (the carry-versus-scarcity curve reading where inventory levels and storage capacity utilization express through term structure per the theory-of-storage framework; the storage-capacity-constraint awareness where full tanks and saturated elevator space force distressed selling regardless of price signals, the physical limit behind collapse episodes; the inventory-financing dynamics where contango carry trades absorb surplus into visible stocks with the funding-cost sensitivity that unwinds them; the restocking-cycle analysis where low inventories across complexes trigger precautionary buying waves that amplify price recovery); the estimation-and-verification craft (the stock-estimation methods spanning official surveys, satellite measurement of tank shadows and grain piles, flow-based mass-balance inference, and commercial intelligence gathering with each method's error profile documented; the triangulation discipline where multiple independent estimates bound true values with disagreement itself signaling information gaps; the revision-behavior tracking where official stock restatements reveal prior estimation error patterns useful for real-time correction; the surprise-event inventory audits where supply disruptions trigger rapid reassessment of buffer adequacy across affected complexes); and the inventory-market-linkage analysis (the stocks-price-sensitivity mapping where identical supply shocks produce different price responses depending on buffer position, the conditional-volatility insight for scenario work; the stocks-curve-coherence monitoring where inventory data and term structure must tell consistent stories or one contains error or information per the coherence-audit discipline; the inventory-cycle-phase reading where destocking and restocking transitions mark turning points across business and commodity cycles connecting to the macro companion's cycle framework; the crisis-vulnerability assessment where thin buffers across multiple complexes simultaneously define systemic risk states warranting position and portfolio adjustment) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the visible-only inventory analyst whose buffer assessments miss farm, strategic, and shadow stocks dominating some complexes, remediated by the visible-invisible spectrum discipline; the level-without-flow reader who quotes absolute stock numbers without days-of-supply or stocks-to-use normalization, remediated by the buffer-function measures; the single-source stock believer whose Chinese or opaque-reserve estimates never face triangulation, remediated by the multi-method verification craft; the curve-inventory disconnector whose storage views never reconcile against observable term structure, remediated by the coherence monitoring; the capacity-blind crisis modeler whose shortage scenarios ignore that full storage limits price transmission and empty storage amplifies it, remediated by the constraint-awareness discipline; and the revision-ignorant estimator who never updates priors from official restatements, repeating the same errors, remediated by the revision-behavior tracking with detection methods as the diagnostic.

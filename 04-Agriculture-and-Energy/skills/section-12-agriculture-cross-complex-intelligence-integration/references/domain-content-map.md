## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the integration discipline connecting agricultural, energy, metals, and macro information flows into coherent whole-market assessment: the linkage-map architecture (the feed-energy-food triangle where crude prices transmit through ethanol and biodiesel mandates into crop demand, fertilizer and freight costs into production economics, and protein demand into grain consumption, the central cross-complex engine; the dollar-and-rates overlay where currency strength and financing conditions price across every commodity simultaneously per the macro companion's transmission channels; the growth-industrial linkage where manufacturing activity connects base metals, energy demand, and freight rates into a common cycle signal; the substitution-and-competition web where wheat-corn feed switching, gas-coal power competition, and protein substitution create cross-price constraints bounding relative moves); the transmission-channel analysis (the biofuel-channel quantification where blending policy and margin economics determine how much crude movement reaches crop balances, with the threshold and mandate dynamics that make transmission nonlinear; the cost-channel tracking where energy prices enter fertilizer, drying, pumping, freight, and processing costs across agricultural production with the lag and pass-through patterns per the economics companion; the demand-channel monitoring where income and employment effects of energy prices shift food and industrial commodity consumption; the financial-channel observation where commodity-asset correlations, index flows, and macro positioning transmit shocks across complexes through portfolio mechanics per the financialization literacy); the coherence-audit practice (the cross-complex consistency checking where analyst views on corn, crude, fertilizer, freight, and dollar must satisfy the linkage arithmetic or explain divergence as the potential edge; the relative-value signal detection where linkage-implied relationships dislocate creating spread opportunities or revealing information in one complex the others have not priced; the lead-lag pattern monitoring where energy moves historically lead agricultural cost adjustments and macro shifts lead industrial commodities, with regime-dependent reliability; the contradiction-resolution discipline where incoherent views force reexamination of the weakest assumption rather than comfortable compartmentalization); the integrated-balance construction (the multi-complex scenario architecture where single drivers including weather droughts, conflict escalations, and policy shifts propagate through connected balances simultaneously per the scenario disciplines; the portfolio-impact translation where complex-level views aggregate into exposure maps across growth, inflation, weather, and policy factors per the risk companion's decomposition; the stress-linkage analysis where crisis scenarios test cross-complex correlation breakdowns and liquidity evaporation together per the stress-testing discipline; the horizon-integration where daily event intelligence, seasonal calendar patterns, and multi-year cycle positions combine into layered views with explicit timeframe attribution); the monitoring-system design (the dashboard architecture organizing cross-complex indicators by linkage theme rather than single-market convenience, applying the executive-communication companion's dashboard design to intelligence operations; the alert-system layering where threshold crossings in any complex trigger linkage-relevance review across connected markets; the daily-integration-ritual where morning review explicitly asks what overnight moves in one complex imply for others held or watched; the weekly-synthesis discipline producing the written whole-board assessment whose regular practice compounds cross-market pattern recognition); and the integrated-analysis craft (the top-down-bottom-up reconciliation where macro regime views and complex-specific balances inform each other with divergence investigation per the EIC framework companion's methodology; the crowded-trade detection across complexes where positioning concentration in linked markets amplifies unwinding risk beyond single-market assessment; the opportunity-ranking discipline where cross-complex divergences compete for analytical attention by expected value and confidence; the communication-integration where whole-board views reach decision audiences through the structured formats of the executive companion rather than complex-by-complex data dumps) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the siloed-complex analyst whose corn view ignores the crude and dollar context that agricultural balances embed, remediated by the linkage-map discipline; the transmission-assumer who applies historical pass-through elasticities without mandate, margin, and regime conditioning, remediated by the quantification practice; the coherence-neglecter whose simultaneous views on connected markets contradict without examination, remediated by the audit practice; the single-complex scenario builder whose drought or conflict analysis omits energy, freight, and financial propagation, remediated by the multi-complex architecture; the dashboard-dump communicator whose cross-market information reaches audiences without synthesis or priority, remediated by the communication-integration craft; and the lead-lag-mechanical trader who arbitrages historical timing patterns through structural breaks in transmission, remediated by the regime-conditioning monitoring with detection methods as the diagnostic.

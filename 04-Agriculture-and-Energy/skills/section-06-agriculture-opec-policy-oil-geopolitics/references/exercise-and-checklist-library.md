# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Track the state-actor dimension through OPEC+ architecture (organization evolution
**Scenario:** provide a realistic agriculture and energy market intelligence scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** track the state-actor dimension through OPEC+ architecture (organization evolution, membership structure, spare-capacity concentration, decision mechanics), quota systems (target architecture, compliance triangulation, cheat dynamics, quota-export distinction), fiscal motivation (breakeven mapping, share-versus-price oscillation, reserve horizons, intra-cartel tension), announcement intelligence (meeting calendars, leak signaling, parse craft, implementation lags), the geopolitics overlay (conflict mapping, sanctions tracking, state relations, disruption history), demand-side geopolitics (strategic reserves, IEA coordination, security policy, price interventions), and analysis craft (reaction-function estimation, unity assessment, share-war judgment, scenario integration), avoiding headline calculation, unity assumption, leak literalism, disruption panic, sanctions blindness, and static reaction functions failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

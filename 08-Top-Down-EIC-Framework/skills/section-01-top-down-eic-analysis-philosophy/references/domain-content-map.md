## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the methodological foundation whose three-level cascade this course builds into a working signal-production system: the top-down concept (the level-cascade logic where Economic conditions frame Industry behavior which shapes Company and Asset outcomes, each level constraining and informing the next with explicit transmission assumptions between them; the top-down-versus-bottom-up comparison where aggregate-first analysis trades detail richness for coherence and scalability, with the reconciliation practice where the two directions must eventually agree or reveal information; the filter-metaphor operationalization where global events enter a wide aperture at the Economic level and pass through progressively specific screens until actionable signals emerge with defined instruments and risk, the course's central machine; the systematic-versus-discretionary balance where the framework provides structure and documentation while judgment operates within each level, avoiding both checklist automatism and unstructured intuition); the EIC architecture definition (the Economic level scope covering growth regimes, inflation states, policy postures, liquidity conditions, and cross-border dynamics whose assessment sets the environmental priors for everything downstream per the macro and rates companions; the Industry level scope spanning sector structure, cycle position, supply-chain state, and competitive dynamics whose analysis translates economic conditions into specific market-complex implications per the economics and index companions; the Company-Asset level scope where instrument selection, contract analysis, balance assessment, and positioning evaluation convert industry views into tradeable expressions per the derivatives and complex-specific companions; the level-interface discipline where each transition carries explicit transmission assumptions that get documented, tested, and invalidated rather than assumed silently); the transmission-assumption system (the economic-to-industry mappings where rate, growth, inflation, and currency states connect to sector performance through documented sensitivities with regime conditioning per the rotation and transmission frameworks of the companions; the industry-to-asset mappings where sector and complex conditions translate into specific contract behavior through supply-demand balances, curve structure, and positioning per the complex courses; the mapping-maintenance discipline where transmission elasticities and relationships get re-estimated as regimes evolve, the fossilization-prevention system; the breakdown-protocol where transmission failures trigger investigation distinguishing temporary noise, structural change, and analytical error per the coherence-audit traditions); the event-filtering concept (the event-universe definition spanning scheduled data releases, policy decisions, geopolitical developments, weather and supply disruptions, and market-structure events whose exhaustive intake precedes selective processing per the intelligence-foundations discipline; the materiality-screen design where events face rapid Economic-Industry-Asset relevance assessment determining processing depth with the attention-economics awareness that most events warrant no action; the signal-emergence criteria where filtered analysis produces candidate signals only when divergence from market-implied expectations exists, the edge-definition discipline from the intelligence companion applied systematically; the reject-and-defer categories where events get explicitly classified as immaterial, already-priced, or awaiting-development with documentation preventing silent dropping of potential signals); the framework-governance foundation (the process-documentation standard where the complete cascade from event intake through signal output operates from written procedures enabling consistency, review, and improvement per the operating-system traditions; the decision-rights clarity defining who assesses, who challenges, and who commits across the framework stages with the single-analyst adaptation for independent practitioners; the quality-gates architecture where each level transition carries verification checkpoints preventing premature signal promotion per the validation-gate disciplines across this curriculum family; the framework-evolution management where structural changes to the system itself receive deliberate review rather than drift, connecting to the maintenance disciplines of the final sections); and the analyst-posture formation (the hypothesis-driven analysis where levels test explicit propositions rather than accumulating impressions, the scientific-method adaptation per the falsification disciplines of the macro companion; the probabilistic-thinking requirement where every level assessment carries confidence and scenario weights rather than binary conclusions per the calibration traditions; the speed-depth-tradeoff management where event urgency and signal horizon determine analysis depth allocation per the intelligence-workflow disciplines; the humility-systems where framework outputs face the model-limitation awareness that structured error remains error, documented across every companion course's failure libraries) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the framework-fetishist whose elaborate level analysis never reaches signal production because the filter's output stage was never designed, remediated by the event-to-signal completeness discipline; the level-skipping improviser whose macro headline becomes a trade idea without industry and asset verification, remediated by the interface discipline and quality gates; the bottom-up-denier who refuses company and contract-level evidence that contradicts aggregate priors, remediated by the reconciliation practice; the silent-transmission assumer whose level connections rest on undocumented assumptions nobody tests, remediated by the mapping-maintenance system; the binary-conclusion producer whose level assessments lack probability and scenario expression, remediated by the probabilistic-thinking requirement; and the process-drift operator whose framework mutates invisibly until reviews cannot compare periods, remediated by the evolution-management discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Assess the financial environment through policy analysis (monetary positioning, fiscal evaluation, mix classification, divergence mapping), rate structure (curve states, real-rate architecture, term premia, funding ecosystems), liquidity assessment (central-bank tracking, market-liquidity evaluation, credit conditions, regime classification), conditions synthesis (composite construction, policy interaction, global integration, growth feedback), event integration (calendar management, scenario construction, communication parsing, surprise protocols), and transmission output (rate-liquidity-currency mappings with documentation standards), avoiding headline classification, level-only reading, calm assumption, index opacity, event unpreparedness, and undocumented transmission failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the second dimension of the Economic filter where policy postures, rate structures, and liquidity conditions translate regime states into the financial environment assets inhabit: the policy-stance assessment (the monetary-policy-positioning where each major central bank's stance gets classified through rate levels versus neutral estimates, balance-sheet trajectory, and communication tone per the reaction-function frameworks of the rates companion; the fiscal-stance-evaluation where impulse measures, issuance patterns, and political calendars locate fiscal policy per the government-channel systems of the macro companion; the policy-mix-classification where monetary-fiscal combinations produce distinct market environments with the documented asset-behavior patterns per the policy-mix analysis traditions; the policy-divergence-mapping where cross-country stance differences define the carry and flow landscape per the divergence frameworks of the rates companion); the rate-structure analysis (the global-curve-state assessment where level, slope, and curvature across major curves locate the rate environment per the curve-analytics discipline; the real-rate-architecture where real yields across tenors define the discount-rate foundation for all asset valuation per the valuation-engine frameworks; the term-premium-state reading where premium estimates and their drivers inform curve sustainability assessment per the decomposition traditions; the funding-rate-ecosystem where money-market spreads, repo rates, and cross-currency basis reveal financial-system stress per the dollar-system monitoring of the rates companion); the liquidity-environment assessment (the central-bank-liquidity tracking where balance-sheet aggregates, facility usage, and reserve levels measure policy liquidity supply per the Fed-operations frameworks; the market-liquidity-state evaluation where spreads, depth, and resilience indicators across asset classes locate trading conditions per the systemic-liquidity disciplines of the index companion; the credit-conditions-monitoring where spreads, lending standards, and issuance volumes gauge private financial conditions per the financial-cycle frameworks; the liquidity-regime-classification where abundant-normal-stressed states condition transmission reliability and position-management requirements per the stress-regime literatures); the financial-conditions synthesis (the composite-index construction where rate, credit, currency, and equity components aggregate into financial-conditions assessment with the weighting and normalization disciplines per the indicator-construction traditions; the conditions-versus-policy interaction where markets tighten or ease financial conditions independently of central-bank action, partially self-implementing policy per the transmission awareness of the Fed section; the global-conditions-integration where dollar funding and cross-border flows synchronize financial environments across markets per the global-dollar frameworks; the conditions-growth-feedback mapping where financial states predict economic trajectory with documented lags, closing the loop to the regime assessment of the prior section); the event-and-calendar integration (the policy-calendar-management where central-bank meetings, fiscal deadlines, and political events structure the Economic level's forward view per the event frameworks of the intelligence companion; the policy-scenario-construction where alternative decision paths receive pre-analyzed market implications feeding downstream levels per the scenario disciplines; the communication-parsing systems where statements, minutes, and speeches update policy assessments through structured analysis per the text-parsing traditions of the rates companion; the surprise-response-protocols where unexpected policy actions trigger immediate cascade re-execution with pre-positioned branches per the event-response disciplines); the economic-to-industry transmission output (the rate-sensitivity-transmission where discount-rate states map to duration-sensitive sectors, valuation multiples, and carry economics across complexes per the sector and curve frameworks; the liquidity-transmission where financial conditions map to risk-appetite behaviors, flow patterns, and volatility regimes affecting all asset classes per the flow and volatility systems; the currency-transmission where policy divergence and dollar states map to commodity pricing, export competitiveness, and cross-market flows per the transmission webs; the transmission-documentation standard where each mapping carries its elasticity estimates, regime conditions, and historical verification per the mapping-maintenance discipline) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the policy-headline classifier whose stance assessment rests on latest decisions without reaction-function and mix context, remediated by the positioning frameworks; the curve-level-only reader whose rate analysis misses slope, premium, and funding dimensions, remediated by the structure-analysis completeness; the liquidity-calm-assumer whose environment assessment extrapolates current conditions into stress scenarios, remediated by the regime-classification discipline; the conditions-index-opaque user whose composite financial-conditions readings never decompose into driving components, remediated by the construction transparency; the policy-event-unprepared cascade whose framework meets central-bank surprises without scenario branches, remediated by the calendar and scenario integration; and the transmission-undocumented mapper whose Economic conclusions reach Industry level through implicit assumptions nobody can test, remediated by the documentation standard with detection methods as the diagnostic.

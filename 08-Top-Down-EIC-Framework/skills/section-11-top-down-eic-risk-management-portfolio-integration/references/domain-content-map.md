## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the risk layer where individually constructed signals aggregate into portfolios whose survival and performance depend on systematic risk management: the portfolio-aggregation framework (the exposure-consolidation where all active signals map into unified factor, complex, currency, duration, and volatility exposures per the decomposition traditions of the risk companions; the net-versus-gross analysis where offsetting positions reduce net exposure while gross levels determine margin, cost, and liquidity demands with the hidden-leverage awareness; the concentration-mapping identifying where exposures cluster by event, complex, counterparty, and liquidity class per the concentration-analysis disciplines; the aggregation-freshness discipline where exposure calculations reflect current market values and correlations rather than entry-time assumptions per the mark-to-market traditions); the limit-and-budget system (the risk-budget architecture where total portfolio risk allocates across strategies, complexes, and horizons with utilization monitoring per the budgeting frameworks of the risk companion; the limit-hierarchy design spanning position, sector, factor, drawdown, and liquidity limits whose breach protocols get pre-committed per the limit-systems traditions; the stress-limit-calibration where limits reference stressed rather than normal volatility and correlation states preventing calm-market overcommitment per the regime-conditioning disciplines; the governance-and-override discipline where limit changes and breaches follow documented authority rather than in-moment negotiation per the escalation protocols); the volatility-and-scenario management (the portfolio-volatility-targeting where aggregate exposure scales with volatility regime maintaining consistent risk-through-time per the volatility-management traditions; the scenario-portfolio-testing where the complete portfolio faces historical replays and hypothetical stresses including funding and liquidity dimensions per the stress-testing disciplines of the risk companion; the tail-risk-budgeting where crash exposure across signals gets explicit quantification and hedging decisions per the tail-management frameworks; the correlation-regime-monitoring where stress-convergence risk gets tracked through regime indicators triggering pre-emptive de-risking per the correlation-disciplines); the liquidity-risk management (the portfolio-liquidity-profiling where positions distribute across liquidity tiers with stressed-exit timeline analysis per the capacity-honesty disciplines; the funding-and-margin-management where margin requirements, financing costs, and call-risk buffers get actively managed across the portfolio per the margin-cycle lessons of the derivatives companion; the exit-sequencing-plans where portfolio reduction under stress follows predetermined order addressing the most vulnerable exposures first per the contingency-planning traditions; the liquidity-regime-adaptation where portfolio construction adjusts position sizes and instrument choices to prevailing liquidity states per the state-dependence disciplines); the drawdown-and-crisis protocols (the drawdown-response-ladder where portfolio loss levels trigger graduated de-risking sequences pre-committed before stress per the drawdown-management traditions; the crisis-mode-activation where systemic events shift portfolio management into capital-preservation posture with defined triggers and actions per the crisis-playbook disciplines; the recovery-and-rebuild-protocols where post-stress re-engagement follows structured assessment rather than revenge trading per the normalization frameworks; the psychological-support-systems where decision quality under drawdown gets protected through process adherence, review rituals, and pre-commitment devices per the decision-quality traditions); the performance-risk integration (the risk-adjusted-performance-monitoring where returns get continuously assessed against risk taken with the attribution integration per the evaluation disciplines of the CFA companion; the risk-efficiency-analysis where exposure utilization against return contribution identifies wasted risk capacity and overcommitted areas per the attribution traditions; the strategy-lifecycle-risk where signal-source decay, crowding development, and regime change get monitored as portfolio-level risks per the alpha-lifecycle disciplines; the capital-allocation-evolution where risk-budget distribution adapts to demonstrated strategy performance with the overfitting-caution per the allocation traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the signal-collection portfolio whose individually reasonable positions aggregate into unmeasured concentrated exposure, remediated by the consolidation framework; the calm-market-limited system whose budgets and limits reference normal volatility until stress reveals their inadequacy, remediated by the stress calibration; the volatility-blind sizer whose constant position counts produce wildly varying risk-through-time, remediated by the targeting discipline; the margin-cascade victim whose portfolio meets correlated margin calls without buffer planning, remediated by the funding management; the drawdown-improviser whose loss response escalates emotionally rather than following pre-committed ladders, remediated by the protocol systems; and the risk-return-disconnector whose risk management operates without performance feedback integration, remediated by the monitoring synthesis with detection methods as the diagnostic.

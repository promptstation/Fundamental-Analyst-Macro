## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Run complex-specific industry analysis through taxonomy foundations (agricultural-energy-metals-financial families with internal distinctions), agricultural analysis (crop states, livestock integration, softs monitoring, policy tracking), energy analysis (crude states, refining layers, gas regionalization, transition tracking), metals analysis (precious-base-battery systems, policy environments), financial analysis (rate-currency-index environments, structure evolution), cross-complex integration (shared drivers, linkage networks, attractiveness ranking, rotation perspective), and output standardization (state reports, cadence calibration, dashboard integration, historical archives), avoiding aggregation errors, financial neglect, cross-complex incoherence, cadence mismatch, archive absence, and imported frameworks failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the Industry filter's application dimension where general structure and cycle analysis specializes into the concrete market complexes the framework trades: the complex-taxonomy foundation (the agricultural-complex definition where grains, oilseeds, livestock, and softs share biological production cycles, weather dependence, and policy entanglement whose common analytical grammar the economics companion develops and the framework deploys as one industry family; the energy-complex structure where crude, products, natural gas, and power share extraction economics, geopolitical exposure, and storage systems with the sub-complex distinctions that prevent false aggregation per the energy companions; the metals-complex organization where precious monetary metals, industrial base metals, and battery materials share extraction cycles but diverge in demand drivers requiring family-within-family analysis per the metals economics; the financial-complex treatment where rates, currencies, and equity indices function as industries whose structure analysis covers market organization, participant ecology, and product systems per the financial companions); the agricultural-complex industry analysis (the crop-complex state assessment combining global balance positions, hemisphere cycle phases, policy program status, and weather-regime context into the industry prior per the balance and calendar disciplines; the livestock-complex integration where herd cycles, feed costs, and protein demand connect grain industry state to animal-protein outlooks per the protein systems; the soft-complex monitoring where sugar, coffee, cocoa, and cotton carry their own production geographies and policy systems requiring distinct industry tracking per the tropical-crop calendars; the ag-policy-environment tracking where farm bills, biofuel mandates, and trade agreements reshape industry economics across legislative cycles per the policy-overlay section); the energy-complex industry analysis (the crude-complex state where OPEC-plus posture, shale-cycle position, global inventory levels, and demand-growth regime combine into the industry assessment per the crude intelligence systems; the product-and-refining layer where capacity utilization, crack-margin states, and product-balance tightness differentiate downstream from upstream conditions per the refining economics; the natural-gas-regional analysis where LNG connectivity progressively unifies formerly separate regional industries with the transition-state assessment per the gas systems; the energy-policy-and-transition tracking where sanctions regimes, climate policy, and investment discipline reshape industry structure across horizons per the policy and transition sections); the metals-complex industry analysis (the precious-metals state where real rates, central-bank demand, investment flows, and monetary-regime context drive the industry per the macro-linkage frameworks; the base-metals assessment where Chinese demand composition, mine-supply cycle position, smelting margins, and inventory states combine per the metals economics disciplines; the battery-materials industry tracking where energy-transition demand growth, project pipelines, and technology evolution create the newest and fastest-evolving complex requiring dedicated monitoring; the metals-policy-environment where resource nationalism, trade measures, and exchange-structure evolution reshape industry behavior per the state-actor frameworks); the financial-complex industry analysis (the rate-market state where curve positioning, policy divergence, funding conditions, and supply calendars define the industry environment per the rates companion's landscape section; the currency-market assessment where divergence patterns, terms-of-trade shifts, and flow regimes structure FX industry conditions per the currency frameworks; the equity-index environment where valuation states, earnings cycles, passive-structure evolution, and volatility regimes define index-market conditions per the index companion's systems; the market-structure-evolution tracking where electronic trading, product innovation, and regulation reshape financial-complex behavior across all three domains per the structure-evolution disciplines); the cross-complex industry integration (the shared-driver-mapping where dollar strength, growth regime, and policy states affect multiple complexes simultaneously requiring the coherence assessment per the transmission webs; the substitution-and-linkage-networks where feed-energy-food connections, metals-energy cost links, and cross-asset flows create industry interdependence per the linkage architectures of the intelligence companion; the relative-attractiveness-ranking where industry states across complexes compete for analytical attention and capital allocation per the opportunity-ranking disciplines; the complex-rotation-perspective where industry-cycle divergence creates cross-complex rotation opportunities analogous to sector rotation within equities per the rotation frameworks); and the industry-output standardization (the complex-state-report format where each tracked complex produces documented industry assessment with state classification, driver analysis, cycle position, and transmission priors per the output disciplines; the review-cadence-calibration where complex review frequency matches information velocity with weekly states for active markets and monthly for structural assessment per the monitoring traditions; the cross-complex-dashboard integration where industry states across complexes present in unified format enabling portfolio-level comparison per the dashboard disciplines; the historical-state-archive where industry classifications accumulate enabling base-rate analysis of state transitions and subsequent asset behavior per the record-keeping traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the complex-aggregation-error where metals or energy get treated as monoliths missing sub-complex divergence driving actual prices, remediated by the family-within-family discipline; the financial-complex-neglect whose industry analysis covers only physical commodities while rate and index complexes lack structured assessment, remediated by the financial-industry frameworks; the cross-complex-incoherence where simultaneous industry states contradict shared macro drivers without reconciliation, remediated by the integration mapping; the review-cadence-mismatch where slow structural analysis misses fast cycle shifts or vice versa, remediated by the frequency calibration; the state-archive-absent analyst whose industry classifications leave no history for base-rate learning, remediated by the archival discipline; and the imported-framework error where equity-sector analysis templates get applied to commodity complexes without physical-market adaptation, remediated by the complex-specific methodologies with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the Asset filter's verification stage where industry priors face asset-level fundamental analysis confirming, refining, or contradicting the transmitted view: the balance-verification system for commodities (the contract-specific supply-demand accounting where complex-level balances from the Industry filter get refined to contract granularity including deliverable supply, regional balances, and quality differentials per the balance-sheet disciplines of the economics companion; the stocks-and-inventory verification where exchange-registered, commercial, and strategic inventories get tracked against balance implications per the inventory-intelligence systems; the flow-verification practice where export pace, shipments, and throughput data test balance assumptions in near-real-time per the flow-monitoring disciplines; the balance-divergence investigation where contract prices inconsistent with verified balances trigger either information-disadvantage review or opportunity assessment per the incoherence-detection traditions); the valuation-anchoring for financial assets (the index-valuation verification where aggregate multiples, implied ERP, and earnings expectations get extracted and compared against Economic-level discount-rate and growth assessment per the valuation-engine disciplines of the index companion; the rate-fair-value analysis where policy-path expectations, term premia, and parity conditions locate fair value against market pricing per the valuation traditions of the rates companion; the currency-valuation-frameworks where PPP variants, real effective measures, and external-balance assessments provide long-horizon anchors per the fair-value disciplines of the FX sections; the implied-expectation-extraction discipline where current prices get reverse-engineered into embedded fundamental assumptions defining the divergence that constitutes potential edge per the reverse-engineering traditions); the company-and-issuer verification (the constituent-fundamental checks where major index components' earnings, guidance, and sector data verify or challenge index-level conclusions per the FSA and equity-analysis skills of the CFA companion; the producer-and-consumer-behavior verification where corporate hedging patterns, capex decisions, and inventory behavior of industry participants reveal physical-market truth per the commercial-intelligence traditions; the credit-and-balance-sheet signals where industry participants' financial health indicators confirm or stress-test cycle-position assessment per the credit-analysis disciplines; the management-guidance-aggregation where company outlooks aggregate into industry verification with the bias-adjustment awareness per the guidance-anatomy traditions); the market-implied-fundamental comparison (the price-implied-balance extraction where futures curves and option surfaces get read backward into market-implied supply-demand expectations per the curve-information disciplines; the sentiment-and-positioning cross-check where crowd positioning reveals whether fundamental views are already expressed by the market per the positioning-analysis traditions; the volatility-implied-event-assessment where option prices quantify market-assessed probability of fundamental scenarios per the implied-distribution readings; the triangulation-synthesis where personal fundamental analysis, market-implied expectations, and positioning evidence combine into divergence assessment with explicit edge articulation per the three-way reconciliation disciplines); the verification-outcome taxonomy (the confirmation category where asset-level evidence supports transmitted priors with quantified conviction strengthening per the signal-aggregation frameworks; the refinement category where verification adjusts magnitude, timing, or expression without reversing direction, requiring transmission-package updates; the contradiction category where asset-level evidence opposes Industry or Economic priors triggering the level-conflict resolution protocol per the contradiction-flagging disciplines; the insufficient-evidence category where verification cannot resolve with available data, producing explicit uncertainty statements and monitoring triggers per the confidence-communication traditions); and the verification-documentation standard (the evidence-inventory record where every verification conclusion lists supporting data with vintages and reliability grades per the source-discipline traditions; the divergence-quantification statement where the gap between personal assessment and market-implied expectation gets numerically expressed with confidence bounds per the probabilistic disciplines; the conviction-score-assignment where verified views carry structured conviction ratings feeding position sizing per the confidence-scoring systems; the re-verification-schedule where conclusions face planned reassessment as new data arrives per the monitoring-cadence traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the prior-confirmation hunter whose verification searches only for evidence supporting transmitted industry views, remediated by the contradiction-protocol discipline; the granularity-mismatch verifier whose contract-level conclusions rest on complex-level data without deliverable-supply and regional refinement, remediated by the contract-specific accounting; the implied-expectation skipper whose fundamental views never get compared against market pricing, leaving edge undefined, remediated by the extraction and triangulation discipline; the constituent-ignorant index analyst whose index conclusions never face company-level verification, remediated by the aggregation checks; the verification-timeout failure where analysis-paralysis prevents decision under event deadlines, remediated by the speed-depth management and insufficient-evidence categories; and the undocumented-verifier whose conclusions lack evidence inventories preventing review and calibration, remediated by the documentation standard with detection methods as the diagnostic.

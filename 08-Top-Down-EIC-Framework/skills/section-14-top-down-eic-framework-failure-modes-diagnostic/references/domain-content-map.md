Diagnose framework failures through taxonomy construction (input-processing-judgment-output categories), bias audits (confirmation detection, anchoring measurement, narrative capture, incentive awareness), transmission diagnostics (mapping validation, breakdown triage, drift monitoring, coherence audits), signal quality (expectancy decomposition, false-positive analysis, miss analysis, decay recognition), portfolio diagnosis (aggregation failures, drawdown forensics, limit evaluation, crisis review), evolution management (structural detection, version control, overfitting guards, comprehensive review), and diagnostic culture (blameless analysis, error normalization, skill development, honest records), avoiding outcome-only grading, bias blindness, mapping fossils, untracked misses, recency overfitting, and blame cultures failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the diagnostic layer where the framework examines its own failures, the meta-discipline that sustains analytical system reliability across regimes: the failure-taxonomy construction (the input-failure category where data errors, source gaps, and intake misses corrupt analysis before processing begins with the detection systems per the data-quality traditions; the processing-failure category where classification errors, cascade shortcuts, and mapping misapplication corrupt analysis despite correct inputs per the workflow-audit disciplines; the judgment-failure category where bias, pressure, and overconfidence distort analysis despite correct process per the behavioral literatures; the output-failure category where correct analysis fails through expression, sizing, timing, or communication errors in the final stages per the construction and execution traditions); the bias-audit systems (the confirmation-bias detection where analysis records get examined for evidence-search asymmetry supporting prior conclusions per the audit protocols; the anchoring-measurement where initial estimates including pre-event positions and early data get tested for persistent influence on final conclusions per the judgment literatures; the narrative-capture assessment where compelling story structures override contradictory quantitative evidence with the story-versus-arithmetic reconciliation discipline per the narrative-honesty traditions; the incentive-bias-awareness where position ownership, performance pressure, and career context systematically color analysis with the pre-commitment and review countermeasures per the ethics traditions); the transmission-diagnostic protocols (the mapping-validation systems where Economic-Industry-Asset relationships face periodic statistical re-estimation and episode-testing per the maintenance disciplines; the breakdown-triage where failed transmissions get classified into temporary noise, regime change, structural break, and mapping error with proportionate responses per the diagnostic traditions; the elasticity-drift monitoring where transmission coefficients get tracked through rolling estimation revealing gradual decay before failure per the drift-review systems; the cross-level-coherence audits where simultaneous level conclusions face consistency verification catching cascade corruption per the coherence disciplines); the signal-quality diagnostics (the expectancy-decomposition where signal performance separates into view-correctness, expression-efficiency, sizing-appropriateness, and timing contributions per the attribution traditions; the false-positive-analysis where signals failing divergence criteria get examined for filter-calibration errors per the quality-systems disciplines; the miss-analysis where market moves without framework signals get traced through intake, classification, and processing stages identifying system gaps per the review traditions; the decay-pattern-recognition where signal-source effectiveness erosion gets detected through rolling performance monitoring per the alpha-lifecycle disciplines); the portfolio-level failure diagnosis (the aggregation-failure analysis where individually sound signals produced portfolio-level problems through unmeasured correlation, concentration, or liquidity interaction per the risk-integration traditions; the drawdown-forensics where loss episodes decompose into market event, framework failure, and risk-system failure components per the post-mortem disciplines; the limit-system-evaluation where breaches and near-misses get analyzed for calibration adequacy per the governance traditions; the crisis-performance-review where stress-period behavior gets assessed against protocols with improvement extraction per the crisis-review traditions); the framework-evolution management (the structural-change-detection where market-structure evolution including product innovation, participant shifts, and regulation alters framework validity per the evolution-monitoring traditions; the version-controlled-modification discipline where framework changes get documented, tested, and dated maintaining analytical comparability per the change-management systems; the overfitting-guard systems where framework modifications responding to recent failures get tested against broader history preventing recency-driven degradation per the modeling-caution traditions; the periodic-comprehensive-review where the complete framework faces scheduled deep audit against accumulated evidence per the system-audit disciplines); and the diagnostic-culture foundations (the blameless-analysis practice where failure diagnosis focuses on system improvement rather than personal fault enabling honest reporting per the psychological-safety traditions; the error-normalization where mistakes receive treatment as expected information sources within probabilistic systems rather than character failures per the calibration cultures; the diagnostic-skill-development where failure-analysis capability itself receives deliberate practice and mentoring per the capability traditions; the honest-record-commitment where diagnostic findings enter permanent documentation resisting the revision temptation per the research-integrity traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the outcome-only grader whose framework evaluation rests on P-and-L results conflating process quality with luck, remediated by the decomposition disciplines; the bias-blind operator whose analysis lacks audit systems leaving confirmation and narrative capture undetected, remediated by the audit protocols; the mapping-fossil keeper whose transmission relationships survive regime changes untested until breakdown, remediated by the validation systems; the miss-untracked system whose framework gaps never surface because absent signals leave no record, remediated by the miss-analysis discipline; the recency-overfitter whose framework mutates with each recent failure losing accumulated validity, remediated by the guard systems; and the blame-culture diagnostician whose failure reviews produce defensive documentation rather than honest improvement, remediated by the culture foundations with detection methods as the diagnostic.

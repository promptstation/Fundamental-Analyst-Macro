# Domain Content Map — Company/Asset Level III: Positioning, Market State, and Timing Context

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Assess market context through positioning states (instrument audits, crowding percentiles, commercial-speculative decomposition, cross-instrument coherence), market states (volatility regimes, liquidity determination, correlation reading, technical awareness), timing analysis (event windows, seasonal context, flow calendars, catalyst sequencing), entry planning (strategy selection, size sequencing, cost minimization, abandonment criteria), reversal risk (unwind scenarios, stop maps, adverse selection, exit inventories), and integration output (state summaries, timing synthesis, risk packages, monitoring triggers), avoiding context blindness, timing perfectionism, positioning literalism, technical override.
2. Plan absence, and exit orphans failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the Asset filter's market-context stage where verified fundamentals meet the positioning and microstructure reality that determines timing, expression cost, and reversal risk: the positioning-state assessment (the instrument-level positioning audit where CFTC categories, exchange data, and option-implied positioning locate the specific crowd state in each candidate instrument per the positioning-intelligence systems of the companions; the crowding-percentile analysis where current positioning ranks against historical distributions defining reversal-vulnerability states per the extremes methodologies; the commercial-versus-speculative decomposition where hedger behavior and speculative interest carry different information per the flow-interpretation disciplines of the derivatives companion; the cross-instrument positioning coherence where related contracts and complexes show consistent or divergent crowd behavior revealing information migration per the coherence traditions); the market-state evaluation (the volatility-regime assessment where current and term-structure volatility states condition position sizing, structure selection, and hedging costs per the volatility-regime frameworks; the liquidity-state determination where spreads, depth, and resilience locate execution conditions affecting achievable entry and exit per the systemic-liquidity disciplines; the correlation-regime reading where cross-asset correlation states determine portfolio interaction of candidate positions per the regime-conditioning traditions; the technical-structure awareness where price levels, trend states, and flow-attracting structures condition timing without overriding fundamental logic per the price-behavior literacy); the timing-context analysis (the event-window mapping where upcoming releases, expirations, and policy events create timing considerations for entry, hold, and exit decisions per the calendar-integration disciplines; the seasonal-context integration where calendar patterns from crop seasons through expiration cycles condition timing probabilities per the seasonality-evidence traditions; the flow-calendar-awareness where mechanical flows from index rebalancing, option expiry, and month-end activity create predictable pressure windows per the mechanical-flow analysis; the catalyst-sequencing design where multiple expected catalysts get ordered by timing and impact potential structuring position building and adjustment per the lifecycle-planning traditions); the entry-execution planning (the entry-strategy selection where immediate, staged, limit-based, and event-triggered approaches match conviction strength and timing analysis per the execution-craft disciplines; the size-sequencing design where initial positions and add-triggers get predefined by verification strength and market-state evolution per the sizing-integration traditions; the cost-minimization execution where liquidity windows, algorithm selection, and spread management reduce entry costs per the transaction-cost disciplines; the abandonment-criteria definition where entry plans carry explicit conditions for standing down when market state deteriorates or information changes per the no-trade disciplines); the reversal-and-exit-risk assessment (the unwind-scenario analysis where positioning extremes define potential forced-flow sequences against new positions per the deleveraging-cascade mechanics; the stop-and-liquidation-map awareness where concentrated stop levels and margin thresholds create predictable volatility zones per the microstructure ecologies; the adverse-selection-review where immediate post-entry price behavior carries information about unseen flow requiring rapid reassessment per the market-reading disciplines; the exit-option-inventory where planned exits across profit-targets, time-decay, invalidation, and stress scenarios get defined before entry per the lifecycle-management systems); and the context-integration output (the market-state-summary format where positioning, volatility, liquidity, and timing context condense into documented assessment per the output standards; the timing-recommendation-synthesis where fundamental urgency meets market-state friction producing entry-timing guidance with confidence statements per the decision-support traditions; the risk-context-package where positioning and state analysis feeds the risk-management integration of the following sections with explicit vulnerability identification; the context-monitoring-triggers where state changes including positioning shifts, volatility transitions, and liquidity deterioration activate reassessment per the alert-system disciplines) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the context-blind fundamentalist whose verified views enter without positioning, volatility, or liquidity assessment, remediated by the integration requirement; the timing-perfectionist whose wait for optimal context delays entry until the fundamental edge decays, remediated by the sufficiency-standard discipline; the positioning-literalist who treats crowd extremes as timing signals rather than vulnerability context, remediated by the interpretation frameworks; the technical-override victim whose price-level analysis reverses verified fundamental conclusions without transmission logic, remediated by the subordinate-role discipline; the entry-plan-absent positioner whose market entries improvise under live conditions, remediated by the execution-planning standards; and the exit-option-orphan whose positions lack predefined exit inventory until stress forces improvisation, remediated by the lifecycle-integration discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

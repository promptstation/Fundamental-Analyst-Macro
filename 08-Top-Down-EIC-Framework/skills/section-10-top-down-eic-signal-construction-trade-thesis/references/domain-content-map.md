## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Construct trade signals through anatomy definition (thesis structures, conviction decomposition, falsification specification, quality tiering), specification craft (instrument finalization, sizing frameworks, entry sequencing, cost budgets), scenario architecture (tree construction, payoff mapping, stress overlays, adjustment ladders), portfolio integration (contribution analysis, correlation assessment, capacity checks, offset recognition), communication production (decision documents, risk packages, stakeholder variants, record management), and quality systems (pre-commitment review, consistency audits, expectancy tracking, craft evolution), avoiding thesis orphans, conviction inflation, scenario absence, context ignorance, improvisation dependence.
2. Review skipping failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the construction stage where verified divergences become fully specified trade theses with documented logic, sizing, and lifecycle management: the signal-anatomy definition (the thesis-statement structure where every signal articulates the fundamental view, market-implied expectation, divergence magnitude, transmission logic, catalyst sequence, and horizon in standardized format per the documentation traditions; the conviction-components decomposition separating view-confidence, edge-size, and timing-certainty whose product determines actionable conviction per the confidence-scoring systems; the falsification-specification where each thesis carries explicit invalidation conditions including data thresholds, price levels, and time limits per the falsification disciplines of the macro companion; the signal-quality-tiering where construction completeness and verification depth classify signals into commitment levels with corresponding capital treatment per the quality-gate architectures); the trade-specification craft (the instrument-and-structure finalization where the expression universe selection of the asset level gets locked with complete specification including contract months, strikes, ratios, and roll plans per the selection disciplines; the sizing-determination framework where conviction scores, volatility states, correlation exposure, and risk budget combine into position size per the risk-budgeting traditions of the portfolio companions; the entry-sequencing plan where timing analysis converts into staged execution with add-reduce triggers and abandonment conditions per the entry-planning disciplines; the cost-budget-integration where all-in transaction, carry, and hedging costs get computed against expected edge defining net expectancy per the cost-analysis traditions); the scenario-and-payoff architecture (the scenario-tree construction where bull-base-bear branches carry probability weights and price implications producing expected-value distributions per the scenario disciplines across the curriculum; the payoff-profile-mapping where instrument structures translate scenarios into P-and-L outcomes with the asymmetry analysis informing structure selection per the payoff-thinking traditions; the stress-overlay-testing where tail scenarios including liquidity evaporation and correlation convergence test position survival per the stress-testing disciplines; the adjustment-ladder design where predefined responses at scenario thresholds replace improvisation with the branch-by-branch planning per the lifecycle traditions); the portfolio-context integration (the exposure-contribution analysis where each signal's factor, complex, and direction exposures get mapped against existing portfolio positions per the factor-decomposition disciplines; the correlation-and-diversification assessment where new signals interact with holdings under both calm and stress regimes per the portfolio-integration traditions; the capacity-and-concentration checks where aggregate exposure by complex, factor, and event stays within governance limits per the limit systems; the hedge-and-offset-recognition where existing positions partially express or hedge new signals with the net-exposure calculation preventing double-counting per the netting disciplines); the thesis-communication production (the internal-decision-document where complete signal packages support commitment decisions with the recommendation-first structure per the executive-communication companion's memo disciplines; the risk-officer-package where exposure, stress, and limit implications present in risk-system formats per the risk-communication traditions; the stakeholder-briefing variants where signals reaching external parties including clients and management carry appropriate depth and confidence language per the audience-layering disciplines; the record-and-distribution management where thesis documents enter versioned archives supporting audit, review, and calibration per the documentation systems); and the construction-quality systems (the pre-commitment-review discipline where signals face structured challenge including devil's-advocate examination and checklist verification before capital commitment per the review traditions; the construction-consistency-audits where signals across analysts and periods follow standardized formats enabling comparison and aggregation per the standardization disciplines; the expectancy-tracking systems where constructed signals' expected versus realized performance accumulates into construction-quality measurement per the calibration traditions; the construction-craft-evolution where template, sizing, and scenario methods improve through documented iteration per the framework-evolution management) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the thesis-orphan whose market positions exist without documented logic, sizing rationale, or invalidation conditions, remediated by the anatomy discipline; the conviction-inflator whose sizing reflects enthusiasm rather than decomposed confidence components, remediated by the scoring framework; the scenario-absent constructor whose payoffs get imagined only in the favorable branch, remediated by the tree-construction requirement; the portfolio-context-ignorer whose individually reasonable signals aggregate into concentrated hidden exposure, remediated by the contribution analysis; the improvisation-dependent manager whose positions lack adjustment ladders until stress forces panic decisions, remediated by the ladder-design discipline; and the review-skipper whose signals bypass challenge before commitment, converting the framework's quality gates into formalities, remediated by the pre-commitment discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.

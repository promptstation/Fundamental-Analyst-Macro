# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Construct trade signals through anatomy definition (thesis structures
**Scenario:** provide a realistic top-down EIC analysis scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** construct trade signals through anatomy definition (thesis structures, conviction decomposition, falsification specification, quality tiering), specification craft (instrument finalization, sizing frameworks, entry sequencing, cost budgets), scenario architecture (tree construction, payoff mapping, stress overlays, adjustment ladders), portfolio integration (contribution analysis, correlation assessment, capacity checks, offset recognition), communication production (decision documents, risk packages, stakeholder variants, record management), and quality systems (pre-commitment review, consistency audits, expectancy tracking, craft evolution), avoiding thesis orphans, conviction inflation, scenario absence, context ignorance, improvisation dependence.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

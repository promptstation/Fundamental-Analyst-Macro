## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the first filter stage where global economic conditions get classified into the regime states that condition every downstream transmission: the regime-classification architecture (the growth-inflation quadrant system where expanding-contracting growth crossed with rising-falling inflation produces the four canonical states whose asset-behavior base rates the framework deploys as priors per the regime traditions of the index companion; the policy-regime overlay where easing-tightening and conventional-unconventional states modify quadrant implications per the central-bank frameworks of the rates companion; the financial-conditions dimension where credit spreads, funding costs, and liquidity states add the stress-normal axis completing the environment description; the regime-definition discipline where each state carries explicit indicator criteria, entry-exit rules, and historical episode archives preventing impressionistic classification); the indicator-system construction (the growth-indicator panel where activity surveys, labor data, production statistics, and trade flows combine into current-state assessment with the diffusion and momentum readings per the indicator disciplines of the macro companion; the inflation-indicator panel spanning price indices, wage measures, expectation proxies, and commodity input costs per the price-system sections; the policy-indicator tracking where central-bank communication, rate paths, and balance-sheet operations locate policy stance per the Fed-watching and divergence frameworks; the indicator-weighting methodology where signal reliability, lead-time, and regime-relevance determine composite construction with the reweighting discipline as evidence accumulates); the nowcast-and-state estimation (the current-state nowcasting where mixed-frequency indicators produce real-time regime probability estimates per the nowcasting methods of the macro companion; the transition-detection systems where regime-change signals from indicator divergence, market pricing, and structural breaks trigger classification review per the break-detection disciplines; the real-time-vintage discipline where classification uses only information available at decision time, preventing hindsight-contaminated regime histories per the data-vintage traditions; the state-persistence-assessment where regime duration distributions and transition probabilities inform forward expectations with the base-rate humility per the cycle frameworks); the global-regime integration (the dominant-economy-weighting where US, European, Chinese, and Japanese regime states combine into global classification with the spillover-evidence weighting per the global-cycle research; the synchronization-measurement where cross-country regime alignment determines whether global factors or national divergences drive asset behavior per the international frameworks; the EM-regime-layer where dollar-funding conditions and vulnerability rankings add the emerging-market environment per the global-dollar systems of the rates companion; the commodity-economy overlay where resource-exporter and importer regime differences connect economic classification to the physical complexes per the economics companions); the market-implied-regime extraction (the price-implied-state reading where curve shapes, equity valuations, credit spreads, and currency levels reveal the regime markets currently price per the implied-information disciplines across the companions; the implied-versus-assessed divergence analysis where personal regime classification differing from market pricing defines the potential edge requiring explicit articulation per the expectation-extraction traditions; the consensus-tracking integration where survey forecasts and strategy-house views locate the crowd's regime assessment against which divergence gets measured; the regime-pricing-timeline awareness distinguishing what markets price now from what they will price at horizon, the horizon-matching discipline from the valuation traditions); the economic-level output design (the state-statement format where the Economic level produces a documented regime assessment with indicators, probabilities, transition scenarios, and market-implied comparison per the output-standard disciplines; the downstream-transmission-package where regime conclusions feed Industry-level priors through the documented mappings of the architecture section with explicit sensitivity statements; the revision-protocol where state assessments update on schedule and on trigger events with version tracking per the change-management traditions; the confidence-communication where regime calls carry calibrated certainty language per the uncertainty disciplines of the executive companion applied to internal analysis); and the level-failure-mode library (the regime-anchoring failure where prior classifications persist against contradicting indicator evidence through confirmation filtering; the indicator-cherry-picking failure where composite construction unconsciously weights confirming signals; the transition-lag failure where classification updates trail market pricing because trigger criteria were set too conservatively; the single-economy blindspot where national regime focus misses the global factor dominating asset behavior; and the implied-regime-copying failure where personal assessment merely adopts market pricing, eliminating any divergence edge before analysis begins) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the impressionistic-classifier whose regime calls rest on narrative rather than indicator criteria, remediated by the classification-architecture discipline; the stale-composite operator whose indicator weights survive regime and reliability changes unreviewed, remediated by the weighting methodology; the hindsight-regime historian whose backtested classifications used unavailable data, remediated by the vintage discipline; the synchronization-assumer whose global regime applies uniformly across divergent national states, remediated by the integration layer; the divergence-unarticulated analyst whose regime view never gets compared against market-implied pricing, remediated by the extraction and comparison discipline; and the output-orphan whose Economic assessment never feeds documented Industry-level transmission, remediated by the output-design standards with detection methods as the diagnostic.

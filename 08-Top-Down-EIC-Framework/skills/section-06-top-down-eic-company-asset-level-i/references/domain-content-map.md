## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Select expression instruments through universe mapping (complex inventories, liquidity tiers, cost comparison, access audit), characteristic analysis (exposure purity, payoff profiling, horizon matching, mechanics literacy), the structure toolkit (calendar spreads, intermarket structures, option libraries, synthetic fluency), the selection process (view translation, multi-alternative comparison, refinement iteration, no-trade classification), fundamental integration (contract balances, curve assessment, positioning context, event overlay), and documentation standards (rationale records, specification sheets.
2. Review triggers, contribution statements), avoiding default expression, cost blindness, contaminated exposure, mechanics ignorance, forced trades, and rationale absence failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the Asset filter's first stage where industry priors meet the concrete instrument universe whose selection determines whether analysis survives contact with markets: the expression-universe mapping (the complex-instrument inventory where each market complex maps its complete tradeable set including futures, options, spreads, ETFs, swaps, and physical-market instruments per the instrument landscapes of the derivatives and complex companions; the liquidity-tier classification where front-month depth, deferred-contract thinness, and option-strike concentration define executable size by instrument per the liquidity disciplines; the cost-structure-comparison where spreads, commissions, margin, financing, and roll costs rank expression alternatives by total carry per the cost-analysis traditions; the access-and-capability audit where account permissions, clearing arrangements, and operational capacity constrain the theoretical universe into the practical one per the operational-risk disciplines); the instrument-characteristic analysis (the exposure-purity assessment where instruments carry intended exposure plus unintended contamination including currency effects, roll dynamics, and counterparty risk per the expression-matching disciplines of the rates and index companions; the convexity-and-payoff profiling where linear futures, asymmetric options, and structured combinations produce different risk-reward geometries for identical views per the payoff-thinking foundations; the horizon-compatibility matching where instrument expiry, liquidity migration, and carry profiles suit specific view timeframes per the horizon-layering disciplines; the settlement-and-mechanics literacy where delivery versus cash settlement, exercise conventions, and notice-day mechanics constrain holding periods per the contract-mechanics mastery of the derivatives companion); the spread-and-structure toolkit (the calendar-spread expression where curve views trade through intra-market spreads with the storage-economics linkage per the curve sections of the companions; the inter-commodity-and-intermarket structures where relative-value views express through ratio trades, crush and crack margins, and cross-complex spreads per the margin-trading systems; the option-structure library spanning verticals, calendars, risk reversals, and collars whose selection matches conviction, volatility assessment, and cost budget per the strategy libraries of the derivatives companion; the synthetic-construction fluency where parity relationships enable equivalent exposure through multiple routes whose cost and risk differences create selection advantages per the pricing-web disciplines); the asset-selection decision process (the view-to-instrument translation where industry priors and asset-level fundamental assessment combine into candidate expressions with explicit rationale documentation per the thesis-construction disciplines; the multi-alternative comparison discipline where at least three expression routes face structured evaluation on exposure, cost, risk, and liquidity before selection, preventing default-expression failure per the anti-pattern libraries; the expression-refinement iteration where initial selection faces stress-scenario and adverse-move analysis with structure adjustment per the lifecycle-planning traditions; the no-trade-classification where analysis concludes that no instrument adequately expresses the view or edge is insufficient after costs, with explicit documentation preventing forced trades per the signal-quality disciplines); the asset-level fundamental integration (the contract-specific-balance analysis where individual contract fundamentals including deliverable supply, regional basis, and quality differentials modify complex-level priors per the balance disciplines of the economics companions; the curve-and-carry-assessment where current term structure and roll economics favor or penalize each expression direction per the curve-mechanics sections; the positioning-context-integration where crowd positioning in specific instruments conditions entry timing and reversal risk per the positioning disciplines; the event-calendar-overlay where contract-specific events including expirations, notices, and report days shape execution sequencing per the calendar-management traditions); and the selection-documentation standard (the expression-rationale record where selected instruments carry documented justification against alternatives enabling review and learning per the decision-journal traditions; the specification-sheet discipline where every position records contract terms, multiplier, margin, liquidity state, and mechanical constraints preventing operational surprise per the mechanics-mastery requirements; the review-trigger-definition where instrument-level changes including liquidity shifts, margin adjustments, and rule changes trigger reassessment per the monitoring disciplines; the portfolio-contribution-statement where each expression's intended contribution to portfolio exposure and return gets explicit statement per the risk-budgeting connections of the next sections) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the default-expresser whose every view routes through naked front-month futures without alternatives comparison, remediated by the multi-alternative discipline; the cost-blind selector whose edge calculations ignore roll, spread, and financing carry consuming the thesis, remediated by the cost-structure comparison; the contaminated-exposure holder whose instrument carries unintended currency, sector, or convexity effects never assessed, remediated by the purity-assessment discipline; the mechanics-ignorant positioner who discovers delivery, notice, and exercise rules through operational crisis, remediated by the settlement literacy; the forced-trade producer whose analysis concludes no-edge but positions anyway through action bias, remediated by the no-trade classification; and the rationale-absent selector whose instrument choices leave no comparison record preventing expression-skill development, remediated by the documentation standard with detection methods as the diagnostic.

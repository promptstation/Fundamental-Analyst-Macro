## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the applied case dimension where the complete framework runs against documented market episodes, building the pattern library that turns methodology into judgment: the case-analysis methodology (the episode-reconstruction discipline where historical cases get analyzed through the framework levels using only information available at the time, the real-time-vintage practice preventing hindsight contamination per the data-vintage traditions; the decision-point-mapping where each episode identifies the moments when analysis could have produced signals and what the framework stages would have yielded per the event-processing disciplines; the outcome-attribution analysis where market results decompose into framework-correct execution, framework-error, and pure-unpredictability categories per the calibration traditions; the lesson-extraction protocol where each case produces explicit framework modifications, mapping updates, or scenario-library additions per the knowledge-management systems); the macro-regime case library (the inflation-transition cases where 2021-2023 regime shift from low-inflation era assumptions through cascading policy tightening demonstrates Economic-level transition detection, Industry-level duration-sensitivity mapping, and Asset-level expression timing with the documented forecast failures across the progression; the financial-crisis episodes spanning 2008 and March 2020 where liquidity-regime collapse demonstrates the stress-transmission failures that normal-regime mappings produce per the crisis-case traditions; the divergence-cycle cases where 2014-2015 policy divergence and currency episodes demonstrate the complete cascade from central-bank analysis through cross-asset transmission per the rates companion histories; the regime-stability periods where extended calm demonstrates the framework's low-signal operation and the discipline of not forcing trades per the no-trade classifications); the commodity-complex case library (the weather-market episodes where drought and crop-failure events demonstrate the complete cascade from meteorological intelligence through balance revision to curve and option expression per the agricultural case traditions; the supply-shock cases spanning geopolitical disruptions and cartel decisions where 1970s embargoes through modern OPEC-plus episodes document shock-propagation patterns and premium dynamics per the energy case libraries; the cycle-turn cases where multi-year production cycle transitions demonstrate Industry-level cycle analysis converting to Asset-level positioning across extended horizons per the production-cycle traditions; the storage-and-curve episodes where inventory extremes produced documented curve behaviors validating or challenging the transmission mappings per the theory-of-storage case evidence); the financial-complex case library (the curve-and-policy cases where tightening cycles produced documented yield-curve behavior testing the rate-transmission mappings per the curve-episode traditions; the volatility-regime episodes including the 2018 volatility-product collapse and pandemic volatility extremes demonstrating market-state assessment failures and successes per the volatility case libraries; the positioning-crowding cases where documented squeeze and unwind episodes including commodity corners and currency crises demonstrate the positioning-context disciplines; the cross-asset-contagion episodes where transmission webs activated across complexes documenting the coherence-audit value per the crisis-transmission histories); the framework-failure case collection (the transmission-breakdown cases where documented mapping failures including relationship inversions and elasticity shifts demonstrate the fossilization risks per the structural-break literatures; the level-skipping disasters where analysis failures trace to bypassed framework stages with the specific missed-verification documentation per the diagnostic traditions; the confirmation-cascade episodes where bias laundering through framework structure produced systematically wrong signals with the audit-trail evidence per the behavioral literatures; the signal-proliferation failures where capital and attention fragmentation across excessive candidates degraded portfolio performance per the capacity-discipline traditions); the case-library maintenance (the taxonomy-and-indexing systems where episodes organize by regime, complex, failure mode, and lesson type enabling retrieval during live analysis per the library-management traditions; the analog-matching practice where current situations get compared against library cases with the structural-difference discipline preventing mechanical analogy per the historical-analog traditions; the living-library discipline where recent episodes enter analysis while older cases receive periodic revalidation against updated data and understanding; the team-case-circulation where case analyses distribute across analytical teams building shared pattern recognition per the knowledge-sharing systems); and the judgment-development synthesis (the pattern-recognition-cultivation where repeated case analysis builds the intuitive assessment that operates alongside explicit framework execution per the expertise-development traditions; the calibration-through-cases where historical base rates from case libraries condition probability assessments preventing both overconfidence and excessive caution per the calibration disciplines; the humility-through-failures where the failure-case collection maintains awareness of framework limits, the epistemic-hygiene that separates professional from promotional analysis; the judgment-framework-balance where experienced analysts know when to follow cascade output and when documented unusual conditions warrant deviation with explicit rationale per the override-governance traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the hindsight-contaminated case student whose episode analyses use information unavailable at decision time, producing false confidence in framework power, remediated by the real-time-vintage discipline; the cherry-picked-case curator whose library confirms framework effectiveness while failure episodes stay unexamined, remediated by the failure-collection requirement; the analog-mechanical applier whose current analysis copies historical episodes without structural-difference assessment, remediated by the matching discipline; the lesson-unextracted reviewer whose case studies entertain without producing framework modifications, remediated by the extraction protocol; the library-fossil keeper whose case collection stops accumulating at study completion, remediated by the living-library discipline; and the intuition-suppressor whose framework adherence forbids the pattern-recognition judgment that case analysis exists to build, remediated by the judgment-balance traditions with detection methods as the diagnostic.

# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Navigate the index landscape through concept and function (aggregation principles
**Scenario:** provide a realistic equity index analysis scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** navigate the index landscape through concept and function (aggregation principles, benchmark dominance, instrument evolution, taxonomy), construction methodology (weighting schemes and concentration, inclusion mechanics and flow events, dividend conventions, change monitoring), the US ecosystem (S&P complex, Nasdaq character, Dow-Russell patterns, sector products), the global landscape (European systems, Asian distinctness, MSCI architecture, cross-listing), product mapping (futures-options mechanics, ETF arbitrage systems, swap layers, option scale effects), participant structure (passive dominance, corporate buyers, derivative overlays, retail-systematic layers), and information function (aggregate signal decomposition, cross-index reading, microstructure extraction), avoiding methodology ignorance, variant confusion, product mismatch, passive denial, and single-ecosystem provincialism failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

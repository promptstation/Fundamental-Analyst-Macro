## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the corporate-behavior layer whose earnings generation, capital return, and financing decisions drive index fundamentals and create observable flow patterns: the aggregate-earnings system (the index-earnings construction where reported and operating earnings across constituents aggregate with the accounting-convention and non-GAAP-adjustment issues that make published figures differ by provider; the earnings-quality assessment where accrual patterns, one-time items, and tax effects require normalization for trend analysis per the financial-statement traditions of the CFA companion; the revenue-margin-decomposition discipline separating volume-price-driven revenue growth from margin management in earnings trajectory analysis; the estimate-ecosystem where consensus aggregation, revision momentum, and whisper dynamics create the expectation baseline earnings events trade against per the surprise disciplines); the buyback machine (the buyback-scale reality where repurchases represent the largest single demand source for US equities across most periods, making corporate behavior a primary flow variable; the authorization-execution pipeline where announced programs convert to actual purchases over quarters with the execution-pattern analysis of daily-window rules and price-sensitivity behavior; the buyback-driver decomposition where tax-advantaged distribution preference, EPS-management incentive, capital-allocation philosophy, and cash-accumulation trends drive program decisions per the corporate-finance literature; the cyclical-behavior pattern where buyback volume rises in expansions and collapses in stress, procyclically amplifying both directions per the documented evidence); the equity-issuance offset (the gross-versus-net-supply distinction where issuance, secondary offerings, and convertible supply partially offset buyback demand with the net-flow calculation analysts must construct; the issuance-cycle behavior where companies issue at valuations management deems favorable creating observable supply waves per the market-timing literature; the SPAC and structural-issuance varieties whose waves shift aggregate supply dynamics by era; the employee-compensation supply where option exercise and stock-based compensation create continuous dilution requiring per-share adjustment in earnings analysis); the corporate-balance-sheet cycle (the leverage-cycle tracking where corporate debt issuance, credit-quality migration, and refinancing walls interact with earnings capacity per the credit-cycle frameworks of the cycle section; the cash-holding dynamics where accumulated corporate cash provides recession buffer and buyback fuel with the precautionary-motive shifts through uncertainty regimes; the capex-cycle analysis where capital expenditure waves drive earnings capacity and sector demand with the equipment-and-structures decomposition signaling cycle confidence; the M&A-cycle behavior where merger waves create flow patterns, premium effects, and sector consolidation with the financing-condition dependence documented); the earnings-event system (the earnings-season rhythm where quarterly reporting concentration creates five-week information cascades with the aggregate revision patterns driving index revaluation per the event disciplines; the guidance-anatomy where forward corporate guidance aggregates into index earnings expectations with the conservatism-sandbagging and beat-raise dynamics documented in the literature; the earnings-surprise market-response evolution where reaction magnitudes and drift patterns shift with information environment and positioning; the cross-sector earnings intelligence where early reporters from cyclical sectors provide macro-verification signals for later reporters and aggregate forecasts per the information-cascade traditions); the shareholder-yield synthesis (the total-yield construction combining dividends, buybacks, and buyback-adjusted metrics where shareholder yield versus bond yields informs the equity-bond comparison central to allocation flows; the payout-policy-cycle analysis where yield preferences shift with tax regimes, rate environments, and governance pressure; the dividend-growth aggregate tracking as the income-component of index total return with the aristocrat-behavior stability through cycles; the yield-competition dynamics where rising bond yields challenge equity income attractiveness triggering allocation shifts per the discount-rate channel); and the corporate-flow integration (the flow-accounting framework where corporate buybacks and issuance, household allocation, pension rebalancing, foreign investment, and fund flows compose the demand-supply ledger whose net shifts drive valuation per the flows section; the corporate-cycle-coherence checking where earnings trajectory, buyback capacity, leverage position, and capex plans must tell consistent stories or reveal stress or opportunity; the policy-and-tax-overlay where corporate tax changes, regulation, and industrial policy shift earnings and flow behavior with the fiscal-policy transmission from the macro companion applied to corporate decisions; the long-run structural tracking where margin trends, concentration increases, intangible-asset growth, and globalization shifts reshape the corporate engine across decades defying short-sample projection) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the EPS-obsessed analyst whose per-share focus misses that buyback-driven EPS growth leaves aggregate value unchanged, remediated by the aggregate-versus-per-share discipline; the buyback-extrapolator whose demand models assume corporate purchasing persists through the stress where it historically collapses, remediated by the cyclical-behavior evidence; the gross-flow accounter whose supply-demand ledger double-counts or omits issuance and compensation dilution, remediated by the net-flow construction; the guidance-literalist whose earnings forecasts accept corporate sandbagging patterns without adjustment calibration, remediated by the beat-raise dynamics literacy; the single-quarter extrapolator whose earnings-trend calls rest on one season's surprises against cycle-position context, remediated by the cycle-integration discipline; and the yield-comparison simplifier whose equity-bond analysis ignores risk, growth, and liquidity differences behind headline yields, remediated by the total-yield framework with detection methods as the diagnostic.

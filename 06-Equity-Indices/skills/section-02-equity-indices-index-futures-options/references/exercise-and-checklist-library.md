# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Trade index derivatives through futures architecture (contract families
**Scenario:** provide a realistic equity index analysis scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** trade index derivatives through futures architecture (contract families, cash settlement mechanics, fair-value basis systems, continuous trading), basis and carry analytics (implied rate extraction, dividend sensitivity, calendar spreads, arbitrage programs), the options complex (cash-settled mechanics, VIX connection, zero-days revolution, strategy ecosystem), expiration and event mechanics (witching cycles, month-end flows, roll dynamics, settlement microstructure), execution practice (depth analysis, stop ecology, cross-venue coordination, cost modeling), risk specifics (margin regimes, gap exposure, correlation convergence, gamma timelines), and product selection (expression matching, leverage calibration, structure library, liquidity conditioning), avoiding basis blindness, settlement surprise, zero-days gambling, margin cascades, single-instrument defaults, and divergence panic failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone

**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the derivative instruments through which index analysis becomes executed positions, with mechanics specific to cash-settled macro instruments: the index-futures architecture (the contract-family mapping where E-mini and micro S&P, Nasdaq, Dow, and Russell contracts plus global equivalents provide size-graduated exposure with the notional and multiplier arithmetic every position calculation requires; the cash-settlement mechanics where special opening quotations computed from constituent opening prints define final settlement, differing fundamentally from commodity delivery per the derivatives companion; the fair-value-basis system where futures trade at spot plus carry minus dividends with the basis dynamics revealing rate expectations, dividend forecasts, and supply-demand pressure in one observable spread; the continuous-trading advantage where near-twenty-four-hour sessions make index futures the global macro expression vehicle whose overnight moves lead cash opens); the basis-and-carry analytics (the fair-value calculation discipline where implied financing rates extracted from basis levels reveal market-perceived funding conditions and demand pressure per the cost-of-carry foundations; the dividend-forecast sensitivity where index futures basis embeds expected dividends over contract life with the estimation uncertainty concentrated in index-level dividend forecasting; the calendar-spread behavior where inter-contract spreads express carry, event, and positioning views with the quarterly-cycle liquidity patterns; the arbitrage-program reality where index-arb trading connects futures and cash baskets whose activity signals and execution affect both markets during volatility); the index-options complex (the SPX-style cash-settled option mechanics with European exercise, section-1256 tax treatment in US context, and the multiplier conventions distinguishing index from single-stock options; the VIX-connection where volatility-index products derive from index-option prices making the options market the source of the fear-gauge complex per the volatility section; the zero-days-to-expiration revolution whose daily-expiry products reshaped option volume, gamma dynamics, and intraday market behavior with the dealer-hedging feedback documented in recent research; the strategy-ecosystem spanning covered-call income products, collar structures, and dispersion trades whose aggregate flows condition index behavior); the expiration-and-event mechanics (the triple-and-quadruple-witching cycles where simultaneous futures and option expirations concentrate rebalancing flows with documented volume and volatility patterns; the month-end and quarter-end effects where pension and index rebalancing create predictable flow pressure per the passive-structure analysis; the roll-period dynamics where futures position migration creates calendar-window behavior analysts anticipate; the settlement-day microstructure where SOQ computation windows attract gaming-adjacent strategies and volatility requiring execution awareness); the execution-and-liquidity practice (the depth-and-book analysis where E-mini market structure with its queue dynamics and large-order visibility shapes execution strategy; the stop-and-momentum ecology where concentrated stop levels and algorithmic responses create self-reinforcing intraday moves during stress; the cross-venue coordination where futures, ETFs, options, and cash baskets price simultaneously with divergence windows creating both risk and opportunity; the cost-modeling discipline where spread, impact, and financing costs across instrument alternatives determine efficient expression per the transaction-cost traditions); the risk-management specifics (the margin-regime behavior where exchange margin adjustments through volatility cycles force leveraged-position adjustment per the margin-procyclicality lessons of the derivatives companion; the overnight-gap exposure where continuous trading reduces but does not eliminate gap risk around news and weekend events; the correlation-convergence awareness where index positions across geographies and styles diversify in calm and converge in stress per the crisis sections; the option-gamma-timeline management where positions' gamma profiles evolve through expiry cycles changing hedging behavior market-wide per the dealer-flow analysis); and the product-selection synthesis (the expression-matching discipline where view horizon, size, cost sensitivity, and tax context select among futures, options, ETFs, and swaps per the strategy craft of the positioning sections; the leverage-calibration awareness where index futures embed substantial notional leverage demanding explicit risk-budget translation per the fixed-income mathematics analogy; the structure-library fluency spanning protective collars, ratio spreads, calendar structures, and volatility expressions whose payoff-risk profiles suit different convictions; the liquidity-state conditioning where instrument selection adjusts to regime with crisis-state execution assumptions differing from calm-state optimization) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the basis-blind positioner whose index view ignores carry, dividend, and funding components embedded in futures prices, remediated by the fair-value analytics; the settlement-mechanics surprised whose expiration-week positions meet SOQ volatility and rebalance flows unprepared, remediated by the event-mechanics calendar; the zero-days gambler whose daily-expiry option trades ignore gamma-decay acceleration and dealer-hedging feedback, remediated by the microstructure literacy; the margin-cascade victim whose index leverage survives calm markets until margin cycles force liquidation at lows, remediated by the regime-aware sizing; the single-instrument default whose every view routes through naked futures when options or spreads carry better risk profiles, remediated by the expression-matching discipline; and the cash-futures divergence panicker who trades basis dislocations as fundamental signals without arbitrage-mechanics understanding, remediated by the arbitrage-program literacy with detection methods as the diagnostic.

# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Analyze systemic liquidity through the concept framework (dimension decomposition
**Scenario:** provide a realistic equity index analysis scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** analyze systemic liquidity through the concept framework (dimension decomposition, state dependence, commonality findings, measurement dashboards), market-maker ecology (dealer capacity constraints, HFT asymmetry, DMM arrangements, cross-market hedging), structural determinants (regulation and capital channels, structure evolution, passive interaction, product effects), spiral mechanics (funding-market transmission, margin-loss interaction, fire-sale externalities, halt architecture), regime monitoring (classification practice, calendar patterns, event anticipation, cross-market coherence), constrained execution (strategy adaptation, capacity honesty, impact modeling, contingency planning), and policy outlook (central-bank backstops, regulatory evolution, technology frontier, fragility debates), avoiding calm-depth extrapolation, spread-only quoting, HFT belief, event-window execution, halt optimism, and capacity dishonesty failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.

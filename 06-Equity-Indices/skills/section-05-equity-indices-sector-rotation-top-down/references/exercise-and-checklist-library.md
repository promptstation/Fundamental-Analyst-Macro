# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Practice sector rotation through architecture literacy (classification systems
**Scenario:** provide a realistic equity index analysis scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** practice sector rotation through architecture literacy (classification systems, eleven-sector macro mapping, endogenous composition, sub-industry granularity), cycle frameworks (asset-clock traditions with caveats, early-mid-late-recession leadership hierarchies), rate sensitivity (duration analogies, financial mechanics, real-asset paradoxes, regime evidence), inflation mapping (pass-through discrimination, revenue beneficiaries, demand destruction, regime hierarchies), the top-down process (regime classification, sensitivity modeling, relative-value assessment, timing discipline), sector-specific linkages (technology-financials-energy-consumer-industrials frameworks), and rotation mechanics (expression vehicles, pairs construction, rebalance flows, crowding monitoring), avoiding clock mechanics, sector monoliths, single-factor rotation, late entry, composition blindness, and liquidity assumption failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.

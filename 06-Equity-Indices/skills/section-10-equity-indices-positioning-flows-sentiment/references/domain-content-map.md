## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the flow and positioning intelligence whose demand-side evidence completes the fundamental picture of what moves indices beyond valuation and earnings: the flow-accounting framework (the demand-supply ledger construction where household allocation, pension and insurance rebalancing, fund flows, corporate activity, and foreign investment compose aggregate demand against issuance supply per the corporate-flows section's accounting extended to all participants; the marginal-buyer analysis identifying which participant class sets prices in each regime, the key question flow analysis answers since average holders do not price; the flow-persistence taxonomy where structural allocation shifts differ from tactical positioning and mechanical rebalancing in duration and price impact; the measurement-system literacy covering fund-flow trackers, custody and clearing data, survey evidence, and broker flow reports whose coverage gaps and biases require triangulation per the intelligence disciplines); the fund-flow ecosystem (the mutual-fund and ETF flow dynamics where retail and advisor allocation shifts arrive through documented weekly reporting with the passive-structure mechanical-investment consequences; the active-passive migration trend where structural flow reallocation reshapes price discovery, liquidity distribution, and concentration dynamics per the structural-determinants analysis; the hedge-fund-positioning data where prime-brokerage reports, futures positioning per the CFTC systems, and thirteen-F filings track leveraged exposure with the lag and coverage limitations documented; the pension-and-insurance mechanics where liability-driven rebalancing triggers systematic equity buying after drawdowns and selling after rallies, the counter-cyclical flow pattern with predictable magnitude estimation); the corporate-and-official flows (the buyback-window dynamics where corporate repurchase activity concentrates by calendar and price behavior per the corporate section with the blackout-period rhythms; the insider-transaction evidence where aggregate executive buying and selling carries documented information content with the noise-filtering discipline; the sovereign-and-official allocation where reserve managers and state funds shift equity exposure with observable custody and benchmark-migration evidence; the cross-border-flow tracking where foreign investment in US equities and US investment abroad flow through TIC-style reporting with the dollar-cycle connection per the global-dollar frameworks); the sentiment-measurement system (the survey-indicator family spanning consumer confidence, investor sentiment polls, and advisor-bear-bull measures whose extremes carry documented contrarian value with the timing-unreliability caveats per the survey-reading disciplines of the macro companion; the market-internal-sentiment gauges including breadth statistics, advance-decline dynamics, new-high-low ratios, and up-down volume whose deterioration or improvement signals regime state; the derivative-implied-sentiment spanning put-call ratios, skew levels, and volatility term structure per the volatility section's extraction methods; the positioning-extremes methodology where historical percentile ranking of leverage, exposure, and crowding measures defines reversal-vulnerability states per the crowding assessment of the rates companion); the flow-price-impact analysis (the impact-estimation literature quantifying how dollar flows translate into price pressure with the multiplier debates and regime-dependence documented; the mechanical-flow events where index rebalances, option expirations, and fund reconstitutions create calendar-predictable price pressure exploitable with capacity limits per the expiration mechanics; the reflexive-flow dynamics where price moves trigger flows that amplify moves through momentum products, volatility targeting, and margin mechanisms per the spiral literatures; the flow-divergence analysis where price and flow disagreement reveals either accumulation, distribution, or measurement gaps requiring resolution per the coherence disciplines); the sentiment-cycle integration (the sentiment-phase mapping where euphoria, complacency, anxiety, and capitulation states connect to cycle phase and volatility regime with the indicator-composite construction; the contrarian-timing discipline where sentiment extremes inform probabilistic reversal assessment rather than mechanical counter-trading per the base-rate traditions; the narrative-tracking practice where dominant market stories shift through documented phases with media and social-sentiment measurement per the text-analytics capabilities of the alternative-data companion; the sentiment-fundamental reconciliation where flow and sentiment evidence either confirms, extends, or contradicts fundamental views with the explicit resolution discipline per the triangulation traditions); and the flow-integration craft (the weekly-flow-review ritual where published flow data updates the demand-supply ledger and marginal-buyer assessment per the operating-rhythm disciplines; the flow-forecasting practice where mechanical flows get projected from known triggers while discretionary flows get modeled from valuation, sentiment, and macro drivers; the flow-scenario integration where stress and rally scenarios include flow-response assumptions per the crisis and cycle sections' forced-flow mechanics; the positioning-aware-trade-construction where entry timing and expression account for crowd position per the decision-layer disciplines of the rates companion) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the fundamental-only analyst whose valuation and earnings views ignore that flows set prices at tradeable horizons, remediated by the marginal-buyer framework; the flow-data-literalist who treats one survey or one week's fund flows as trend against noise and coverage limitations, remediated by the triangulation discipline; the sentiment-extreme timer who mechanically fades bullish surveys without regime or catalyst context, remediated by the probabilistic contrarian discipline; the mechanical-flow blind executor whose entries collide with rebalance and expiration pressure windows, remediated by the calendar-event awareness; the reflexive-flow denier whose equilibrium analysis ignores that price moves generate flows that move prices further, remediated by the spiral-mechanics literacy; and the ledger-incomplete accountant whose demand-supply analysis omits corporate or official flows dominating the period, remediated by the full-participant accounting with detection methods as the diagnostic.

# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Connect macro to index valuation through the framework foundation (discount-rate decomposition
**Scenario:** provide a realistic equity index analysis scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** connect macro to index valuation through the framework foundation (discount-rate decomposition, earnings-power anchors, multiple-as-expectation reading, horizon discipline), the discount-rate channel (real-yield duration sensitivity, risk-premium dynamics, regime-conditioned reactions, cross-asset coherence), the earnings channel (top-down determinants, margin cycles, sector composition, revision evidence), indicator mapping (growth-inflation-policy sensitivities with regime signs, leading indicators), forecasting craft (earnings model construction, implied-expectation extraction, sensitivity estimation, probabilistic integration), and dispersion analysis (cross-market comparison, sector rotation, concentration questions, conditioned percentiles), avoiding isolated multiples, single-channel reactions, permanent sign assumptions, aggregation fantasy, extraction avoidance, and percentile literalism failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone

**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.

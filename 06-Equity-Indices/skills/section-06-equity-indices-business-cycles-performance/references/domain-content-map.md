## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the business-cycle layer whose phase dynamics govern index return distributions, earnings trajectories, and risk behavior across full cycles: the cycle-equity relationship evidence (the leading-turn documentation where equity markets bottom before recessions end and peak before expansions mature, with the average lead-time statistics and their wide dispersion demanding probabilistic rather than mechanical application; the cycle-phase return distribution analysis where expansion phases carry positive-skew return profiles and late-cycle phases show compressed means with fat left tails per the historical record; the recession-behavior library where severity, duration, and policy response determine drawdown depth and recovery speed across documented episodes; the growth-scare versus inflation-scare regime distinction where identical data surprises carry opposite index effects depending on which fear dominates, the reaction-regime framework from the valuation section applied cyclically); the earnings-cycle mechanics (the earnings-recession anatomy where index earnings decline through revenue contraction and margin compression with the operating-leverage magnification documented across cycles; the margin-cycle position tracking where unit labor costs, capacity utilization, and pricing power evolve through cycle phases per the margin analysis of the valuation section; the earnings-recovery sequence where revenue stabilization precedes margin recovery precedes estimate upgrades with the revision-cycle momentum feeding index price recovery; the level-versus-growth-cycle distinction where growth-rate deceleration without absolute contraction produces different earnings and price behavior than classical recession per the cycle taxonomy of the macro companion); the credit-and-financial-cycle overlay (the credit-cycle interaction where lending standards, spreads, and default rates reinforce or cushion the business cycle through corporate financing conditions per the financial-cycle frameworks; the financial-conditions-index reading as the composite transmission gauge where tightening conditions predict growth and earnings deceleration with documented lags; the leverage-cycle dynamics where corporate and household balance-sheet repair phases extend recoveries while leverage-buildup phases amplify expansions and subsequent contractions; the banking-stress transmission where regional and systemic banking problems tighten credit with equity-sector concentration in financials and credit-sensitive cyclicals per the crisis-episode library of the rates companion); the policy-cycle integration (the monetary-policy-lag transmission where tightening effects arrive in earnings with long variable lags, making policy pivots poor immediate timing tools but reliable medium-horizon signals per the transmission-mechanism literature; the fiscal-cycle effects where government spending impulses and tax changes shift aggregate demand with the multiplier-conditioning debates from the macro companion applied to equity earnings; the election-and-political-cycle evidence whose statistical reality separates modestly from folklore requiring the data-mining skepticism of the modeling traditions; the policy-put dynamics where market expectations of central-bank support in equity drawdowns alter risk-taking behavior and volatility structure with the regime-dependence of put credibility); the cycle-indicator application to equities (the leading-indicator-to-index mapping where yield curve, claims, permits, and spreads inform equity-cycle positioning with the horizon and reliability calibration per the indicator systems of the macro companion; the coincident-confirmation discipline where employment, income, and production data confirm cycle phase for earnings-trajectory assessment; the nowcasting integration where high-frequency activity tracking updates current-state growth estimates feeding earnings models per the nowcast traditions; the cycle-position probabilistic language where phase assessments carry confidence bounds acknowledging the real-time dating difficulty documented across the cycle literature); the cross-asset cycle coherence (the bond-equity cycle-consistency check where curve shape, credit spreads, and equity behavior must tell coherent cycle stories or reveal information in the divergence per the coherence-audit traditions; the commodity-cycle alignment where industrial commodity prices and equity cyclicals share demand drivers with divergence signaling either inventory distortion or information per the cross-complex web; the currency-cycle linkage where growth divergence drives both FX trends and relative equity performance connecting to the rates companion's divergence frameworks; the global-cycle synchronization analysis where national cycle phases align or diverge determining whether global equity exposure diversifies or concentrates per the international section); and the strategy-cycle integration (the exposure-management framework where cycle-phase priors condition index exposure levels through sizing and hedge overlay rather than binary in-out timing per the portfolio disciplines; the style-and-sector cycle tilts combining the rotation frameworks with cycle-phase evidence for relative positioning; the drawdown-preparation discipline where late-cycle states trigger liquidity, hedging, and scenario preparation per the stress sections; the recovery-participation systems where cycle-bottom probabilities and policy-response expectations structure re-entry approaches avoiding both premature commitment and confirmation-lagging delay) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the mechanical-lead-timer who forecasts index turns from average recession lead times ignoring dispersion and regime variation, remediated by the probabilistic application discipline; the growth-scare static whose data-reaction sign assumptions hold across inflation regimes without reexamination, remediated by the fear-regime monitoring; the credit-cycle ignoramus whose equity cycle views omit lending standards and spread dynamics that transmit and amplify, remediated by the financial-overlay integration; the policy-pivot chaser who buys central-bank easing as immediate equity signal against documented lag evidence, remediated by the transmission-lag discipline; the phase-overconfident analyst whose cycle calls carry no confidence bounds despite real-time dating difficulty, remediated by the probabilistic language practice; and the single-asset cyclist whose equity cycle view contradicts simultaneous bond and commodity signals unexamined, remediated by the cross-asset coherence checks with detection methods as the diagnostic.

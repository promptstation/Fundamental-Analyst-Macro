# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Navigate global equity linkages through architecture (capitalization geography
**Scenario:** provide a realistic equity index analysis scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** navigate global equity linkages through architecture (capitalization geography, classification systems, listing structures, access regimes), transmission systems (common-factor dominance, US spillover asymmetry, regional clusters, contagion analysis), relative-market craft (conditioned valuation comparison, revision geography, hedging decisions, country-risk overlay), regional literacy (European structure, Japanese transformation, China complexity, EM aggregate behavior), portfolio integration (allocation frameworks, diversification honesty, beta management, tactical geography), and information infrastructure (timezone monitoring, cross-market data, international positioning, local networks), avoiding unaware home bias, naive convergence, unhedged positioning, EM monoliths, timezone blindness, and static contagion modeling failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone

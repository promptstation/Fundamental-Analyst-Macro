## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the decision layer where valuation, cycle, flow, volatility, and positioning evidence combine into constructed index views and trades: the evidence-integration framework (the multi-lens assessment discipline where every index view synthesizes valuation anchoring, cycle-phase priors, policy and rate context, flow and positioning states, and volatility regime into a structured composite rather than single-dimension conviction; the lens-conflict resolution where disagreeing evidence streams force explicit weighting decisions and horizon separation rather than selective emphasis, the coherence-audit tradition applied internally; the time-horizon layering where long-horizon valuation gravity, medium-horizon cycle and policy drivers, and short-horizon flow and event forces get assigned distinct views that may legitimately disagree; the confidence-calibration system where composite assessments carry probability statements and scenario weights per the probabilistic-positioning disciplines of the rates companion); the signal-construction practice (the valuation-signal family where implied ERP, yield-gap, and percentile measures against rate conditioning define long-horizon expected-return anchors per the valuation section's frameworks; the cycle-signal construction where indicator composites, credit conditions, and earnings-revision momentum feed phase assessment per the cycle section with the leading-indicator base rates; the flow-signal system where ledger imbalance, mechanical-flow calendars, and sentiment extremes define demand-side pressure per the flows section; the volatility-regime signal where VIX level and structure, skew state, and gamma-positioning estimates condition expected price behavior and hedge costs per the volatility section; the signal-combination methodology where independent evidence aggregates with correlation-awareness preventing the triple-counting failure documented in the rates companion); the strategy-expression library (the directional-expression selection spanning futures, ETFs, options, and swaps whose cost-leverage-liquidity profiles suit different horizons and conviction levels per the instruments section; the volatility-conditional structures where collars, risk reversals, and ratio spreads express combined directional and volatility views with the dealer-flow awareness of structure consequences; the relative-expression family including style spreads, sector rotation pairs, and cross-market relatives isolating specific views from index beta per the rotation and global sections; the hedge-overlay architecture where core exposure carries protective structures sized by tail-risk budget per the crash-management disciplines; the calendar-and-event structures where release, expiration, and seasonality windows receive expression through timing and option-expiry selection per the event mechanics); the trade-construction discipline (the thesis-documentation standard where every position records its evidence composite, market-implied expectation, divergence, catalyst, horizon, and invalidation per the view-documentation traditions; the sizing-integration where conviction probability, expected asymmetry, volatility state, and portfolio-factor budget combine per the risk-budgeting disciplines with the leverage-reality awareness of index futures; the entry-sequencing craft where mechanical-flow calendars, liquidity windows, and volatility states time execution without subordinating thesis logic to chart patterns; the lifecycle-planning where favorable, adverse, and time-decay branches carry pre-committed responses per the lifecycle systems of the rates companion); the systematic-discretionary synthesis (the rules-based-component integration where seasonal, mechanical-flow, and volatility-premium patterns with robust evidence receive systematic expression within discretionary portfolios per the factor and premia sections; the discretionary-judgment domain where regime shifts, novel events, and structural breaks receive human analysis that fixed rules misprice per the model-limitation traditions; the override-governance discipline where systematic signals get adjusted through documented rationale rather than silent discretion, preserving the calibration record per the forecast-override evidence of the macro companion; the hybrid-portfolio construction where systematic cores and discretionary satellites combine with explicit interaction rules preventing style-drift contamination); the performance-evaluation system (the view-attribution discipline separating evidence-lens correctness, timing contribution, expression efficiency, and luck per the attribution traditions; the benchmark-relative assessment against appropriate index, volatility-adjusted, and peer references with the horizon matching per the evaluation practices of the rates companion; the calibration-review ritual where forecast distributions, confidence statements, and scenario weights face outcome comparison feeding systematic improvement per the professional-judgment traditions; the strategy-lifecycle monitoring where edges decay through crowding and structural change requiring the alpha-lifecycle awareness from the factor and alternative-data traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the single-lens conviction builder whose valuation, cycle, or flow view ignores contradicting lenses until losses force integration, remediated by the multi-lens framework; the horizon-collapser whose long-horizon valuation anchor drives short-horizon positioning against flow and event realities, remediated by the horizon-layering discipline; the expression-default trader whose every view routes through naked futures when structures carry better asymmetry, remediated by the expression-library fluency; the undocumented-position keeper whose views cannot be audited against outcomes, freezing calibration improvement, remediated by the thesis-documentation standard; the systematic-discretionary blender whose rule overrides lack documentation, corrupting both signal evaluation and judgment learning, remediated by the override-governance discipline; and the attribution-free performer whose returns never decompose into evidence and execution contributions, preventing skill development, remediated by the evaluation system with detection methods as the diagnostic.
